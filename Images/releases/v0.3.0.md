> ## The first release with online scores
>
> **Online leaderboards are off until you turn them on.** To take part:
>
> 1. Open **Settings** from the main menu. It opens on its **ONLINE** page.
> 2. Choose **Nickname**, type the name you want on the boards and press **Enter**.
> 3. Switch **Online scores** to **On**.
>
> From then on every level you clear is sent and ranked. The result page shows your place this month and of all time, **High Scores** in the main menu shows every level's #1, and **Select Level** shows the board of the level you point at. Nothing is sent before you turn it on, and **Remove scores** on the same page deletes everything the server holds about you.

## What's new in v0.3.0

**Online scores**
- Every level now has two **online leaderboards**, this month's and all time. Turn **Online scores** on in Settings (it stays off until you do) and choose a nickname; from then on every level you clear is sent, and the result page shows where you stand (#542–#548).
- **High Scores** in the main menu shows every level's #1 and your own place, a chapter at a time (#685). **Select Level** shows the board of the level you point at, beside the levels, and every tile carries your own best score (#684).
- What is sent: your nickname, a random player id, the level, its score, stars, shots, time and the game's version. The server adds when it arrived and a hashed network address. **Remove scores** in Settings deletes all of it.
- A clear that cannot be sent (no connection, the server away) is kept and goes out with the next one.

**Playing**
- **Power-ups**, one of each a level, arriving chapter by chapter (#213):
  - from the second chapter, the **Swap** trades the next two balls;
  - from the third, the ceiling's **Brake** lifts it back one step;
  - from the fourth, the **Cut** fires a round that cuts a hanging anchor.
- On High and Ultra the hanging cluster sways in a light **wind** (#95).
- Every animation you can skip says so after a moment, the chapter tours included (#699). No HUD stands over a chapter's opening tour (#693).
- The tutorial's praise appears under its instruction, and a card already dealt with no longer flashes back after a camera animation (#673, #700).
- Level One is now called Cannonball (#682).

**Scenes**
- **City:** towers rise in setback tiers, with cornices, facades of different kinds and gear on the roofs. The chapter intros differ shot to shot and pass over the streets (#678, #488).
- **Volcano:** fumaroles, blocks, bombs and spatter cones on the lava field (#679). **Cavern:** stalactites and flowstone columns (#676). **Moon:** rocks on the plain (#680). **Mars:** big craters (#638).
- **Meadow:** a brook into a pond, a path to an old oak on a knoll, and old trees whose crowns are twigs and leaves (#609, #697). **Forest:** broadleaves grown like real trees (#647). **Savanna:** bigger trees standing in their own earth (#663, #670).
- **Desert:** dunes with sharp crests and no spikes (#688). **Mountains:** a new sky and snowfall (#661, #654). Bluer skies over the desert and the savanna (#681).
- The fireworks are redrawn and are bent by the ceiling's glass (#612, #689). Many repeating patterns are gone: the sea, the island's stone, the meadow's flowers, the city's windows and the stars (#674).

**Sound and music**
- The music's **drums come in as the cluster nears the line** (#495).
- The pause plays its own calm loop, and the level's music resumes where it stopped (#668).
- After a loss at the line, the lasers stay audible under the result page (#702).

**Interface**
- Settings are on pages by category (#686), and the online rows show what is left to do (#687).

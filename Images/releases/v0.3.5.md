> ## The Big Top, and a nickname at first launch
>
> **New here?** On first launch the game asks for a nickname for the leaderboards. Type one and press **OK** to take part, or **Skip** to keep your scores to yourself. You can change your mind any time in **Settings → ONLINE**.
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your settings carry over, so online scores stay as they were and the game will not ask. To join the boards: open **Settings** (it opens on its **ONLINE** page), choose **Nickname**, type a name, press **Enter**, and switch **Online scores** to **On**.

## What's new in v0.3.5

**A new chapter: the Big Top**
- The **twenty-first scene**, the inside of a circus tent between shows: red and cream canvas, a bowl of seats round a sawdust ring, strings of bulbs, and turning spotlights in dusty air (#690).
- **Ten soft levels** (111–120) that sag, swing and bend under gravity like rope and cloth. The cluster hangs still until play begins and is then let go, so you see it drop and swing (#690).
- New things to shoot: in **Sandbags**, bags of shot hang on ropes and fall when you shoot through the rope. In **Juggler**, the juggling balls spill their cores when you take their colour. In **Trapeze**, crates stand in the ring, and you can bank shots off them (#257).
- **The Big Top has its own music**: circus eurodance with barrel organ, carousel and glockenspiel.

**Online scores**
- **The first launch asks for a nickname** for the leaderboards. Typing one turns the boards on, and Skip keeps them off (#763).
- **A level you did not finish counts too.** Your best attempt shows on its boards as *not finished* until you clear the level, and every clear ranks above it (#716).
- **Your own row on the boards is magenta now.** The first three places wear a gold, silver or bronze cup, and your row in gold was easy to mistake for one (#739, #725).
- The result page shows its score plate from the first moment, with a turning globe while your score is on its way (#734, #723).

**Playing**
- **A shot lands where the aim's landing preview shows.** Oblique shots no longer graze a ball beside their line and stick somewhere else: a shot now sticks in the previewed cell 98 % of the time, up from 64 % (#696).
- **More time for a last-moment save.** The cluster may sit under the line for 1.5 s instead of 1 s before the level is lost (#710).
- The drop cinematic comes about a fifth less often (#719).

**Interface**
- Settings' Exposure is now **Brightness**, in percent of the scene's own look (#711).
- **Help:** the controls in one clean column, every page in the same frame, and Back, Previous and Next in one row, which gives the text more room (#728, #729, #738).
- With the keyboard or a pad, the highlighted entry of a scrolling list stays in view, and Scene opens on the scene you are in (#740).
- High Scores stops at the first and last chapter instead of wrapping round (#727). The power-up chips are smaller (#703). About's music visualizer is colourful, with falling peaks (#730).
- Smaller fixes: the cannon's loading window is set into the steel (#708), the logo's letters have see-through holes (#731), the plates lost a dark stripe on their right edge (#712), the trophy's beaded band reaches the handles (#724), the meadow's fences are at the trees' scale (#698), and the result page keeps the arena sharp longer, with a gentler glance up at the fireworks (#721, #722).

**Download**
- The zip now holds just **BS3D.exe** and four folders (Content, Levels, Music, Sfx), so the game is easy to find (#714).

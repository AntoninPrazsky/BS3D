> ## A Raspberry Pi 5 download, and the game teaches what it shows you
>
> **New: the game for a Raspberry Pi 5.** The same game from the same tag, for 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS, on the same leaderboards. See *Raspberry Pi 5* under the download below.
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings carry over. **Juggler** changed shape, so it has a new leaderboard.

## What's new in v0.3.6

**Raspberry Pi 5**
- A **linux-arm64 package** beside the Windows zip, built from the same tag (#786, #793). It draws through a renderer of its own, **Potato**, made for the Pi's graphics chip, with smooth edges on the balls and the gun (#789, #804).
- On Windows you can pick **Potato** on Settings' Quality row too, to see the Pi's picture or to play on a weak machine. It applies from the next start (#808).

**Power-ups and special balls, taught as you meet them**
- **One power-up a chapter from the third**: the Swap arrives in the Coil, the Brake in the Tower and the Cut in the Silhouettes, each with a card on the level where its chip first appears (#705).
- **A card for every special ball** the first time it is on a level (the bomb, glass, stone, buckshot, the rainbow ball and the rest), with the ball itself turning on the card (#735).
- **A special round announces itself**: while the rainbow ball or the cutter is loaded, a burst of rays turns round the muzzle and behind its disc in the queue (#820).
- **The Cut takes a whole storey** of the cluster where it strikes, and whatever then hangs on nothing falls. It reaches at most the lower half of the cluster, and while you aim, the balls it would take light up (#692, #823).
- **You can see the Swap**: the two rounds dissolve into each other's colour, and their discs in the queue do too (#705).
- Buckshot looks like a handful of lead shot, and Juggler shows its cores as a band round each ball (#257).
- A level's own tutorial cards come back every time you start it, as long as Settings' Tutorial row is on (#715).

**Gamepad**
- **You feel the game in your hands.** On an Xbox pad, the triggers' own motors answer the shot (RT) and the aim (LT). The pad also shakes for the ceiling's step, the cluster reaching the line and the barrel at its stop. The menus tick under your thumb, and a tab turns with a pulse on its own side (#188, #378, #800).
- The pad stays still while you play with the keyboard and mouse (#817).
- The left stick turns and walks the gun at the speed you push it (#802). In precise aim the right stick slows with the lens and follows the Aim sensitivity row (#819). Left and right walk a row of buttons, on About and in the Jukebox (#805). RT skips a camera takeover (#378).

**Write to the Author**
- From the pause menu, About or Extras, send the author a line about what you just saw (#813). The note carries the level, the build, your settings and graphics card, and the picture of that moment if you leave it ticked. Nothing in it names you or your machine. It goes whether or not online scores are on.

**Extras and the Jukebox**
- The main menu's Scene entry is now **Extras**: the scene picker and a **Jukebox** that plays every recording in the game, chapter by chapter (#704). The scenes are listed in chapter order, each with its chapter's name (#773), and the player's buttons are media symbols (#784).
- Settings' Track row is gone: the Jukebox is where you pick a piece now (#704).

**Settings**
- **Resolution**: draw the 3D below your display's resolution while the menus and the HUD stay sharp (#801).
- **Scenery**, on the Display page: turn the backdrop off and keep the sky, the island, the cluster and the gun (#808).
- The rows line up with the tabs, the on/off rows have a checkbox, and the Online page's privacy note is shown whole (#770, #816, #769).

**The campaign and its scenes**
- The second half of the campaign is in a new order: after the Quarry come the Grid, the Nebula, the Big Top, the Eruption, the Spectrum, the Arcade and the Mirage, so each hard chapter is followed by a lighter one (#803). Your save keeps every level you cleared.
- Fish and shafts of light under the sea, and the Sea's intro dives down to them (#759, #760). No fireworks while the camera is under water (#761).
- The storm's lightning bolt itself is seen, sharp, not only its light (#750). The clouds drift slower everywhere, and slowest in the storm (#809).
- The city intros circle a rooftop with its masts and dishes, close and from above (#780, #783).
- The savanna's leaves grow from twigs (#782). The cave's stalactites stand in front of the island where they should (#755). Falling balls no longer show through the drain's glass at its tip (#762).

**Smaller things**
- The aim's landing preview grows and shrinks instead of dissolving (#794). The rainbow ball's colours flow on instead of jumping back (#821). The muzzle's collar is a band round the muzzle from every side (#812). Balls falling into the drain are drawn steadily on the side profile (#822).
- The barrel says a short technical "uh-uh" when it meets its stop (#811). The laser net's hum after a loss at the line is louder on small speakers (#702). The Quarry's music no longer goes silent for 18 seconds (#772).
- No skip hint over the drop of the shot that cleared the level, and none over the pause page (#699, #814).

> ## The Cut as a moment, new music in three chapters, and the pad in both hands
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Unzip into a **new, empty folder**: this release removes six recordings, and unzipped over an old install the old files would stay and return to the chapters' rotation. Your save and settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D`, so they carry over anyway. The Silhouettes (41–50) and the Quarry (64–70) were rebuilt, so those have new leaderboards.

## What changes play in v0.4.0

- **The Cutter round has a look and a moment of its own**: a dark steel ball with a white-hot saw, a four-beat strike (saw spin-up, a lens zoom, a slash with a brief freeze, what it freed falling in slow motion), and the pad's triggers rise and click (#766, #767).
- **The Silhouettes (41–50) are rebuilt as solids** hung ten different ways, a fish, an umbrella, a bell, a cat as a puppet, a key, an anchor, and glass is first met on Umbrella (#736).
- **The Quarry (64–70) is rebuilt**: each level adds one idea the gun can see (an arch, an overhang, a hung stone, two depths, a sealed crystal), and Colossus closes it as a giant holding the glass up (#742, #849).
- **Every colour carries its own pattern**, in every ball style and in the gun's queue, so colours can be told apart by shape (#737).
- **A lost streak is seen and heard**: the multiplier shatters, "STREAK LOST ×N" says so, the Swap chip flashes when a swap would keep it, and the points a shot gains zoom up past the score (#845, #875, #846).
- **Tall levels play fairer**: the feed lowers the glass only as far as you can reach, and the Brake works only on still glass and never lifts it out of reach (#842, #843, #844).
- **New music** in the Gallery, the Silhouettes, the Grid and the Eruption, and none of it clips any more (#706, #732, #824, #841, #919).
- **Help is a row of tabs** (Controls, Basics, Scoring, Odd balls, Campaign, The glass), and the odd balls turn beside their words (#857, #856, #865).
- **The pad walks the level picker** (LB/RB turn chapters), the menus draw the keys and buttons beside what they do, and the rumble grows with what fell (#848, #799, #810, #836).
- **Displays**: scaled displays (Windows at 125 % or more) draw at full resolution, and mouse aim is faster there, set by the **Aim sensitivity** row (#825); **10-bit colour** is a row on the Display page (#859).
- **Raspberry Pi and the Potato tier** now draw the scenes' backdrops, the balls' patterns and the special kinds' looks (#829, #828, #863).
- **The game counts how each level is played**, on your computer; only with online scores on are those counts sent, per level and nothing else (#709).

…and about 35 smaller changes and fixes: [the full notes](https://github.com/AntoninPrazsky/BS3D/blob/v0.4.0/Images/releases/v0.4.0.md) and the issues since v0.3.6.

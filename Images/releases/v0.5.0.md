> ## Every scene filled out, a red line you can see, a benchmark, and the tutorial in plain words
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D` and carry over. The Mirage (133–140) was rebuilt lighter, so those eight levels have new leaderboards.

## What changes play in v0.5.0

- **The Cavern is a real hall**: an irregular limestone chamber over a black mirroring lake, stalactite curtains and flowstone, every crystal cluster seated in rock (#958, #1017).
- **The Aurora is rebuilt from the ground up**: a moonlit snowfield with drifts and a collar of snow at every trunk, a folded aurora of three arcs with rays and red tops, the Moon casting the wood's shadows, mist between the trunks and a far wood to the last ridges (#963, #960).
- **Every scene gained something to look at**: a station deck on thruster pods in space with a flying saucer and its crew, light cycles on the Grid trailing walls of light, the Giza pyramids over the desert with beetles and scorpions in the sand, two oil platforms out at sea, a research camp on the polar ice, rover tracks on the Moon and on Mars, a far skyline round the city, a melting pearl island in the dream, an old, worn big top, and rain under the storm (#954, #955, #956, #965, #966, #969, #747, #746, #745, #774, #779, #976, #953).
- **On High and Ultra**: clouds are volumes down to the horizon, the savanna's fires are flame, the meadow's oaks have furrowed bark and the forest's spruces needles, and the Forest stands 400 trees denser (#989, #886, #980, #751, #962).
- **Scenes polished**: the meadow's sky is clear azure from the first level, its brook falls from a spring to the pond, its lawn, hay bales and bench are new; the tropical lagoon meets the sand as a real shore; the outback has real rock formations on its horizon, spinifex, saltbush and a cracked crust; Mars has basalt stones and moons that read as moons; snow lies on the Mountains' valley floors; every mesh of every scene is shut, with a check that keeps it so (#1019, #979, #944, #971, #1022, #968, #967, #749, #776, #775, #744, #959, #974, and more).
- **The red line is shown before it threatens**: four laser emitters inside the funnel project the net, which ends exactly at their corners and bows like a lens; the Saturn card lights it, and from Saturn on every level opens with the emitters turning to their aim and the net shown for a moment (#874, #941, #933).
- **The tutorial says one thing a card**, a bold "Fire!" chip helps a player who has stopped, and Amphora teaches that throwing a ball away is a fair way out (#930, #925, #936).
- **The balls you did not use are fired off** at a clear, from a camera beside the gun, and the bonus counts up with them; a click skips it (#881).
- **Celebrations at the loud end**: NEW BEST struck onto the result page with a bounce, a rainbow fill, sparkles and a chime; CAMPAIGN COMPLETE across the frame at the finale; the Swap slides the two rounds through each other with a sound of its own; each power-up chip has a pictogram (#926, #880, #871, #991).
- **Openings**: your very first one plays whole; Continue from the main menu plays the scene's opening (skippable) before handing you the gun; the Eruption opens on the whole volcano, the Cavern out of a passage, the Big Top under its canvas, and the City and the Neon City end on a fast swing through their streets behind the gun (#1021, #870, #975, #984, #752, #972, #973).
- **The glass ceiling hangs from four steel cables**, let out by a winch at each corner as it steps down and wound back in by the brake (#896).
- **The drop cinematic runs at full speed** and flies one curve past the island's rim and under it; it now comes only for a release of a quarter of the level or more, and always on the shot that clears it (#877, #878, #1011).
- **The Mirage (133–140) is lighter** and carries bombs from Seam on (#879).
- **Extras → Benchmark**: a fixed tour of the dearest scenes, then the City's opening into the gun and the gun firing by itself, every frame graphed against your display's budget; it recommends a quality tier and offers to set it. **A finished run sends its figures and your computer's specification** (graphics card and driver, processor, memory, Windows version, display) to the author, without your nickname, whether online scores are on or not; the page says so before you start (#988, #1018).
- **Help and Settings read at a glance**: every Help paragraph stands under a heading of its own, Controls fits one screen, Scoring is four values and a tip, Odd balls say what each ball does; Display is two tabs, Screen and Picture; what online scores send is a short list (#996, #997, #998, #999, #1000, #1001, #1002, #867, #868, #869).
- **In play**: aimed too high, the gun fires along its limit instead of refusing the shot; the ball in the gun shows its pattern through the slot; the points a shot gains turn see-through while you read them (#1008, #939, #935, #937).
- **New music**: the Gallery's Sunset, the Summit's Glacier, five of the Silhouettes' recordings, the Meadow's Sunny and the Reveal's Bossa and Vibes (#905, #906, #907, #904, #908).
- **Menus**: one sharp 3D logo on every tier, High Scores shows every level and marks an unfinished attempt with a hollow star, long names stay readable, the mouse wheel moves a menu a fifth of the window a notch, the Jukebox says what is playing, and About shows the MonoGame and BepuPhysics logos (#929, #981, #898, #977, #1004, #934, #952, #900, #951).
- **Fixes**: the aim beam no longer vanishes against the ceiling's glass, a machine with no audio device plays on in silence, aiming below the floor no longer alarms, a nickname with a rude word in it is refused while you type (#1013, #923, #872, #1020).

The website has a blog now, and the game's own cannon drives round its front page: [bs3d.winphonew.eu](https://bs3d.winphonew.eu/) (#928, #949).

> ## Two scenes rebuilt, a red line you can see, and the tutorial in plain words
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D` and carry over. The Mirage (133–140) was rebuilt lighter, so those eight levels have new leaderboards.

## What changes play in v0.5.0

- **The Cavern is a real hall**: an irregular limestone chamber over a black mirroring lake, stalactite curtains and flowstone, built from references (#958).
- **The Aurora is rebuilt from the ground up**: a moonlit snowfield with drifts and a collar of snow at every trunk, a folded aurora of three arcs with rays and red tops, the Moon casting the wood's shadows, mist between the trunks and a far wood to the last ridges (#963, #960).
- **The red line is shown before it threatens**: four laser emitters inside the funnel project the net, which ends exactly at their corners and bows like a lens; the Saturn card lights it, and from Saturn on every level opens with the emitters turning to their aim and the net shown for a moment (#874, #941, #933).
- **The tutorial says one thing a card**, a bold "Fire!" chip helps a player who has stopped, and Amphora teaches that throwing a ball away is a fair way out (#930, #925, #936).
- **Celebrations at the loud end**: NEW BEST struck onto the result page with a bounce, a rainbow fill, sparkles and a chime; CAMPAIGN COMPLETE across the frame at the finale; the Swap slides the two rounds through each other with a sound of its own (#926, #880, #871).
- **The drop cinematic runs at full speed** and its lens flies one curve past the island's rim and under it (#877, #878).
- **The Mirage (133–140) is lighter** and carries bombs from Seam on (#879).
- **The Forest on High and Ultra** stands 400 trees denser with saplings, snags, logs, ferns, broadleaves in leaf and a far wood on the hills; **the Outback's** far formations are real rock with buttresses, gullies, cast shadows and their own sky, no longer pale hats on the horizon (#962, #967).
- **Scenes polished**: the meadow's brook lies in a swale and flows into the pond as one water, its shrubs and old trees are grown on High and Ultra; the savanna's fires stand on mounds and its bushes are thorn shrubs; the Moon's and the volcano's rocks are fractured stone on High and Ultra; the Forest has a sky of its own and fireflies that glow instead of blink; the city's mast beacons flash out of step; every mesh of every scene is shut, with a check that keeps it so (#892, #970, #890, #889, #887, #883, #777, #961, #902, #974, #938, #943).
- **Help and Settings read at a glance**: every Help paragraph opens on a bold lead-in, Controls fits one screen, Scoring is four values and a tip, Odd balls say what each ball does; Display is two tabs, Screen and Picture; what online scores send is a short list; and the plate under the text is darker (#996, #997, #998, #999, #1000, #1001, #1002, #867, #868, #869).
- **In play**: the ball in the gun shows its pattern through the slot, and the points a shot gains turn see-through while you read them, a black cluster’s on a light glow (#939, #935, #937).
- **New music**: the Gallery's Sunset, the Summit's Glacier, five of the Silhouettes' recordings, the Meadow's Sunny and the Reveal's Bossa and Vibes, regenerated on the model of the desert's family (#905, #906, #907, #904, #908).
- **Menus**: the title wordmark is the logo on High and Ultra, High Scores shows every level, boards keep long names readable and mark an unfinished attempt with a recess and a hollow star, the mouse wheel moves a menu a fifth of the window a notch, High Scores shows the chosen period like a chosen tab, and the Jukebox says what is playing (#929, #898, #934, #977, #1004, #952, #1003, #900, #901).
- **Fixes**: a machine with no audio device plays on in silence, aiming below the floor no longer alarms, NEW BEST’s outline has whole corners, and the frame makes no garbage (#923, #872, #986).

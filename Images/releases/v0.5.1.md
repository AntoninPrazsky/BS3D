> ## Top Players, the menu's own cannon, a backlit keyboard and a faster Low
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D` and carry over. No level changed, so every leaderboard carries on. **The benchmark now runs only with online scores on** (Settings → ONLINE).

## What's new in v0.5.1

- **Extras → Top Players**: the players ranked across the whole campaign by total score, with levels cleared and stars beside it, this month or all time, the top three on a podium with gold, silver and bronze cups, and your own place under the table (#827).
- **The menu has its own cannon** on the island, loaded in the colours of the cluster above it, and now and then the camera flies over to it and round it (#1040).
- **A backlit keyboard plays along** through Windows Dynamic Lighting: the loaded ball's colour, Space at full, the queue on F1–F4, the keys a tutorial card asks for blinking, a rainbow when you win and red when you lose. Settings → CONTROLS → Keyboard lights (#987).
- **The Volcano has a land beyond the lava**: a ragged lava front, rolling country, ridges and a dormant cone, a river and a village with lit windows (#748).
- **The camera eases in** a few seconds into a level, so the cluster fills more of the frame and the gun less; after precise aim it shows the whole frame again for a moment (#1006).
- **Two new hints**: three shots in a row that join nothing, fired without precise aim, bring up "Hold to aim precisely", again every third miss while they go on; and the first ball left stuck after it is shot down gets a card that says so (#1012, #1023).
- **New music in seven chapters**: two recordings in the Quarry, one on the Grid, two in the Nebula, four in the Big Top, three in the Eruption, three in the Arcade and all ten in the Mirage; the Meadow's Sunny is a new recording too (#909–#915, #917).
- **Low runs faster on integrated graphics** in the Cavern, the Meadow, the Savanna, the Aurora, the Volcano and the Mountains, and so does Medium in the Volcano. On Low a few details are simpler: the meadow's grass blades, the mountains' rock, the volcano's smoke column (#1027, #1028, #1029, #1032, #1035, #1037, #1038).
- **The benchmark ends on a storm** of the game's balls that grows until your computer can no longer draw it at 60 FPS: one number to compare machines by. It runs only with online scores on, since its result goes to the author (#988, #1041).
- **Online scores** is a checkbox, and what it sends is a two-column list on Settings and on a Privacy page of its own in About. A nickname like Jane Grey or Mr Darcy is no longer refused for a rude word read across the space (#1034, #1036).
- **Smaller things**: the power-up chips read key, picture, word; the menu's camera no longer flies through the towers of the City and the Neon City on a narrow window (#1030, #1039).

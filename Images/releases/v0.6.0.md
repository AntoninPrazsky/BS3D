> ## Android, the City's new towers, and a Raspberry Pi with scenery in every chapter
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings carry over (Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D`). No level changed, so every leaderboard carries on.

## What's new in v0.6.0

- **BS3D runs on Android.** The same game, levels, music and online boards on a phone or a tablet, drawn with the Windows renderer at a phone's settings: Low, Medium and High with Auto quality holding 60 FPS, and Potato for a weak phone. Play by touch — drag on one side to aim, tap on the other to fire and drag there to walk the gun, a button toggles precise aim, a tap on each power-up chip — or with a gamepad over Bluetooth, rumble included. The menus are made for a finger, the tutorial's cards say what to touch, a tap skips a scene's tour, and the phone's own keyboard dialog types your nickname. On a 120 Hz display the game runs at the display's rate once the phone allows it; Settings → SCREEN → FPS limit can hold it at 60 Hz for a cooler phone. How to install it is in the Android section below (#1047, #1051, #1052, #1056, #1060, #1064, #1070, #1074).
- **The City's towers have plans of their own** — slabs, Ls, podiums, twins and leaning setbacks instead of one block repeated — and on High and Ultra their windows mirror the towers round them (#1066, #1065).
- **Three scenes touched up**: the Desert's tour opens with a shot of the pyramids, the Storm's clouds are cotton wool rather than snowflakes, and the Grid's light cycles turn only right angles (#1068, #1062, #1069).
- **Precise aim can be a toggle**: one press leans the lens in and it stays, the next brings it back, on the mouse's right button and the pad's left trigger alike, and it is the default now; Settings → CONTROLS → Precise aim brings Hold back (#1054).
- **The Cut plays in real time**: no hit-stop and no slow-motion fall when it severs the cluster (#1046).
- **Raspberry Pi 5**: the scenery of fourteen chapters rebuilt for the Pi's picture — windows on the City's towers, the Meadow's path, brook and pond, the Desert's pyramids over far dunes, the Cavern lit by its crystals, the Big Top's seats and bulb strings, the Moon's Earth and the rover's ruts, the saucer over Space, the Grid's light cycles, the Aurora's curtains, steam over the Volcano and flames at the Savanna's campfires, and the Windows cloud deck over every dome scene (#1044).
- **After a clear**, the gun empties as it fires off your unused balls, and the lens no longer leans back into precise aim before the volley (#1075, #1076).

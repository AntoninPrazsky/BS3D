> ## A smoother Raspberry Pi, and a note from the author
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save and settings carry over (Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D`, Raspberry Pi: `~/.local/share/BS3D`). No level changed, so every leaderboard carries on.

## What's new in v0.6.1

- **Raspberry Pi 5: levels start without the stall.** The Pi build comes precompiled, so each scene's first level is up about 1.5 s sooner (#1079), and the first seconds of every level no longer drop to around 9 FPS while the laser net comes up (#1085).
- **Raspberry Pi 5: a note from the author** greets you on the first launch: what the game is, who makes it, and Write to the Author for anything that breaks (#1083).
- **Raspberry Pi 5: F12 always saves the screenshot.** When the game's own folder cannot be written to, the picture goes to `~/Pictures/BS3D` instead (#1082).
- **Every download carries `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`** beside the game, the licences of what it is built on, and About names the graphics each build draws with: DirectX 11 on Windows, OpenGL on the Pi, OpenGL ES on Android (#1081).

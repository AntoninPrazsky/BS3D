> ## Time counts, and the cluster feels every shot
>
> **Played an earlier version?** Your save, your settings and every star you earned carry over (Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D`, Raspberry Pi: `~/.local/share/BS3D`). Scores are now made under new rules, so the online leaderboards start afresh, and your best on a level is set again by your first clear under them.

## What's new in v0.7.0

- **Time counts: the faster you clear, the bigger the TIME BONUS.** It drains under the score, is half gone at the level's par time and all gone at twice par, and pauses while the camera follows falling balls. Four stars now need a fast, clean clear (#1045, #1197).
- **The cluster answers every hit.** A shot pushes the balls it strikes, and the balls a match knocks loose fly off with part of its momentum (#1192, #1248, #1249).
- **A thinner aim line**: one dashed line to where the ball lands, forking at its end into three fading prongs that show the landing spread (#1196, #1235).
- **Balls that wedge instead of falling dissolve after a few seconds**, so you no longer shoot at colours that can never match (#1191).
- **Colours you could mix up are told apart**: orange from brown, and a silver ball in the gun from a black one; the level Amphora trades its brown for blue (#1247, #1259, #1194).
- **On a phone**, Help, Settings, the level and scene pickers, About and the leaderboards are laid out for the screen, every control at least a fingertip wide (#1151, #1152, #1153, #1181, #1184).
- **A note from the author** greets every player on the first launch (#1255).

…and about a hundred smaller changes and fixes: [the issues closed since v0.6.1](https://github.com/AntoninPrazsky/BS3D/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aclosed%20closed%3A%3E2026-11-10).

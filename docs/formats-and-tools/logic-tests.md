# The logic tests (Tests/BS3D.Tests)

Part of [Formats and tools](../formats-and-tools.md).

`Tests/BS3D.Tests` checks **the rules the libraries state as holding "by construction"** (#586): ScoreSim and LevelGen's regeneration check gate the shipped *data*; nothing gated the *rules*. Run `dotnet test Tests\BS3D.Tests` (seconds); `build.yml` runs it on every push.

xUnit, not a console gate, because most checks are one assertion over every shipped level or lattice cell and `[Theory]` + `MemberData` gives each its own named result; `dotnet test` exits non-zero as a gate should. Plain `net10.0`, MonoGame DesktopGL compile-only, referencing the three libraries, Bepu and NVorbis (the game's Vorbis decoder, #726); the Game's device-free files (the descent, the tutorial, the synthesized sounds, `OggTrack` and `Spectrum` among them) are compiled straight in. It draws nothing and finds `Game\Levels` by landmark, and `Game\Sfx` beside it (`Shipped.Sfx`). **Tests run in parallel** (serial until #585): `BallsConstraintsBuilder.GetSphereShapeIndex` caches per simulation in a `ConditionalWeakTable` and the release path's scratch lists are `[ThreadStatic]` (a simulation is driven from one thread).

## What it checks

- **`NeighbourTests`**: b ∈ N(a) ⇔ a ∈ N(b); at most four cells per group; every neighbour exactly 1.0 away in `GetRealPosition`, every other cell of the 3×3×3 window further; the allocation-free enumerator and `CountOccupiedNeighbors` agree with `FillNeighboringCells`.
- **`ConstraintTests`** (every shipped level and `Full.json`, hung as the Game hangs it): the solver's constraint count equals the neighbour graph's edges plus the top level's balls, and each ball's stored handles equal its occupied neighbours (a failed `TryStore` shows here).
- **`LevelFormatTests`**: every shipped level survives `Save` → `Load` unchanged; colour 14 or 0 or an undefined kind is refused (#571); a legacy map is padded by an even number of levels keeping parity.
- **`SaveFileTests`**: `AtomicFile` keeps one `.bak` and no `.tmp`; `PlayerProgress.Load` reports each outcome; the `KeepUnreadable` copy survives two later saves.
- **`ScoringTests`**, every figure derived from `ScoreKeeper`'s constants: award before multiplier rise (capped), a miss resets, `ScoreCeiling` above a hand-built best run, the rating never falls as the score rises.
- **`SleepAndSecretTests`** (#230): `Cannon.Droop` never moves `AimDirection`/`MuzzlePosition`; the secret codes fire on their sequence alone.
- **`TopLevelAttachTests`** (#561): a ball landed beside a top-level neighbour on `Anchor.json` stays within 0.1 of its lattice offset after 6 s (0.009 off; with a **zero offset**, the defect, 3.339); it asserts the world offset is over 1 so a near-zero offset cannot pass for the wrong reason.
- **`MatchReachTests`**, **`StreakCuesTests`** and **`StreakBreakSynthTests`** (#845): `MatchReach` agrees with the match rule's own walk on every colour of every shipped level, placed for real (12 of 736 have nowhere to score at the start, Chest's sealed white and Colossus's buried heart among them, the branch seen to fire), and its cost is printed; a sealed pocket beside a ball on the field's wall counts as somewhere, because the placement's own finders put a shot struck from outside the field into it (seen to fail before `MatchReach` knew it); every cell those finders return for a wall ball struck from any side is among the oracle's landings; the break is told only while the level goes on, and the Swap flashes on one row of its truth table (the owner's three conditions plus the conservative fourth, a round behind that can score); the lost-streak caption over the aim point (#875) stands a few hundred milliseconds, grows with the loss yet stays under a bare match's award, is in full on its first frame and never brightens once it fades, and its clearance stands above the crosshair's own full-strain reach (each seen to fail on a planted fault: a fade in, a caption too big, a clearance of 100 units); the falling run keeps 99.8 % of its energy at 300-4000 Hz, falls note by note and is longer for a lost cap, and a bass-only copy of it fails the band check.
- **`ChapterTests`** (#849): every block of the shipped set is one scene, one dome, one music family and one ball material across its level files, and every level names a family with recordings in `Game/Music` (seen to fail on a level moved to the cavern and to Ember, and on a family misspelt).
- **`CeilingFeedSoundTests`** and **`CeilingFeedPourTests`** (#726): the blue ceiling step's shipped recording, decoded through `OggTrack` as the game loads it, and its bake keep their identity at 300-4000 Hz (75 % and 80 %), have no hiss, rise, and carry under a quarter of the red step's share under 200 Hz; the band check refuses the red step's own recording (3.6 %) and a bass-only bake (0.0 %). A feed's queued steps, let go by `CeilingDescent` itself at 15 to 240 frames a second, climb a semitone each to a fifth; a red step does not break a pour, and a pause longer than the window on the wall clock does not restart one.
- **`MusicDecodeHeadroomTests`** (#919): `puppet-triphop`, which the codec decodes to a peak of 1.086, has no sample at the 16-bit rail (32766 or more) decoded as the music is and 2 287 at unity (seen to fail with the headroom taken out), the headroom in dB and as a factor are one figure, each music voice times it is the volume it had before (0.5, 0.29) and at most 1, and the players' source is read to hold that every headroomed decode is paired with a compensated voice while the effects, the fanfares and the About page's procedural pieces are left alone. All 140 recordings through the decode are a count, not a test: a CI test over them would be minutes.
- **`CannonElevationTests`** (#872, #1008): a push under `MinElevation` never raises `ElevationStrain` and its rubber returns onto the clamp without crossing it; the upper limit keeps its strain and rebound; a shot at either clamp leaves along `FireDirection` at exactly the clamp while the drawn pose stands past it, and never past it while the spring brings the barrel back; the upper rebound under its clamp fires as drawn. Seen to fail with each end's stretch put back into `FireDirection`.
- **`ClusterInvariantTests`** (#585): `Anchor`, `Kiln`, `Vent` and `Full.json` take **300 seeded operations** each (a landing, `ReleaseSameTypeCluster`, three world steps, released bodies removed so handles are reused); after the build and **every** operation `ClusterInvariants.Check` must be empty (map and physics agree; every stored handle is a live `BallSocket` joining its ball to one lattice neighbour or the ceiling; every neighbouring pair exactly one socket). Floors on landings, matches and releases stop a sequence that no longer reaches the release path from passing.

## Seen to fail, and two lessons

Every test was seen to fail by a hand-made break (`BestPractices.md` §10; the per-break table is in the old `docs/formats-and-tools.md` in git). **A break only proves a check if it reaches data the check reads**: connecting cross-level pairs twice from level 1 failed nothing, since no shipped level has a ball on level 1. **A round-trip test proves writing and reading are symmetric, not that a field is persisted**: `[JsonIgnore]` on `"k"` and `"weather"` failed nothing, since an attribute that drops a field on write drops it on read.

# Chapter intro prologues

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md). The flight itself is in [The chapter intro](chapter-intro.md); the remaining scenes' shots are in [the other scenes' shots](chapter-intro-scene-shots.md).

A scene that opens on a **prologue** plays cut-together `IntroShot`s (a path and a look) before the tour's last leg. `Effects/IntroPrologues.For(SceneKind, IntroContext)` picks the builder (`IntroContext`: the renderer, the city, the two plantings, the dome's sun, the wall clock now and to read later, and the pose the intro hands back at); `IntroPaths` holds the shared geometry. A declarative shot list was rejected: builders *search* their scene with reject-and-retry loops, which is logic, not data.

## What every prologue shares

- **A prologue is taken with a cut and skipped with one.** The blend cannot make a cut (it would glide the lens through the towers), so `Begin` sets it to 1 with a prologue and `TrySkip` drops it to 0.
- **After a prologue the tour flies only its last leg** (`ARRIVAL_SECONDS`, 4.5 s): the tour's scene stands lie on a circle round the arena that in a city runs through the towers. **Unless the last shot lands** (`IntroShot.Lands`, #972): a shot whose path and look end on the gameplay pose is followed by no tour leg; `ChapterIntro` holds its last frame while the gun's pose blends back in, a blend between two identical poses.
- **A shot may bank** (`IntroShot.RollAt`, #972): `ChapterIntro.Roll` reaches `RecoilCamera.BaseRoll` in play and in the menu's replay, which writes it every frame, level when no tour flies: written only while one flew, it kept the bank a session left on the camera when Pause and Main Menu were taken mid-swing. The tour itself stays level.
- **One concrete object per shot, an establishing view first.** `[intro]` and `[tour]` name the shots, e.g. `[intro] block 'The Eruption' (9/13), cut 'the flank' 3.2s | 'the crater' 5.4s | cut -> map 43 out at 46deg -> game pose, 4.5s; 13.1s in all`. Totals run 11-17 s, the day city about 20 (its swing home is 6.6-7.6 s and lands), the neon city 22 (22.5 when the roof skim stands in for the rooftop), the meadow 22.9 (#893, its two orbits).
- **`IntroShot` moves without corners (#645)**: `Pose` extrapolates the look-ahead along the last leg (swapping in a direction swung the view in one frame); `At` is a Catmull-Rom (linear interpolation changed velocity at every point).

## The cities (#488, #780)

`Game/Effects/CityIntroShots.cs` builds four shots off the city being drawn (the streets lie ninety units under the island, out of any spline's reach). **Every path runs down a street's centre line**, which keeps it out of the towers. The two cities are two places (the owner, 2026-10-20) and get two reels: the day city's is street, plaza, rooftop and **the swing home** (#972, below); the neon city still plays street, plaza, swing and rooftop and the tour's arrival, as the day city does where no swing home is clear.

- **The street**: 26 units up and 36 degrees down (the cars' and crossings' paint is flat and reads only from above), in the deepest of twelve rolled canyons.
- **The plaza**: an 80-degree orbit rising 32 to 54 over an open block, in the day city 72 to 105 (#972, "we do not want a close view of the square ... the camera should only go higher"). ⚠ **From 16 units up the trees read as holes** (discs painted on the paving); from 30 up they are a canopy.
- **The swing**: round a tower corner into the cross street.
- **The rooftop** (#780): an 85-degree arc round one dressed roof (`CityRooftops.Showcases.Best`), which must stand over every roof within 26 units (else the lens looks down a canyon at walls). The old roof skim is the fallback.
  - ⚠ **The whole mast stays in frame (#840).** Looking at the mast's lower third from a fixed height, a tall mast's beacon ran off the top edge for most of the arc. `FrameMast` bounds the look by the beacon's top (its radius included), held 6 % of the frame's height under the top edge, and the mast's foot, held 15 % over the bottom one, from every point of the arc, and keeps the lower-third look wherever it fits; where no look fits, the lens climbs in half-unit steps until one does (a higher lens sees the mast foreshortened). The foot's margin is the wider because the skip hint stands centred on the bottom edge for the whole intro, up to 12.2 % of the frame's height, and the mast is centred across: at 6 % the foot stood behind the hint. The projection is Hor+, so the window's shape does not change this.
  - The look stays on the mast's own axis (`RoofShowcase.MastFoot`): a mast stands on the roof's middle **cell**, which on a 2×2 roof is off the roof's middle. A roof carries one mast at most, because the neon extras fill only the cells the day's dressing left (#850): where the day's put none, `Best(neon: true)` reports the extras' mast.
  - The beacon is not phased to the shot: the city's beacons flash in eight clusters on the wall clock, each with its own period (`BeaconPeriod` times 0.75 to 1.02, so at most 1.63 s), and a 5-second shot holds at least three flashes of the mast's own cluster wherever the clock stands (`CityRooftopsTests` counts them; a period over 5/3 s would break it).
- **The swing home** (#972, `CitySwing`): the owner's "Spider-Man-style swinging fly-throughs until we finally arrive at the island and the camera gets into the game camera position". A staircase on the street grid (a street, a banked corner, the cross street, a corner the other way, a last street towards the pose), then the release: a curve off the last street's line onto the pose, climbing out of the canyon past the floating island's underside and slowing to rest on the pose, the look easing onto the pose's own over the last 1.6 s. Lands, so no tour leg follows.
  - **A pendulum.** The corners are the tops of the swings (80 over the street) and the legs sag 20 between them, the speed being the drop traded for it (28 u/s round a corner, up to 59 at a sag's bottom); the path is laid out by length and walked by that speed, so its points come out evenly spaced in time. The release decelerates on a cubic in time.
  - ⚠ **Every sag is level at both ends.** Sine sags meet over a corner at an angle, and the look pitched 200 degrees a second over it. And ⚠ **the look follows the path's own direction, averaged over ±0.3 s, not a point ahead**: a chord to a point 0.35 s on cuts the corner, and short across the turn while the drop was not, it pitched the view 140 degrees a second. Averaged, the view turns into a corner before the lens does.
  - **How it arrives.** Along the pose's facing, halfway, or still along the street with the view swivelling onto the pose as it slows: a pose just inside the clearing's edge on an axis bearing can be reached only the last way (without it, 15 of 128 probed poses had no route). A release tighter than a corner's fillet is refused.
  - **Banked** in proportion to the heading's rate of turn (0.1 degree per degree a second), up to 14 degrees, smoothed, level by the landing.
  - **Found, not assumed**: 64 rolled candidates, each refused if any point comes within 2 units of a tower's box (cornices included, through `CitySwing.Towers`, the boxes by block) or the island; the most walled-in of the clear ones flies. `CitySwingTests` over three cities and 36 poses each: a swing for at least 95 % (126 of 128 in the probe), landing exactly on the pose with the game's lens and no bank, never within 1 unit of a box at 1500 poses a shot, and the ride inside its limits (65 u/s, the view turning 170 and pitching 80 degrees a second, 14 degrees of bank, 8 u/s in the last half second, 5.5-8.5 s). Each was seen to fail on a mutation.
- ⚠ **The order alternates what the lens sees** (street, plaza, swing, rooftop; 4.5, 4.0, 4.0, 5.0 s, or the roof skim's 5.5 s where no roof qualifies): two canyon shots in a row from slightly different places read as a glitch. `CityIntroShotsTests` builds the prologue over the real dressed roofs (`CityRooftops.ArrangeShowcases`, no device) and holds a minimum shot length, no equal consecutive names, the swing never next to the street, a minimum turn or travel at every cut, clearance from the real towers' boxes, and the rooftop's whole mast inside the frame, its foot over the skip hint, at 3840×1600, 1920×1080 and 1600×900.

## The volcano (#530) and the aurora (#531)

- **Volcano** (`VolcanoIntroShots`, through `SceneRenderer.VolcanoGroundHeight`, a mirror of `Volcano.fx`), the owner's order (#975): *the volcano* (3.6 s), a push in from 600 to 520 units off the cone's axis, 120 over the plain, 22-45 degrees round from the arena's side and drifting back 8, so the cone, its rivers and steam and the island stand in one frame; *the eruption* (3.8 s), a low push in at the cone's foot (310 to 270 out, 12 over the ground) 85-125 degrees round the other way, the look 40 over the vent; *the crater* (5.4 s), a run **beside the axis, never over it** (straight above a fixed look-at there is no horizontal forward to build an up vector from), lifted over the rim so the bowl opens late. Then the tour's last leg, 17.3 s in all.
  - ⚠ **The old first two shots were one picture.** The flank (an arc 129 out, lens on the vent) and the crater run both stood on the arena's side and looked at the vent: at the cut the lens moved 56 units and the view turned 18 degrees at best. `VolcanoIntroShotsTests` holds every cut to film's thirty-degree rule AND 100 units (the cities' test takes 22 degrees or 40 units); the new cuts turn at least 61 degrees over at least 248 units, and the old flank put back second fails it.
  - **The burst is cued, not hoped for.** A burst is on 4.5 s of every 19, jittered in its period, so a fixed shot met one about a third of the time, and the storm's stretching cannot reach events 9 to 30 s apart. `SceneRenderer.CueVolcanoBurst` puts one at full size 0.9 s into the eruption shot (`VolcanoLava.CueBurst`): the natural bursts that would start between the intro's first frame and the schedule's shortest gap (0.45 of the period, 8.55 s) after the cue give way, one already running is never cut, and the light, the jets, the deck's glow and the boom all read the one schedule (`VolcanoBurstCueTests`). ⚠ **The burst runs on the wall clock and the intro does not**: a pause stops the intro's clock and a hitch holds it to `MAX_INTRO_STEP` a frame, so cued only at the build the burst went off behind the pause page and the eruption shot then showed a quiet cone for the quiet's 8.55 s (found by the review of the first cut). The eruption shot cues it again on its first frame (`IntroShot.Start`, off `IntroContext.Clock`) whenever the intro has fallen more than 0.1 s behind (`ChapterIntroShotStartTests`).
  - The weather fades in from the menu's over `WEATHER_FADE_SECONDS` (8 s, deliberate), so the first two shots play under the previous scene's clouds.
- **Aurora** (`AuroraIntroShots`, off `ForestScatterRenderer.Scatter`'s instance arrays, re-authored in #963): *the wood* (3.4 s) is *found*, the best of 600 rolled runs heading within 35 degrees of the curtains' bearing, measured against every conifer and snag (crown plus 1.4), trunks on both hands weighted (⚠ a bare count chose a grove's edge), inside the far stand's ring (⚠ its spires are not in the planting, and a run among them flew through one: a black frame); *the moon* (3.2 s) glides 1.6 over the open snow beside the island towards the Moon, the lens 7 degrees under it, the wood's shadows running at the lens; *the curtains* (3.4 s) drift 2.2 over the snow behind the island from them, the lens 14 degrees up, on the spot with the fewest trunks round it (clear by seven: ⚠ four put a crown on the lens). Then the tour's last leg, 14.5 s in all. Heights come from `SceneRenderer.AuroraGroundHeight` (the snow as drawn, its drifts included).

## Smoothness of the terrain-hugging shots (#645)

The mountains' pass, the desert's crest and the volcano's crater were "like a roller coaster". `Tests/BS3D.Tests/IntroShotSmoothnessTests.cs` holds the fixes (seen firing on each):

- **Speed**: 30-59 u/s down to about 19-25 (pass 85 units in 4.2 s, crest 72 in 4.0, crater 5.4 s).
- **Height**: `AridIntroPaths.Hug` averaged with one box, whose corners are accelerations. `passes` runs several narrower boxes (three make a piecewise-quadratic blur), half-widths summing to no more than the window, so the lens still never dips under a crest.
- **Pitch**: a lens looking along its travel tips into every slip face; `IntroShot`'s `pitchDamping` (0-1, default 0) levels the look (0.6 crest, 0.4 pass); the crater is pinned on the vent.

Not measured: the outback's approach and the `IntroGround` shots (`passes` and `pitchDamping` are the tools).

# Chapter intro prologues

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md). The flight itself is in [The chapter intro](chapter-intro.md); the remaining scenes' shots are in [the other scenes' shots](chapter-intro-scene-shots.md).

A scene that opens on a **prologue** plays cut-together `IntroShot`s (a path and a look) before the tour's last leg. `Effects/IntroPrologues.For(SceneKind, IntroContext)` picks the builder (`IntroContext`: the renderer, the city, the two plantings, the dome's sun, the wall clock now and to read later, and the pose the intro hands back at); `IntroPaths` holds the shared geometry. A declarative shot list was rejected: builders *search* their scene with reject-and-retry loops, which is logic, not data.

## What every prologue shares

- **A prologue is taken with a cut and skipped with one.** The blend cannot make a cut (it would glide the lens through the towers), so `Begin` sets it to 1 with a prologue and `TrySkip` drops it to 0.
- **After a prologue the tour flies only its last leg** (`ARRIVAL_SECONDS`, 4.5 s): the tour's scene stands lie on a circle round the arena that in a city runs through the towers. **Unless the last shot lands** (`IntroShot.Lands`, #972): a shot whose path and look end on the gameplay pose is followed by no tour leg; `ChapterIntro` holds its last frame while the gun's pose blends back in, a blend between two identical poses. The cities, the meadow, the big top, the savanna and the desert land, each through the shared landing (below).
- **A shot may be posed by the wall clock** (`IntroShot.OnTheWallClock`, #1116): every frame it is on screen it asks where to stand at the wall clock's second, not its own, for a subject that is a function of the wall clock and a lens a few units from it (the Grid's ride beside a light cycle). It still holds for its seconds of the intro's clock, so a pause or a hitch lengthens it and never moves the lens off its subject. Every other shot walks its path on its own eased clock.
- **A shot may bank** (`IntroShot.RollAt`, #972): `ChapterIntro.Roll` reaches `RecoilCamera.BaseRoll` in play and in the menu's replay, which writes it every frame, level when no tour flies: written only while one flew, it kept the bank a session left on the camera when Pause and Main Menu were taken mid-swing. The tour itself stays level.
- **One concrete object per shot, an establishing view first.** `[intro]` and `[tour]` name the shots, e.g. `[intro] block 'The Eruption' (11/14), cut 'the river' 3.5s | 'the eruption' 3.4s | 'the crater' 5.4s | 'the village' 3.6s | cut -> map 43 out at 39deg -> game pose, 4.5s; 20.4s in all`. Totals run 11-17 s, the volcano 20.4 with its river and its village (16.5 at `Low`, #1127, #1220), the cities about 24-26 with their facades (`High` and `Ultra`) and 20-22 without (their swings home are 6.6-8.9 s and land; 21.5 or 22 when the old reel stands in), the meadow 20.4-21.1 at the gameplay pose (its brook home is 10.4-11.1 s and lands, #1107, #1188, #1187), the big top 16.2-17.2 at Tightrope's pose (its gate home is 8.4-9.4 s and lands, #1118; 15.5 when the gate and the tour's leg stand in), the savanna 12.2-15.8 at its levels' poses over 400 scene seeds (its grass home is 2.9-6.0 s and lands, #1108; 14.3 when the reel and the tour's leg stand in), the desert 23.9-24.2 at Rope's pose (its scorpion home is 9.4-9.9 s and lands, #1110; 24.0 when the scorpion shot and the tour's leg stand in).
- **`IntroShot` moves without corners (#645, #1109)**: `Pose` extrapolates the look-ahead along the last leg (swapping in a direction swung the view in one frame); `At` is a Catmull-Rom (linear interpolation changed velocity at every point), whose missing neighbour past either end is extrapolated along the end leg: with the end point repeated in its place the lens left every cut at 69 % of the speed it had a frame later and slowed into the next the same way (`AShotKeepsItsSpeedThroughTheCut`). A shot that lands still ends exactly on its last point.

## Landing on the gameplay pose (`IntroLanding`, #972, #1107, #1108, #1118, #1110)

A last shot that ends on the gameplay pose is a scene's route plus `Game/Effects/IntroLanding.cs`, which does everything that does not depend on the scene. The cities' swing home, the meadow's brook home, the big top's gate home, the savanna's grass home (#1108) and the desert's scorpion home (#1110) fly through it. A scene lays a dense path (cut to 0.5, or laid a frame at a time on the shot's clock, as the desert's is) that ends on `IntroContext.GamePosition`, gives each point up to where the deceleration begins a speed, and scores it; the landing does the rest:

- **The release onto the pose.** `Release`: a cubic Bezier off the line the route is flying along, arriving along a direction `Arrival` rolls (the pose's facing half the time, halfway three times in ten, still along the route the rest, the view swivelling as it slows). `TurnIn`: for a route that goes round the arena, off a circle about the axis onto the pose, the distance easing in from the circle's and the bearing easing out, so it leaves along the circle and arrives down the pose's own radial line (its facing), or partly along the turn (`slant`). ⚠ The distance only shrinks for a pose **inside** the circle: past it every point of the turn in is nearer the axis than the pose, so a scene lays its circle outside the poses it lands on (the meadow's 48 to 60 out, the game's lens 28 to 45). ⚠ **Over a short drop, straight down the facing is a hairpin**: the big top's circle stands only 4 to 13 outside the pose (its king poles stand 46 out), and a turn in with `slant` 0 turned at radii of 2 to 6 where 0.55 turned at 7 to 25, so it rolls more of its arrivals along the turn than the meadow does. ⚠ **A `Release` off a circle onto a pose inside it cannot arrive along the facing**: the circle's tangent 30 to 50 degrees short of the pose points away from it, and the meadow's first cut turned at radii of 5 to 13. `NoTighterThan` refuses a plan that turns tighter than a radius the scene states (a turn's rate is the speed over its radius; the release, decelerating, may take a tighter one than the cruise).
- **Timing** (`Fly`): the route walked at its speeds, then a cubic in time that decelerates steadily to rest on the pose (`ReleaseTimeScale` 2), the points evenly timed. ⚠ A scene starts it early enough for the last half second to be slow: begun only at the meadow's turn in, a 42 u/s cruise still came onto the pose at 9 u/s; begun at least 2.6 s out, the meadow's is at most 5. **In the drone style** (`Style.DroneSeconds`, #1187, the meadow's) the speed falls from the route's to rest on a smoothstep instead, over the same twice the length over the entry speed (`ReleaseTimeScale` 2, which it asks), so the deceleration starts and ends with no step in the acceleration, where the cubic's steady one steps in and out; the meadow comes onto the pose at 1.8 u/s at the most in its last half second, where the steady deceleration came on at 4.4 (`MeadowLandingTests` holds it under 3).
- **Look, bank, lens.** The look along the travel's direction averaged over ±`LookSmoothSeconds` (⚠ not a chord to a point ahead, which cut the city's corners and pitched the view 140 degrees a second), following `LookPitchFollow` of its climb, turned `LookInward` of the way in plan towards the pose's target wherever the route asks it (`Route.Lean`; 0 for the city; the meadow's 0.7 round the island keeps the cluster in frame, where a lean of 31 degrees left the island at the frame's edge), its point raised `LookRise` of the way from the travel's height to the pose's target's where the route leans (0 for the cities and the meadow; 1 for the big top, whose circle runs close under the cluster: looked along level, the cluster stood in the frame's top corner, its glass cut by the edge), easing onto the pose's own look over the last `LookLandSeconds` and ending on it exactly. The bank is `RollPerTurn` of the heading's rate of turn, up to `MaxRollDegrees`, smoothed and level by the landing. The lens eases from `WideLens` times the game's to the game's.
- **The drone style** (`Style.DroneSeconds`, #1187, the meadow's brook home; 0 for every other landing). The look's bearing off the pose's target, as each point sees it, is averaged by a Gaussian of `DroneSeconds` (0.8 s for the meadow, `Steady`), so the arena drifts across the frame slowly whatever the travel under it does: the meadow's view turns at 7.7 °/s at the most over its first 3 s, the swerve round the willows under it, where with the look along the travel it swung at up to 40 °/s, and nowhere faster than 50 °/s, the circle round the island and the turn in off it included (49.9 at the most over `MeadowLandingTests`' sweep of poses). The travel's direction under it is averaged by a Gaussian of half that, its first and last carried on past the ends (⚠ a box cut short at the shot's start is a sum that grows until it first fits whole: the look's turn kinked there, the meadow's at 185 °/s² 0.3 s in). The look lands and the bank levels on a smootherstep (`Smoother`), and the timed points are rounded over `DRONE_ROUNDING_SECONDS` (0.08 s) before the shot's spline is laid through them. ⚠ **A route is walked as a polyline**: its points half a unit apart, each with a speed that steps from one to the next; through as many timed points as a frame has, unrounded, the lens's acceleration jumped by up to 30 u/s² a frame (1787 u/s³), and through 240 over the meadow's 10 s the spline's own knots changed it at each (441 u/s³ in the turn in). The meadow takes 600 points and the rounding.
- **A shot that opens on a subject** (`IntroLanding.Subject`, #1110, the desert's scorpion): the look is held on the subject's point until `HoldSeconds`, then turns off it onto the travel's look over `ReleaseSeconds` in yaw, pitch and distance (a straight line between a point three units off and one thirty out turns the view fastest where it passes the lens), and the bank stays level while it is held. The subject moves on the shot's own seconds (the scene re-cues it on every frame, `IntroShot.Track`) and the shot's points are spaced on its eased clock, so the landing reads it at `IntroShot.Uneased` of each point. ⚠ **From `HoldSeconds` on the look turns off the subject's direction as it was then, not off the subject**: a lens lifting off a few units from a scorpion it still looked at craned down after it, and pitched the view 57 degrees a second down and back up, near three times the drone limit below. Held, the subject falls away down the frame. ⚠ **The look hands over from the subject to that direction eased**, over the release's first `HANDOVER_SHARE` (a quarter) on a smootherstep, as the release itself is: switched in one frame, the turn the look was making to follow a walking subject stopped dead, a step in its rate. A scene lifting its lens off the subject starts letting go that much of the release before the lift (the desert's `LetGo`), so the hand-over is done before the lens climbs: begun at the lift, the look still on the scorpion as the lens rose craned down after it at 160 °/s². The shot's `onFrame` is the scene's (the desert re-cues its scorpion there). With no subject the landing reads none of this, so the cities', the meadow's, the big top's and the savanna's flights do not change with it.
- **The search** (`Best`): rolled candidates each laid in full, the ones a clearance predicate refuses dropped, the highest score flown; null when none is clear, and the scene plays the reel it had before, its tour leg included. The city checks its towers inside its own `Lay`, before the roll its score takes, and passes no predicate.
- ⚠ **The arena's keep-out cannot be a landing's clearance**: `IntroGround.ARENA_KEEP_OUT` refuses any lens within 44 of the axis and the game's lens stands 28 to 45 out, so it refuses every last leg. A landing keeps the lens no nearer the axis than the pose itself (`KeepOutFor`: the pose's distance less half a unit, and the scene's keep-out everywhere else the route already stands outside it); the island's drum, the gun, the glass and the cluster all stand nearer. The meadow, the big top, the savanna and the desert refuse a pose inside the island's own radius plus 2 outright (the menu's crane stands as near as 15 out, over the island), and their reels then end on the tour's leg.
- ⚠ **Nor can what the game's own lens stands inside.** A planting's solids are its meshes' bounding spheres, and some reach the gameplay lens: the savanna's bank round the island and the tufts and stones planted on it (a tuft's sphere 26.6 out reached the Gallery's pose 30 out), and its trees' spheres, up to 31 round an acacia whose crown reaches 17. Held by those spheres the pose itself is not clear and no landing is laid. The savanna holds them by the pose instead (`SavannaLanding`): within the bank's reach the lens is never nearer the axis than the pose nor lower than it less half a unit, and a solid whose sphere reaches the pose is kept at the pose's own clearance of it, less half a unit. What the game's lens clears all level, a lens no nearer clears too. Where the trees' spheres still close every way in, it holds the trees by their own outlines instead (the savanna in [Chapter intro scene shots](chapter-intro-scene-shots.md)).

## Smoothness of the terrain-hugging shots (#645, #1109, #1112)

A lens over rough ground rides like drone footage: its height and its tilt change over a second, never from one frame to the next. `Tests/BS3D.Tests/IntroShotSmoothnessTests.cs` builds every shot whose height `AridIntroPaths.Hug` lays (the desert's erg, pyramids and crest; the mountains' range, pass and summit; the outback's plain, approach and crown; the volcano's river, crater and village; the Moon's and Mars's tracks, each the same run every roll, Mars's over `OffworldGround.MarsCeiling`, the one copy of its ground the CPU has), the desert's shots low on its sand (#1110: the beetle and the scorpion, whose looks follow their creature, and the scorpion home, whose take-off off the sand is laid on its own clock) and every shot of the meadow's (#1187: the valley, the bales, the path, the bench, the brook and the brook home, on `MeadowIntroShotsTests`' planting, a layout a roll), on the shipped configs over twelve rolls (the meadow's two orbits, cheap to build, over 1000 layouts), samples it at 60 fps on its own eased clock, and holds **peaks**, since a judder is one bad frame in a hundred good ones:

| limit | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| view pitch rate | 20 °/s | a drone gimbal's tilt |
| view pitch acceleration | 40 °/s² | that tilt reached in no less than half a second |
| vertical acceleration | 20 u/s² | the climb rate changes over a second, not a frame |
| vertical jerk | 150 u/s³ | the acceleration changes by at most 2.5 u/s² a frame |
| yaw rate | 50 °/s | |
| yaw acceleration | 100 °/s² | that turn reached in no less than half a second, as the tilt is (#1187) |
| jerk, all three axes | 300 u/s³ | the acceleration changes by at most 5 u/s² a frame along any axis (#1187) |
| bank rate | 20 °/s | rolled as the tilt is (#1220) |
| bank acceleration | 40 °/s² | that roll reached in no less than half a second |
| speed (crest, pass, crater) | 28 u/s | "too fast" at 30-59 (#645) |
| clearance | the shot's own, every frame | smooth through a dune is not a fix |
| mean height over the clearance, a shot with no floor | 9 | smooth by flying high is not a fix |

The derivatives are taken from three frames inside each end: a cut is a discontinuity of everything (its speed has its own test, above). The worst on the shipped configs over the twelve rolls: 13 u/s² (the outback's crown, a crane), 122 u/s³ (the crater), 35 °/s² (the pass; the scorpion home 19, as its look leaves the scorpion), 18.9 °/s (the crown), 8.8 over the clearance (the pass, on its worst roll); sideways, 84 °/s² (the scorpion home) and 289 u/s³ (the volcano's village, an orbit at 60 u/s). The volcano's river (#1220), a helicopter's glide at 28 u/s, reads 4.0 u/s², 18 u/s³, 0.4 °/s of tilt, 9.3 °/s and 7 °/s² of turn and 65 u/s³, and banks at up to 6.7 °/s and 25 °/s², the one shot here that banks. The meadow's brook home, a flight round the island at up to 45 u/s, reads 9.6 u/s², 32 u/s³, 13.8 °/s and 20 °/s² of tilt, 48.9 °/s and 50 °/s² of turn and 178 u/s³. ⚠ Its route is pieces laid end to end, each of its own curvature: unrounded and flown with its look along the travel, it turned the view at 56 °/s, swung it at 224 °/s² and jumped the lens at 3111 u/s³ where they met. The highest frame is not held: the lift over a cut crest is a plateau whose height is the crest's cut however wide it is, so a bound on it fails only where the mean already does. A designed floor is held by its own test: a straight climb and a level height are flown exactly as laid, a crane lands on its foot and its top, and the real crown ends on `CRANE_OVER_CROWN` on every roll.

- **Height** (`AridIntroPaths.Hug`): the clearance over the highest ground at each point and on all four hands of it (or the shot's floor, a designed crane, fixed height or the crater's ramp, where higher), **rounded** by a Gaussian over `ROUNDING_SECONDS` (0.7 s) of travel at the shot's peak speed (`IntroShot.PEAK_PACE`, so a fast run is rounded over as much time as a slow one), then **lifted** where the rounding cut a crest under the ground's clearance: by the deepest cut within three `LIFT_SECONDS` (0.4 s) of travel, averaged by a Gaussian of that sigma over three more, so every point keeps its clearance by construction and the lift is a swell, not a step. A box has a corner at each end, and the lens's vertical acceleration jumped at every one; a Gaussian keeps a straight climb and a level height exactly as they are.
  - **A crane's smoothstep is rounded, not kept**: the lens flies nearer a straight climb, over the smoothstep in its lower half and under it in its upper half by about a fourteenth of the rise on a 3.2 s shot (the erg 1.7 units of its 24, the outback crown 4.5 of its 64), with its foot and its top where they were laid. ⚠ What the rounding takes off a floor's own curve is **not lifted back**, and must not be: the lift answers to the ground's clearance alone, never to the floor, because it holds a cut over its reach, and lifting a crane's upper half back would carry its top over the design (the crown 4.5 units over `CRANE_OVER_CROWN` on every roll, the erg 1.7). Flown exactly as designed instead, the crown's smoothstep on the eased clock peaks at 30 u/s², over the limit. The crater's ramp is rounded the same way and passes at most 0.3 under itself.
  - ⚠ **The lift is a plateau, and on a low run it is most of the shot.** It is full at the crest and tapers to nothing six `LIFT_SECONDS` either side, in travel at peak pace: the lens starts to rise that long before a crest it was cut under. One roll of the outback's approach (clearance 2.4) passes a 3-unit outcrop near its end: the lens rises from a sixth of the way in and ends 5.2 over its clearance, where the other eleven rolls end within 0.6 of it. The pyramids' run (clearance 7) flies 4.1 over it on average over the twelve rolls, 5.8 on its worst roll, and up to 12.1 at single moments (14.5 over the sand at the cut on one roll, twice its clearance).
  - ⚠ **The heights are carried on past both ends** for the Gaussian's reach. A window that stops at the ends kinks where it first fits whole: a jolt in the lens's climb a quarter to half a second after every cut, the worst frame of most of the shots. Open ground is read on along the plan's end directions; over a floor the heights are turned over their end point (2 h(end) - h(end ∓ k)), which keeps a straight climb straight and a crane's foot and top where they were laid (read on, the level plain beside the outback crane's foot rounded the foot four units up).
  - `rise` makes a run climb ahead of the ground: the volcano's crater run rounds the highest ground within 1.2 s of travel, which keeps it as high over the flank as it was approved (#530).
  - The cost of the two constants: a narrower lift (0.25-0.3 s) hardly lowers the lens and puts the jerk back over its limit; a wider rounding flies higher (2 s put the pass 11.8 over its clearance).
- **Pitch**: a lens looking along its travel tips into every slip face; `IntroShot`'s `pitchDamping` (0-1, default 0) levels the look (0.6 crest, 0.4 pass).

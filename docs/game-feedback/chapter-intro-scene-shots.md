# Chapter intro: the other scenes' shots

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md). Mechanism and shared rules: [Chapter intro prologues](chapter-intro-prologues.md). Each list is `IntroShot[]` from `Game/Effects/<Scene>IntroShots.cs` (#559), the establishing view first, then the tour's last leg.

## How a path is kept safe

- **Terrain scenes read the land off `TerrainMirror`** (`Prazsky.Core`: CPU mirrors of `Desert.fx`, `Mountain.fx`, `Outback.fx` with `RockLayer`'s monoliths, plus meadow, forest, polar) and lift the lens over a **dilated ground** (`AridIntroPaths.Hug`: the highest ground a few points either side, off the line on both hands, plus clearance), so it rises before a crest and never cuts a dune's knife edge. None of the four arid terrains is seeded by `sceneseed=`.
- **`Game/Effects/IntroGround.cs`** (savanna, forest, tropical, meadow) holds the height mirror and every solid as a capsule (each plant its `PlantFigure`); a path is taken only when all 96 points keep a margin off all of them. ⚠ The savanna's spacing footprints are not the plants (a bush's crown overhangs). ⚠ In the forest a one-unit margin passed under a broadleaf's crown; it is two.
- **`IntroGround.SightClear`** (#610) marches the sight line to the subject, so the baobab walk sees its baobab (not asked of the acacia's track, where neighbours' crowns block). ⚠ Ended exactly on a subject's own sphere, rounding decides whether its last sample is inside it: end it a little short.
- **The Moon and Mars have no CPU mirror**: `OffworldGround` gives a **ceiling** and each path one constant lift of 2.5-3 over it.
- **The strange scenes** have no mesh, so `SceneRenderer` carries host copies of the shaders' placement ("Where the strange scenes' things stand"), and `IntroShot` has a **look-at path** for a moving subject.

## Scene by scene

- **Sea**: *the island* (arc round its flank at the waterline), *the depths* (#759, under the island up through the fish and light shafts, the cluster seen through the funnel's glass; the only shot under water), *the climb to the sun* (#652, ending on the glitter path leading to it). *The swell* was replaced because its cut into the climb read as a glitch (same heading at the lens floor over featureless swell). The flank and climb stay over `CREST_REACH` (4.2) above `LevelY`.
- **Desert**: *the erg*, *the crest*, *the flock*. **Outback**: *the plain*, *the approach*, *the crown*, round the tallest monolith. ⚠ The approach comes in 50-110 degrees off the arena line (it once started beside the island, under its rim). **Mountains**: *the range*, *the pass*, *the summit* (turned round **28 under it**, so it stands over the lens; from ten over, a row of crests could not be told apart).
- **Meadow** (`MeadowIntroShots`, each shot ends on a subject): *the valley* (2.8 s), *the bales* (5.6), *the brook* (3.2), *the path* (3.2), *the bench* (5.6), then the tour's last leg: 24.9 s in all, three past the cities' 22, because the owner asked both orbits slower (#945; the test holds it under 25).
  - **The bales and the bench are slow, close, low half circles (#893)**: `IntroGround.Arc` of pi round the subject, the lens 0.7 rising to 1.1 over the ground under it, the look on the subject's middle, 32 degrees a second round it (39 until #945: the owner's "a little more slowly"). The bench's is the arena's side of it (it faces the arena, the oak behind), across the seat's face.
  - ⚠ **The radius is searched, not computed**: from the subject's sphere plus `ORBIT_AIR` in `RADIUS_STEP`s up to `RADIUS_LIMIT` times that, until the whole subject (a cylinder over each figure's footprint, `FOOTPRINT_SHARE` of the sphere) is in frame at 16:9 with its foot over the skip hint, asked at 13 stands. Since #945 ("a little farther") the search starts `ORBIT_AIR` 4.9 off the sphere and a subject may fill 0.78 of the frame (`FRAME_LIMIT`, 0.88 before): it lands at 7.5 for the bench and 9.8 to 15.1 for a pair of bales, 11.4 on average over 24 layouts (6.3 and 9.3 to 12.4, mean 10.2, before): a bale 3.2 high fills the lens's 47 degrees from 5.5 off its near rim, and the end of a pair is the near rim. The lens's height changes the bales' by under 2 % over 48 layouts (mean 10.2 with it at 0.7-1.1, 10.3 at 0.9-1.3), so it stays low.
  - ⚠ **The lens keeps off the old trees by their STEM** (the oak's crown sphere reaches below the ground, `docs/scenes/meadow.md`): held whole, every half circle round the bench was refused. It keeps off every low solid within reach (`MeadowFigures.Solids`), 3 units off the pond's shore and the brook's bank (their reeds; the bed is under the water), and sees the subject from every one of five stands (`SightClear`, which ends 0.5 outside the subject's own sphere: ended on it, rounding refused half of them).
  - **A group of bales is the bales within 0.8 of touching**, a pair before a lone bale before a row of three (`MeadowIntroShots.ShapeRank`: ranked by shape, not by the distance from two, which would tie a lone bale with a row), nearest the arena first. `MeadowIntroShotsTests` (a layout of its own from the planting's constants, since the planting needs a device) holds the half circle, the length, the lens's height, the clearances, the water and the frame at 3840x1600, 1920x1080 and 1600x900, each seen to fail with its check removed (`Framed`, `Dry`, `Clear`, `SightClear`, the half circle, the seconds, the lens's height, the stem rule, the order of the groups; the frame test's own floor over the skip hint fails with the floor raised or the look raised). ⚠ `Framed`'s own hint floor is the one check never seen to bind: removed, no layout of 24 changed enough to fail a test.
  - **The bench's shadow per tier**: the bench is a park bench since #1022 (`MeadowBenchGeometry`: slats 0.16 deep and 0.07 thick, 0.024 apart, at the bench's scale, `BENCH_SCALE`), so Low and Medium's 2048 map (0.127 units a texel) holds a slat in 1.2 texels and High's 4096 (0.063) in 2.5: the slats' gaps do not show in the shadow on the ground at any tier, it reads as one seat-shaped shadow whose edge sharpens with the tier. ⚠ The figures this line carried (a plank 3.5 texels deep, 13 to 19 % of the frame's pixels differing between `alt=shadowmap=2048` and `4096`) were measured on the box bench and were not re-taken. ⚠ `shadowmap=` alone is ignored by the Testbed, see "The dials" in `docs/testbed/capture-and-measurement.md`.
- **Savanna**: *the plain*, *the baobab*, *the acacia*. **Forest**: *the forest*, *the grove*, *the tree*; ⚠ a run *through* the wood (the aurora's shot) does not carry here (240 trees; the few clear runs had five trees within twelve units). **Tropical**: *the lagoon*, *the beach*, *the palm*.
- **Dream**: *the marbled sky*, *the glass*, *the underside* (#655, replacing a dolly into one soft orb). ⚠ **The dream writes no depth**, so the island covers any solid in front of it: shots refuse a lens whose line to the subject or funnel a solid's bound touches. The solids orbit on the wall clock, so shots are laid out for the seconds they play in (`IntroContext.WallClock`).
- **Cavern** (#958, #984): *the passage* (out of a passage off the hall, low over the water, the island ahead; *the hall* where no passage clears), *the crystals* (from beyond the cluster, the island behind it), *the shaft*, *the underside*, every path checked against the hall's own geometry before it is used ([The cavern](../scenes/cavern.md#the-chapter-intro)).
- **Storm**: *the deck*, *the tops*, *the strike*, **caught, not hoped for**: when is a pure function of the wall clock (`TryGetSceneEvent`), where is fixed the first time it is asked at or after the onset (#750), so the builder stretches the first two shots (2.6-4.4 s) to open the third about 1.2 s before it, and tests every lens point against every cell (inside a puff the frame is milk).
- **Polar**: *the icesheet*, *the crevasses*, *the pressure ridge*; the crevasse pass keeps off the research camp (#747).
- **Big Top** (#752; the owner: "so that we see the ceiling ... a view from a spectator's place forward to the island. And cuts"): *the canvas* (low on the ring floor beside a king pole, the look climbing the pole and on over the roof towards the crown), *the seats* (a dolly along a row high in the stands at a seated eye's height, `CircusIntroShots.SeatY` being `Circus.fx`'s rake, kept off the poles' bearings and the entrance gap, the look on the island), *the gate* (in through the performers' entrance, low). The tent has no CPU geometry, so `CircusIntroShotsTests` holds every pose to its figures: inside the wall and under the canvas cone, over the floor and the rake, off the island, the gate inside the gap. Until it, the Big Top was the one scene with no prologue and flew the generic tour, inside the poles' ring.
- **Moon, Mars, space and the Grid use ABSOLUTE lenses** (each frames one object); other prologues widen the lens they are handed, so a tour replayed from the menu shows what play will.
  - **Moon**: *the plain*, *the crater* (⚠ a 2.3 to 1.5 radii rise smeared under motion blur, so it travels half that), *the Earth*. **Mars**: *the plain*, *the mesa* (only across open plain), *Phobos* (⚠ over bare plain it was a tan sky with a dot, so a mesa stands on it).
  - **Space**: *the station*, *the drain*, *the nebula* (⚠ looking out from outside the rim it had no scale). **Grid**: *the grid*, *the cube*, *the ring*, every path tested against every solid as its box (`SceneRenderer.GridSolids`, `TryGetGridRing`) and against sight lines (⚠ a slide ten units clear of a tower still ended on its dark side).
  - ⚠ The Moon's Earth shot showed diagonal sky streaks: the motion blur's background reprojected at the cluster's depth (see the #559 note under "Motion blur" in `docs/rendering.md`); the tour has no motion blur.

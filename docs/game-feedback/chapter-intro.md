# The chapter intro

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md).

The campaign's other camera takeover (#267). When the level about to be built is the **first index of a new block** (new scene, dome, music theme and style; "The campaign is nine blocks of five" in `docs/formats-and-tools.md`), the camera tours the arena first. `Game/Effects/ChapterIntro.cs` owns the flight; `GameplayScreen` owns the trigger, the block detection and the gun not answering. Scene-specific shots: [prologues](chapter-intro-prologues.md) and [the other scenes' shots](chapter-intro-scene-shots.md).

## Trigger, log line, replay

- **The trigger reads the milestone celebration's block query.** `LevelSet.HasBlocks` false refuses the feature (`BlockRange`'s fallback would open an intro before every level); otherwise `BlockRange(index, out first, out _); first == index` is the whole test.
- **And on every Continue from the main menu** (#870, `GameplayScreen.RequestWelcomeTour`), whatever the level's place in its chapter and whether or not the chapter was toured this launch: the owner, a player continuing has probably not played for a while and needs a moment to settle, so the same full prologue, skippable. The request is spent by the next build, so a retry, the next level or the picker after it plays none, and a resume from the pause page (a standing session) plays none at all. Its `[intro]` line says `on Continue`. `continue` on the command line presses the menu's first entry at boot.
- **Shown once a run of the program, never persisted**: `_chapterIntroShown`, a `HashSet<int>` keyed by the block's **first index** (names are not unique), never cleared, so a retry does not replay it; `Add`'s return value is test and record at once. Not `PlayerProgress`: a fresh launch shows every opening again.
- **One `[intro]` line per opening**, with the block's name and place and the net turn: `[intro] block 'The Meadow' (1/11), 'the flowers' 46 out at 6deg -> in -> map 40 out at 42deg -> game pose, 9.5s`.
- **Photograph an opening with `level=` alone**: ⚠ `tour` with `level=` held the intro on its first frame, HUD up (cause not chased). Launch without taking focus (a keystroke typed into the terminal once landed in the window and skipped the intro).
- **Replay from the scene menu (#406).** Picking a scene on `ScenePage` flies its tour against the menu's backdrop; it is the very same `ChapterIntro`, and while it flies the page **stands aside** (`MenuPage.StandsAside`, #861): the host draws none of it (its focus prompt included, which hiding the plate alone left over the flight) and hands it no walk, Enter, wheel or Back. Space, Enter, Escape, a click, or the pad's A, B or right trigger skips the tour instead (`BackdropScreen.TrySkipTour`, `[skip] scene tour` in the log), so the press that skips cannot also pick another scene under it, which broke the flight. **The menu's flight waits while a replayed tour flies** (`AdvanceOrbit` with no time): the tour ends on the pose it was handed at its start, and a flight that had gone on turning under a 20-second reel was 76 to 114 degrees round by then, so the blend back swept round the arena. The chapter flythrough's own skip hint shows over it (`PlayHud.DrawSkipHintAlone`, in the hand the menus last saw). Not from the pause's scene list, where no tour flies (#636). ⚠ **`tour` on the command line** opens the scene menu with the flight already running: a synthetic click never reaches this window, so a script cannot photograph a replay otherwise. `sceneseed=` pins a scene's scatter, not the shot rolled through it (`BackdropScreen.TOUR_RANDOM`).

## The shot

- **A pose and a blend, the drop cinematic's shape** ([The drop cinematic](drop-cinematic.md)): the level's solved figures go in at `Begin`, `UpdateCamera` Lerps the pose over the gameplay pose, and at a blend of 0 the gameplay pose returns bit for bit. No `TimeScale` (the cluster hangs).
- **An arc of the scene, not the map**: **four Catmull-Rom keys of position and look-at**: the environment (wide, low, across the rim), the arena (the bowl and drain), the map (one proper look from a high bearing) and the **arrival, the gameplay pose verbatim**. The first two legs are the scene's own (#289): `SceneRenderer.TryGetViewpoint` ("The scenes' observation points" in `docs/scenes.md`). `DURATION_SECONDS` is 9.5.
- **The wide legs scale off the level's solved stand-off** (`GameCameraFit.Solve` has run inside `InstallLevel`): the environment key at that distance x a rolled 1.9-2.4, the map key at 1.25-1.45x, look-ats off `ArenaIsland.RADIUS`/`TOP_Y`.
- ⚠ **The stands are swept in polar terms (#409).** In Cartesian terms two near-opposite stands put the chord through the arena's axis and the floor pushed the lens *up* over the island, looking down the funnel (81 degrees). `Begin`/`Frame` spline (azimuth, elevation, radius), so no leg bows inward; the azimuths are **one continuous turn ending on the gun** and `ChooseTurn` picks the direction against it. `Frame`'s floor (`gameDistance * 0.92`) never fires now and reports `[intro] WARNING` if it does (#519).
- ⚠ **A hitch holds the flight (#645)**: `ChapterIntro.MAX_INTRO_STEP` (a 20 fps frame) caps one frame's advance.

## Handing control back

- ⚠ **The release must not be gentler than the drop cinematic's.** `Engaged` gates the gun on `_blend > 0f` and an exponential crosses the 0.002 snap only about 6.2 taus in: a `BLEND_OUT_TAU` of 0.5 left a **3.1 s dead zone** where a skipped tour held a gun that would not fire. It is 0.16; the grab, `BLEND_IN_TAU` 0.35, is gentle on purpose.
- **Skip is Space / LMB / gamepad A or right trigger / a touch**, through the same gate: `GameplayScreen.CameraTakeoverEngaged` is `_cinematic.Engaged || _chapterIntro.Engaged` and `SkipCameraTakeover` tries both `TrySkip`s. **At most one is ever engaged** (the intro blocks the shot the drop cinematic needs; `TearDown` resets it). `SKIP_LOCKOUT` is 0.3 s (the button that advanced into the level also skips). Hint: "The skip hint" in [the drop cinematic](drop-cinematic.md).
- **Every opening can be skipped, the player's very first included (#1106, the owner: always).** A new chapter's, a Continue's and the first one on a save with nothing cleared all take a skip on the first press after `SKIP_LOCKOUT` and show the skip hint (the scene menu's replay skips through its own `TrySkipTour`, above): `TryBeginChapterIntro` reads nothing of the save, `SkipCameraTakeover` asks `_chapterIntro.TrySkip()` alone and the hint is handed `_chapterIntro.Running` alone. A save written while the first opening was held whole carries an `opening` lesson that nothing reads. `ChapterIntroSkipTests` holds the lockout at 30 to 144 Hz with and without a prologue, and holds the screen's source to those three rules.
- **A click skips on any fresh press in the picture** (`TakeoverSkip.MouseSkips`, `TakeoverSkipTests`), the one that takes a free cursor included: with the pointer free (a pad or keyboard arrival with the mouse parked outside the window) the first click in the picture takes the cursor and skips at once. Three presses do not skip: the click that brought the window forward (`EdgeInputAllowed` is false that frame), a button already down (the press that started the level is in the snapshot `CoveredChanged` takes), and a free pointer's click over the title bar or another window. The skip needs no aim, so it does not wait for `MouseAim.Initialized` as the shot does.
- **`skiptakeover=<t1,...>`** presses the skip on the wall clock through `SkipCameraTakeover`, outside the focus gate: with `nofocuspause` and a fresh `userdata=`, a run launched without focus shows the first opening's `[intro]` line followed by `[skip] chapter intro`.
- **The HUD is not over the tour (#693).** `ChapterIntro.HudOpacity` is 0 for the tour and prologue, then `1 - Blend`, whole on the frame `Engaged` lets the gun answer; a skip out of a prologue brings it back at once. The fade goes through the overlay layer (#438, [defocus](level-end-defocus.md)) via `BS3DGame.FadeOverlayLayer` (premultiplied, so one white tint fades the readout as a sheet); under a page's blur the blur's composite takes the layer. **Only the intro is gated**: the drop cinematic and line-loss takeover keep their HUD (#639), so the gate reads the intro's own opacity, not `CameraTakeoverEngaged`.

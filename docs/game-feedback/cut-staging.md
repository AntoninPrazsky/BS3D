# A cut is made

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md).

Using the Cut is staged as an event (#767): the press, the flight, the severing and the fall, each seen, heard and felt, and every beat saying what the cut did. A cut through a storey with nothing hanging on it is still an event (the severing alone); a bigger result adds beats on top. The rules of the Cut itself (the storey, the lower half, the preview) are in [Power-ups](../game-session/powerups.md#cut-the-cutter-round).

The pieces: `Game/Effects/CutStaging.cs` holds the session's state of the beats, `CutBeats` the curves as pure functions of each beat's age, `CutRumble` what the pad is asked, `Blasts`' second style the strike's picture, `CutSynth` the two sounds. The design came from references drawn with both models (`C:\Users\panrd\AI\sd\out\767-zimage`, `767-klein`, prompts in `prompts-767-cut-staging.json`): a long white-hot blade line through the cluster, a grinder's fan of sparks, the line cooling into amber fragments, and the falling part's cut face glowing white-hot.

## The beats

| Beat | Clock | Picture | Sound | Hand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Press** (R) | wall | The saw spins up from rest to `CUTTER_SPIN_FLYING` over `CHARGE_SECONDS` (0.42 s) and eases back to the loaded idle over `CHARGE_SETTLE_SECONDS`; the round's light surges to `1 + CHARGE_GLOW` (1.6) of its accent; the collar flares (`CUT_CHARGE_COLLAR_FLARE`), the rays flare and reach further; the Cut chip flares white-hot into its spent dim | The **charge**, from the muzzle | A hold rising under the charge, mostly the triggers (0.45), a little of the heavy motor (0.22), then a click on both triggers (0.7) at the top |
| **Flight** | wall | The lens pinches by `PINCH` (5 %, in 0.04 s, out 0.38 s); what the round will cut off stays lit while it flies | The shot's own: the flight is a few hundredths of a second, so the slice's zing is the blade arriving | The shot's own |
| **Severing** | world (hit-stop: wall) | `Blasts.Cut`: a white-hot flash, the **slash**, a grinder's sparks, a white-hot light on the balls round it; the camera heaves (`CUT_RUMBLE` + `CUT_RUMBLE_GROWTH` × size); a **hit-stop**, the world at `HIT_STOP_SCALE` (0.15) for 0.07 s; the award waits 0.7 s (`PlayHud.CUT_AWARD_DELAY`), so it does not stand over the slash | The **slice**, from the struck point, in place of the landing's note | One knock, raised **to** 0.45 heavy / 0.65 light (never added onto the shot's thump), 0.8 on both triggers |
| **Fall** | world (slow motion: wall) | The freed part's cut face glows white-hot and cools as it falls (`FALL_GLOW` × `HeatAtDepth`, the storey cut at full, a storey down under half); the storey left above the cut glows too (`FACE_GLOW`); slow motion from 0.6 to 0.35 of the world's speed, held 0.25 to 0.75 s by size, back over 0.3 s | The release's pops for the count | The release's roll-off along its own curve under its ceiling (#810), for every ball the cut let go, the storey included |

- **One size for every beat**: `CutBeats.Size` is the release rumble's logarithm (`LandingRumble.Size`), so the pad, the slow motion and the effect grow along one curve and cannot drift apart. The slow motion grows with what hung on the storey alone (orphaned); the effect, the sound and the hand with everything freed.
- **A cut with nothing hanging on its storey** gets the hit-stop and comes straight back up: no fall to slow. **A cut that engages the drop cinematic** gets no slow motion of its own: the cinematic's runs exactly as it always has. **Never two slow motions at once**: `WorldTimeScale` is the cinematic's while it has the lens and the cut's otherwise, `CutStaging.Strike` is told when the cinematic took the lens, and a cinematic that engages during a cut's slow motion (a shot already in the air landing a big release) ends it (`[skip] cut slow motion, the drop cinematic took over`).

## The slash

The strike's figure is `Blast.fx`'s **blade style** (`BlastShape.z` 1, `.w` the half-length), in the blasts' one buffer and one draw, so the next power-up round's impact is one more style of the same thing.

- **A long thin streak through the struck point, built in the world**: level (square to world up) and square to the lens, so a cut through a storey reads as a horizontal line through the cluster from any bearing. It reaches the storey's own spread either side of the strike plus a ball (at least 2.5 units), shoots out over about a tenth of a second, holds white-hot, thins, cools to amber (the cutter's `CutterEmber`) and burns through into torn lengths over `SLASH_SECONDS` (0.9 s). A static noise along it decides where it parts, never a pattern sliding along it. No ring and no disc (#389's lesson, #640).
- ⚠ **Pulled towards the lens by half its reach** (at most 4 units): pulled only a ball's width, the storey's own balls either side of the strike hid all but a few glints of it (photographed, Elephant). Moving it along the line to the lens keeps the struck point where it is on screen.
- **The sparks are a grinder's**: the blasts' baked directions flattened onto the cut's plane, thrown harder with less drag, white-hot cooling to amber, half again as bright and big as a blast's.
- **Its light** is the blasts' one `SceneLights` slot, white-hot, never under half strength however little the cut freed.
- Its sound and its jolt are not `Blasts.Update`'s: the session plays the slice and the knock on the strike's own landing.

## Rules it keeps

- **Never a rule.** `Activate` still never calls the scorer or feeds the ceiling's cadence; the strike is scored as it always was. The slow motion is the world's alone (the HUD, the lens and the gun run on) and a shot fired during it ends it (`[skip] cut slow motion`) and leaves as it always would, so nothing eats an input. The press holds nothing back: the round can be fired at any moment of it.
- **The same at any frame rate.** Every beat is a closed-form function of its age, and the saw's angle is the closed-form integral of its speed (`CutBeats.SpinOffset`): ⚠ changing the speed inside `clock × speed` makes the blade leap (#766's note). `CutStagingSessionTests` run the session's own `CutStaging` on a real cut of a hung slab, stepped as the game steps it (the wall clock's beats, then the world at the slow motion's scale), at 30, 60, 75 and 144 Hz against a 2 kHz reference: the world time the slow motion costs and the faces' heat agree to a frame's rounding, and a stepping that counts frames instead of seconds is seen to fail the same check. ⚠ **The charge never spins the saw past `CUTTER_SPIN_FLYING`**: faster, the teeth alias at 30 Hz, the strobe #766 had to fix. A pause stops every clock feeding the beats.
- **The ceiling waits for the fall.** A cutter is a shot, so it can queue a ceiling step; released on the step's wall-clock hold, the red alarm, its sound and its rumble played over the fall and the glass slid sooner in the world's time. The step is held through the cut's slow motion as through a takeover (`CeilingDescent.Update`), and comes down after it.
- **The charge is the round's.** Its glow and its spin are drawn on the loaded cutter wherever it sits, so a Swap that moves it back a slot takes them with it while its sound and its hold in the hand go on; ⚠ applied at the muzzle alone, a swapped cutter leapt by the whole spin offset each time it left the muzzle and came back. The rays and the collar are the muzzle's, and flare only while the cutter is there.
- **The preview hands over to the heat.** The balls the preview lit stay lit while the cutter flies (`CutterInFlight`), and at the strike the heat takes over the same balls on the same frame; the face's and the fall's glow ride the preview's channel (`PhysicsBall.CutPreviewGlow`), which the ceiling's alarm wave still outranks.
- **Every tier draws every layer**: one more slot of the blasts' one draw, the light the blasts already use and two glows through the ripple's existing channel, no new pass. The Potato path draws the same through GamePi's `Blast.fx` port; it has no rays, as before.

## Looking at it

- `powerups=cut:1 cut=<t> fire=<t>` with an `aim=` that strikes where wanted: on Teapot, `aim=2:26:0` cuts the bottom storey (16 destroyed, nothing freed), `aim=2:29:0` frees 51 and engages the drop cinematic; on Elephant `aim=2:24:0` frees 56 (`"dropCinematic": false` in the run's `Settings.json` keeps the lens for the cut's own slow motion).
- ⚠ **`shot=` cannot frame a strike**: the flight is a few frames, and a capture at 1920×1080 costs about half a second, which moves every later frame and ends a slow motion before the next frame is drawn. **`cutshot=<s1,...>`** saves the frames at those seconds of the world's clock after a cutter strikes, and prints each one's wall time and the world's speed (`[cutshot]`).
- Each use prints `[cut] press: ...` (the pad's hold and click) and `[cut] strike: ...` (destroyed, orphaned, size, the slash's reach, the knock, the fall's rumble and the slow motion's length).

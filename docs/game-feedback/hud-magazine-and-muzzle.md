# The magazine strip, the chips and the muzzle's marks

Part of [The game: feedback](../game-feedback.md). Sibling of [the in-play HUD](hud.md).

How the player reads "which round fires next" and "is it special": a 2D strip in the corner and 3D marks on the gun.

## The magazine strip (bottom right)

- **Why:** from the play pose the barrel hides the muzzle end, so the next round shows as a small ellipse and the rest through glass.
- Flat discs **in FIRING order, head leftmost and largest, ringed**. **The HEAD's origin is solved, for a full magazine ending flush with the margin**, so it does not slide as the row shortens.
- **`HUD_MAG_GAP` is the clear space between drawn edges** (halo to halo): measured between fills, each halo ate `HUD_MAG_RIM` and the discs read as touching blobs.
- Colours via `TypeColor`: **no second palette**. A dark halo behind each disc and a light outline inside, or the 8-ball is a hole.
- **A plain round's disc carries its colour's figure** (#737), the one the ball carries in the bore (`BallRenderSet.ColourCueOf`, so the style's own set): the fill in the figure's ground ink and the figure in its own ink, both baked the way `TypeColor` bakes a colour, the figure over the fill and under the outline. The figure is the ball seen face-on along its frame's Z, its frame's Y up (an emblem stands upright, a band lies level), baked per figure and radius by `ColourCueField.BakeDisc` (the shader's field ported line for line, the shader's smoothstep edge) and cached; `Reset` bakes the level's set at both radii so no colour's first arrival in the strip bakes mid-play; after a window is resized mid-level, each colour bakes once more at the new radius on its first arrival, mid-play. A swap's fading disc cross-fades its two figures. No figure on a special round (the wildcard, the Cutter: that kind first), with `colourcue=none`, or on the Potato path, whose balls carry none (`BallRenderSet.DrawsColourCue`).
- **A wildcard's disc cycles with the ball it stands for** (#330): every tint off the magazine (strip, muzzle mark, aim beam) goes through `GameplayScreen.LoadedColour`.
- **A Cutter's disc is the round itself** (#766): `DrawCutterDisc` puts a baked face-on saw in place of the fill, a dark steel ball with a ring of hooked teeth glowing in `BasicEffectParamsProvider.CutterAccent` (white-hot at the tips, cooling to amber) round a washer with three bolts, the ball's own face in the bore drawn flat. Never the colour dealt into its slot, which nothing shows: the beam, the collar and the smear take the accent from `GameplayScreen.LoadedTint`, and its ring on the cluster profile in flight and its award's number take it too (`BallMarker.Cutter`, `BallLanding.Kind`).
- **Never more discs than shots left** (`ScoreKeeper.ShotsRemaining`), and the barrel agrees (#262): `CollectMagazineBalls` takes the same `Math.Min(Magazine.SIZE, remaining)` (see `docs/game-session.md`).
- **Discs are antialiased**: `DrawDisc` draws each disc and ring as ONE quad from a cached coverage texture. ⚠ The old scanline version used `MathF.Round` (.5 to even), drawing half the rows twice and the rest never (a combed strip that reads as a shader fault): floor(x + 0.5).

## The power-up chips

- **Swap** (`Ｅ`, pad `⇐` X) above the strip, **Brake** (`Ｑ`, pad `⇑` Y) one row above, **Cut** (`Ｒ`, pad RB) above that. **Three states, by brightness and words, not colour** (#844; amber means gain): **ready**, keycap and word in white, the count if more than one; **not now**, a charge kept that a press would not spend yet, keycap dimmed and the words in full saying why ("Brake · wait" while the glass moves, "Brake · not needed" with nothing to lift, #692/#843; "Cut · not now" with a special round in the bore or no shot left); **used up**, the whole chip dimmed and "Swap used". The owner could not tell "used" from "not now" when both were the same dimmed chip. Each only on a level that offers it (Swap from chapter three, Brake from four; "The tools' cards" in `docs/game-session.md`).
- **The Swap points at the cure** (#845): while a streak above ×1 runs, the player has a Swap and the round in the bore has **nowhere to score** (the owner's rule of 2026-10-11), and the round behind it, which the Swap would load, has somewhere (a conservative reading added to his rule, not his: a swap onto a second dead round is no cure), the Swap chip **flashes** in the streak's amber (a halo behind the keycap and the words, both tinted towards it: a flare at the onset, then a breath every `SWAP_HINT_PERIOD` from `SWAP_HINT_FLOOR` to full) and says **"Swap · keep ×3"** in place of its count. Amber is the HUD's colour for gain, and keeping a streak is one. The rule is `StreakCues.SwapIsTheCure`; "nowhere to score" is `MatchReach`'s certain answer, asked once per colour per state of the field (`BallsMap.Revision`), so a frame costs two array reads. Seen on Chest (`level=52 seed=14 streak=3`): its white lines a sealed hold, so a white round in the bore has nowhere to score until the box is opened.
- **The Cut's press flares its chip** (#767): `PlayHud.PulseCutChip` lights the chip's halo in the cutter's white-hot (not the amber of gain) as its charge goes into the round, falling off at the chip pulse's own rate into the spent chip's dim.
- **Own fonts** (#703): `HUD_FONT_CHIP_PROMPT`/`HUD_FONT_CHIP_DETAIL` are the card pair times 0.8 (a hint up all level at card size was far too huge); `HUD_SWAP_ABOVE_STRIP` = `HUD_MARGIN`.
- **Each chip has its pictogram** (#991, `PowerupPictograms`), left of the keycap's column, centred on its ink and as tall: the **Swap** two balls trading places round a pair of arrows, the **Brake** the glass plate a chevron lifts off the balls under it, the **Cut** the Cutter's own saw blade. Told apart by shape, not colour (all white, in the chip's own state and halo), drawn as a signed distance at the pixel size it is shown at and baked whenever the HUD's fonts are (a resize), so it is crisp at any resolution and one drawing serves any size. ⚠ The Swap's arrows drawn through its balls ran the three into one ring with a hole in it at chip size. `PowerupPictogramTests` holds that each covers 15 to 60 % of its square, stays inside it, and no two overlap past 0.55 of their union at 40 and 64 pixels.
- **Keycaps stand in one column** (#694): `ChipColumnLayout` sizes it by the widest glyph either device draws, captions start at one left edge, measured once per font pair so it never moves between "Swap" and "Swap used" or keyboard and pad. `ChipColumnLayoutTests`.

## The muzzle round's mark: a collar on the gun

- **A ripple failed** (#175): it adds white light, washing out the colour to be read (a same-hue flare was invisible, and the negative branch is one `float3` per draw, so it cannot carry a slot's hue). **A camera-facing billboard failed** (#425): depth-read, it showed whatever sliver the barrel did not occlude, never the same shape twice (`BallGlow` is deleted).
- **Now `CannonRig.DrawMuzzleCollar`**: a lathed steel band ringing the tube just behind the muzzle face, glowing in the round's colour, opaque, turning with the gun. **Around the tube, not on the face**: the play camera sees the gun from behind.
- Rate `MUZZLE_MARK_HZ`, its own: on no harmonic of the cluster's heartbeat or the ghost's beat (`GhostBeat.HZ`), since a collar in the loaded colour pulsing in step with the ghost would read as one locked pair. Gated by `_previewBeamVisible` ("would a shot leave the barrel this instant"). Its breath was set by argument: captures scheduled against a 1.6 Hz sine measure the sampler, so quote no screen figure without a frame-accurate capture path.
- **`CannonRig.CollarHue`** (#478): ball tints keep a floor in their other channels, so the ring was pastel; the normalised hue is pushed twice as far from its own grey (`COLLAR_SATURATION`) and renormalised (a per-channel power turns yellow orange). A bright saturated colour clips every channel and goes **white**. The push comes from the sky (`SetCollarSky`, fed by `ApplySkyLighting`, `COLLAR_BRIGHTNESS_DIM` to `COLLAR_BRIGHTNESS_BRIGHT`): **the sky decides how loud, never what colour**.
- **Precise aim (#321):** leaned in, nothing rejects the mark, so its strength falls with `_preciseAim.Blend` to `MUZZLE_GLOW_ADS_STRENGTH` (a floor: the only 3D statement of the next colour) and its breath is damped out. The overview is untouched: `PreciseAim.Step` snaps `Blend` to exactly 0 on release.

## A special round turns rays round the muzzle (#820)

- While the bore holds a wildcard or the Cutter and `_previewBeamVisible`, `Effects.MuzzleRays` draws one quad **bolted to the barrel**, square to the bore at `CannonRig.MuzzleFaceZ`: nothing inside `CannonRig.CollarRadius`, a corona, two sets of rays turning opposite ways (seven broad, thirteen fine, hashed lengths, #640), added light, depth-read.
- **Wildcard: a rainbow** (any colour). **Cutter: its accent**, white-hot (`BasicEffectParamsProvider.CutterAccent`, #766), pushed as the collar pushes it (`CannonRig.CollarHue`: the burst takes linear light, and the raw sRGB tint read paler than the collar beside it); its collar, beam and smear take the accent too (a red collar under the rays read as a red ball). Over-driven or whitened roots went white; keep `MUZZLE_RAYS_RADIANCE` unwhitened.
- **The Cut's charge flares them** (#767), with the collar under them: the burst's light by `CUT_CHARGE_RAYS_FLARE` and its reach by `CUT_CHARGE_RAYS_REACH`, the collar's by `CUT_CHARGE_COLLAR_FLARE`, all times the charge, so they surge with the round and settle back over its second.
- Leaned in: strength 25 %, reach 1.35 with `_preciseAim.Blend`. **The strip says it too**: `PlayHud.DrawRoundRays` bakes a burst (`RayBurstTexture`) behind that round's disc.
- Cost about 0.015 ms (3840×1600, `High`, Pennant, `wildcard=1`). **Not on the Potato path** (no `MuzzleRays` in GamePi).

## The loaded balls do not breathe (#252)

Balls in the barrel take no ripple and no heartbeat: they glow steadily, and the pulsing is the gun's, in one place. The Cutter's only motion there is the whole round turning slowly as one saw (#766), never a pattern crawling over it. The pulse uniforms are per renderer, so it is a different DRAW: `BallRenderSet` keeps **two planes** of buckets, `BallDrawFrame.Add`'s `still` flag routes into the second, and **each pass states its own `PulseDepth`** (never inherits). **Released balls take the still plane too** (#473); the shot in flight still breathes. The Testbed still breathes its magazine.

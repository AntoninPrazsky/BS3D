# Crates: a wall to bank a shot off

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

**A crate is an upright box in a level's play space that a shot banks off like a mirror** (#257): a bounce off a ball is a curve nobody can read, a bounce off a flat face is one anybody can, so a level can put the gap the player needs round a corner. The first level with crates is Trapeze.

- **The bounce is solved by `Prazsky.BS3D.Physics.Crates`, not by Bepu** (Bepu has no restitution, and the preview must draw a bank shot before it is fired): a lossless **reflection** (`Crates.TryFindFirstFace` against the box grown by the ball's radius, `Crates.Reflect`) that both sides call. The simulation's half, `Crates.BounceShots` (from `PhysicsWorld.PerStepForces`, after the wells), puts the ball where the flight ends **less one step of its new velocity**, so the integrator's `p += v*dt` lands it exactly. The narrow phase never pairs a shot in flight with a crate (`ContactEvents.MarkCrate`); released balls and spent shots meet it as the static box it also is (`Crates.AddStatics`). `Crates.AddSpecs` is the one installation the session and LevelGen's probes share.
- **With crates the preview steps the whole flight, gravity included** (`ShotPlacement.TryFindFirstHitCurved`'s `crates` argument), as the simulation does. `CrateTests.ThePreviewAndTheSimulationBankOffACrateIntoTheSameBall` holds the **flight** exactly but the first ball touched only up to a lattice neighbour (a bank shot meets the cluster at a slant and Bepu's pick depends on the pose to the last bit; the exact-ball assertion failed on CI).
- ⚠ **Rules the review found**: a step whose flight reaches a ball before the face is not bounced; a shot slower than `Crates.MIN_BOUNCE_SPEED` is spent as a miss (a lossless bounce never decays, and a shot dropped on a crate's top would hold the level open for ever).
- **Probes treat a crate conservatively**: the arrival probe counts a line that meets a crate as **stopped**, so a crate can only take a landing away from a level's promise; the sag gate banks its shots through the same solver.
- **The level field is `crates`** (`Level.Crates`, `CrateSpec`: `x`, `y`, `z` the centre in world units from the field's floor, `w`, `h`, `d` full sizes), installed by `InstallCrates` once the field is fitted. **Axis-aligned**: the six faces of an upright box are the planes a player reads best. Stand crates out past the field's edge and low enough not to hide the rig from the play camera.
- **Drawn as a wooden crate** (`CrateMesh`) with its frame flush with the bounds the bounce is solved against, so the faces the eye reads are the planes the ball bounces off. Crates cast no sun shadow; the MapEditor neither shows nor places them (it keeps a loaded level's crates through a save).

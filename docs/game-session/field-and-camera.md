# The field, the camera fit and tall levels

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

## The field is fitted to the level

`FitFieldToMap` derives size and depth per level. The field's top hangs at `max(FIELD_TOP_Y, CEILING_DEATH_Y + FIELD_FLOOR_MARGIN + topLevel/sqrt2)`: a deep field is **raised** so a ball in its lowest cell rests on the death line, one descent from loss (pinning the top let a 30-level map lose on the frame it was built). The empty levels an author leaves under a layout are starting clearance, not dead space. The `[field]` line logs top, floor and lowest-ball clearance. The glass's rest height (`CeilingDescent.Reset`), `_clusterCentreY` and the plate's footprint follow; `FitCeilingToMap` rebuilds the plate's mesh per level, so `BuildLevel` re-runs `ApplySkyLighting`.

- **`_clusterWorldOffset` has X and Z.** `BallsMap.Center` lands on the origin only when the top level is a shifted (odd-index) one starting at index 0; the residual is measured through `GetRealCenteredPosition` and folded into the offset the builder and the contact handler take. ⚠ `Center` takes the top level's **midpoint** (#561), so the offset is only the layout's inset; `AttachBallToStructure` must take the offset too, or a top-level landing's ceiling socket anchors at the raw cell (a median 5.04 units off; `SagProbe` lands through the same method).

## The camera and the gun are solved to the field

`FitCannonAndGameCameraToLevel` runs on every level load and aspect change over the shared `GameCameraFit.Solve`. The stand-off is **bisected** for the smallest distance at which the field's eight corners, the top of the glass and a box around the gun fit **both** frustum half-angles; the aim height from bisecting the elevation range. The gun stands `GameCameraFit.CANNON_CAMERA_STANDOFF` in front of the **lens** (the magazine in its slot is a HUD element and keeps its size), held off by three lower bounds (the field's footprint; the drain's mouth, `CANNON_DRAIN_CLEARANCE`, the one that binds on every shipped level; the resting aim inside the clamp). The solves alternate three rounds (a sub-0.1 residual survives to the third), the gun is moved **once** after, and the field's vertical extent enters `Solve` as two arguments (here level 0 sits at `_clusterWorldOffset.Y`; the Testbed's lattice frame is the world frame).

- **The forward walk** (#364): the drain used to put the walk's near end on the rest stance, so W did nothing. `CANNON_ADVANCE_FORWARD` floors the rest radius: the gun rests 2 units out, walking 2 in and 4 out (the owner's ruling: each unit of forward room stands the whole campaign further out and flattens the resting shot; two are worth it, four are not).
- **The lens follows a retreat and not an advance** (`CANNON_ADVANCE_FOLLOW`, #322): the gun backs towards the lens, so otherwise the walk's far end left it nearer than the 11 units the stroke cap was sized for (`CANNON_ADVANCE_STROKE`).
- **The lens trails the A/D turn** (`BEARING_TRAIL_SECONDS`, #366): a first-order chase of the bearing **angle** through `WrapAngle` (a vector lerp cuts the chord and goes the long way round across pi). ADS gets none (`PreciseAim.Blend` interpolates it out); the Game's alone.
- **The overview tilts with the aim** (`AimTilt`, #720; the Game's alone, the #366 route): the look-at turns about the lens with the barrel's elevation, so more of what the gun points at is in the frame (the owner: the whole gun need not be). Its zero is the aim the level opens on (`Cannon.RestElevation`, or the clamp a tall level opened against where lower: `OpeningElevationLimit`, held for the attempt), so the opening frame is the fit's bit for bit and a walk tilts nothing. It reads the hand's elevation (`RestElevation + ElevationAim`), not the pose: the #431 rubber is the clamp refusing a push, a shot fired through it leaves at the clamp (`FireDirection`, #1008), and a lens following it would bob at a held clamp. `GAIN` 0.3 bent by a tanh onto `UP_MAX` 9° and `DOWN_MAX` 5°, chased critically damped on wall time (`FOLLOW_SECONDS` 0.125 s: 90 % of a flick taken up in 0.5 s, a steady sweep trailed by 0.25 s, and it accelerates into a flick instead of jumping with it); the step is the closed form, so every frame rate lands alike. **Bounded by what the frame must keep**: up by the muzzle (`GUN_KEEP_FRACTION`, 12 % of the frame over the bottom edge: the owner's condition that some barrel or body stays in view, the sight glass a bonus), down by the top of what the fit framed (`TOP_KEEP_FRACTION`, 3 % under the top edge, at the plate footprint's four corners, on the glass's underside or `FramedTopY`, whichever is lower; 3 % is about a ball's radius, so a top ball stays whole). **The overview end only**: `PreciseAim.BlendedPose` lerps from the tilted look-at and the chase runs held or not, so pressing or releasing RMB moves nothing by itself and a release lands on a tilt that has followed the barrel all along.
  - **What it gives on the shipped fits** (One, Teapot, Totem at 16:9 and 2.4:1; the vertical binds on all six): 8.2° up at the gun's ceiling, 3.2° at 45°, which puts Teapot's 45° landing 21 % under the top edge instead of 13 %. The muzzle stays at least 27 % of the frame over the bottom edge at every aim (39 % at the ceiling), so the gun keep has not bound. **Down is 0.5° at a level's opening**: the fit stands its framed top at its own margin (4.3 % under the edge), so a low aim leans the frame further only once the glass has descended under that top. A tall level opens on its clamp, so it gets no up-tilt while the clamp holds, and its framed band stays in the frame at every aim. When `LiftTallAimOnceFramed` lifts the clamp the barrel stays put and so does the zero, so the lift moves nothing and a raise past the old clamp tilts as on a short level. ⚠ Read live off `ElevationLimit`, the zero jumped to the rest at the lift and dipped the frame with no hand on the aim (a 20-level cut of Totem: zero 30.7° to 33.8°, the down room's 0.54° taken).
  - **What the up-tilt costs a low horizon** (#171's Outback, and Savanna, on One and Elephant): the horizon sits 76 % down the frame at the opening, 83 % at 45° and 95–96 % at the gun's ceiling, so at a steep aim the plain is a strip under the gun and the far rocks and trees stand on the bottom edge. The scene still reads, and only while the barrel is up.
  - ⚠ `logtilt` prints the tilt a frame (asked, allowed up and down, given; the pitch; where the top row, the muzzle and the beam's end sit in the frame). A `shot=` frame stalls the run ~0.4 s at 1600×900, so keep shots off the instants a press or release is read across, or the stall reads as a jump.
- ⚠ **Re-measure fit figures after any change to the fit** (a table went seven units stale once): read them off `play level=<name>` and the `[camera]` line.

## Tall levels: a column framed from the bottom

`FRAMED_LEVELS` is the tallest slice the fit frames: a field that deep or shallower is framed whole; a deeper one is framed from its floor to `FramedTopY()`, the rest above the frame. `Ten.json` (Column) is the first. Uncapped, the fit stands back and a level three times as deep renders a third the size; capped, depth becomes the level's *height* and so its length. `FramedTopY` measures from the floor, so a resize frames the same window although the glass has descended.

- **The descent also answers the state.** One ball into a band cuts fifty loose, so a shot-count cadence is the wrong delivery. `FeedTallColumn` runs on every landing and queues the descents that bring the underside back **into reach and no further** (#843, the owner's rule: a feed exists so the player can shoot something at all; only the red pressure steps may take the glass very low). `StepsIntoReach` asks `AimReachability`, in the clamp's own arithmetic (rest stance, held horizontal reach, the current clamp), for the fewest steps after which the underside's whole level is a facing shot, measured from `CeilingDescent.QueuedTargetY` (the hang once queued steps land, so a pour is not asked for twice). `CeilingDescent.Feed` queues that, **bounded by the climb**: never more than brings the underside back to where the level hung it, which is what it queued before #843. It only **adds** descents and skips an **empty** map (`GetLowestOccupiedLevel` of a cleared field is the top level and queued 20 descents). Once the clamp has lifted, the whole frame is in reach and later landings owe nothing (steps queued before the lift still come down). The reach is searched no further than the climb still owes (`CeilingDescent.FeedHeightOwed`), so most landings make no reachability walk at all. ⚠ Before #843 the feed did the descending on a tall level: 193 blue steps against 15 red over The Spectrum in the owner's run. The log line: `[ceiling] feeding <n> step(s) into reach: ... (<m> would have taken it back there)`.
- **The aim is limited to the working band the load held** (`Cannon.ElevationLimit`, `SolveElevationLimit`), so a tall level is not cut off the glass in its opening shots and nothing above the frame is shot blind. It is `AimReachability`'s geometry run backwards: the column's underside plus `TALL_AIM_HEADROOM_LEVELS` plus `TALL_AIM_MARGIN`, **capped at the framed top** (`TallAimBandTop`). **Held for the attempt (#842, the owner's ruling):** `HoldTallAimBand` takes the band at the load, the clamp is solved against the field's cells at the load's hang (`_clusterWorldOffset`) from the gun's rest stance (`RestRadius`, `RestTrunnionY`: the island is a dish), and a resize re-angles the same height for the new rest. A ceiling step does not re-solve it, so the descent brings more of the column into the same reach. ⚠ Re-solved per step (#582 to #842) it fell about 2° a step, Totem from 24.9° to 10.5°, while short levels kept 80°.
- **The clamp lifts once the column hangs whole in the frame** (`LiftTallAimOnceFramed`, on each step): when the highest occupied level is at or under `FramedLevelTop` where the glass is taking it, the limit goes to `Cannon.MaxElevation` until the next load (a ratchet; a brake does not re-clamp). It prints `[aimlimit] lifted <before> -> <after> deg: ...`. On a full column that is a descent of `(Levels - FRAMED_LEVELS) / sqrt2`.
- **The Game runs `AimReachability` itself** at every load and resize, against `ElevationLimit` and over the working band the load held (the whole field, within the cluster's reach, once the clamp has lifted); the Testbed's `aimcheck` does not answer for these. ⚠ `AimReachability.Check` takes the world offset (zero in the Testbed): in the lattice frame it read cells up to five units too high and called Column unfinishable.

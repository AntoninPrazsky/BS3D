# Boards and High Scores

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

What the player sees of [the online scores](online-scores.md).

## The result page's plate

- **The plate is this ending's or none (#707, #716)**: `LevelResult.Submitted` says whether this ending went at all, and its visibility is written against the widget's own `Visible` every frame, not toggled against a flag kept beside it (a loss page once stood under a stale "Sending your score…"). On a loss it says "attempt", and **a zero-star row says "not finished"** wherever a board is drawn.
- **It stands from the page's first frame; only its rows wait for the reveal (#734)**: an answer before the stars land says "Your clear was sent." A player with the switch ON and **no score server** (a hand-made build; only a release names one) is told "Online scores are on, but this build has no score server to send to."
- Once the service accepts a clear the game fetches the level's two boards (`GET /v1/boards/{file}`: top five plus the player's row) through the same worker; `OnlineSession.ResultMonthBoard`/`ResultAllTimeBoard` hold them and `ResultGeneration` moves when anything shown changes. THIS MONTH and ALL TIME, **"You #N of M"** in `BS3DGame.BoardYouColor`. **The first three ranks wear a cup** (#725; PromptFont U+1F3C6, `BS3DGame.PlaceColor`, in a column reserved on every row). The plate is **centred between the column and the screen's right edge** (#701; `BOARD_COLUMN_GAP`, `BOARD_MIN_EDGE_MARGIN`, `BOARD_MIN_WIDTH`) with its width **computed** and content held to it. No answer reads "The score server did not answer. This clear is saved and goes out with your next one." A player who has not opted in gets **one** hint line, once a session (`TakeHint`).
- **A board's name is cut to its column, never drawn over its neighbours (#934)**: a Myra label does not clip to its cell, and the name used to be the Fill column between auto-sized rank, mark and score ones, so a long nickname was printed across "not finished" and the score (reproduced at 1280x720, 1920x1080 and 3840x1600 against a loopback stand-in of the service). Every column of `BoardView` has a pixel width of its own, worked out by `Fill` from what the rows hold (`BoardLayout.NameWidth`: the rank and the score as wide as the widest row, the mark only while a row wears it, the name has the rest), and `BoardLayout.Ellipsize` cuts each name to its measure with "…" when the row is written, never per frame; a row that wears no mark lets its name span the mark's column (`RowMeasure`). A nickname is at most 16 characters (`Nickname.MaxLength`, and `SanitizeBoard` cuts any other player's to it), so the widest case is sixteen capital Ws. ⚠ **At 16:9 and narrower a row that wears "not finished" leaves its name only three or four characters** (the cup, rank, mark and score and the four gaps take about 630 of the plate's 780 design units (read off the 1920x1080 capture), and at `BOARD_MIN_WIDTH` nothing is left, so the name is not drawn); the rows above it, the clears, have the mark's column too. Giving those names room is a design choice, not a width (the mark as a tint as High Scores' rows do, a second line, a narrower word).
- **The plate grows on a display wider than 16:9 (#934)**: `BoardLayout.Ceiling` is `BOARD_WIDTH` up to 16:9 (the width beside the column the look was judged at) and past it `BOARD_WIDTH` plus half of every pixel the window is wider than 16:9 of its own height, which is what the strip beside the column gains, so the margins hold and the room goes to the names; it stops at `BoardView.FullWidth`, the width at which sixteen Ws show whole beside the widest mark, rank and score. At 3840x1600 that is 1453 design units against 780. The result page, the picker's plate and `LevelBoardPage` (where each of the two boards takes the same ceiling over its own 820) share the one function. Held by `BoardLayoutTests` (the arithmetic over a stand-in face and, by reading `BoardView`'s source, that no column is left to Fill or Auto and every name goes through `Ellipsize`).

## The picker's board and `LevelBoardPage`

The picker's footer has **"Board: <level>"** beside Back, opening **`LevelBoardPage`** (both boards side by side, ten rows a page, cached a minute); `board=<n>[:<page>]` opens it at boot.

## High Scores (#685)

The main menu's entry after Select Level opens `HighScoresPage`, a chapter a page, one row a level; `highscores[=<chapter>[:all]]` opens it at boot.

- **It stops at the ends (#727)**: no wrap (the picker's pips say where the player is; this page has none, and a press must not jump from the last chapter to the first), the off arrow dims its glyph and keeps its slab, `PageSideways` answers false. It opens on the chapter the player has reached.
- **A row** shows the board's #1 and the player's place and opens that level's boards (`OpenLevelBoard`). **Every level shows its real board, the levels ahead of the player and the star-locked ones too (#898)**: the complete table of every level of every chapter is the page's point, so this page does not keep #266's lock (a top score is a level's shape in numbers), which the level picker still does. "You" is empty where the player has no place; a level nobody has cleared says "No clears yet"; and every row opens its level's full boards, since `OpenLevelBoard` checks only that the boards can be asked, so a row is an entry in the pad's walk whenever `Online.Enabled`. **It ranks levels** (ranking players needs a rule weighing levels with different ceilings).
- **A row's tint says whether the player has cleared the level (#898)**: a level with at least one star (`HighScoresRow.Cleared`) rests in the menu's usual slab and bright type; a level not cleared rests in a **recessed** slab (`MENU_ROW_UNFINISHED`, black at alpha 100: a clear step *below* the plate, where the first version, a step above it, was 5 over the plate and 13 under a cleared row on the meadow and by the model lost over a bright backdrop) and the body grey for its number and name. Measured over eight scenes (meadow, desert, sea, polar, moon, storm, space, mountain), the recess stands 18 to 40 grey levels under the plate beside it and 28 to 42 under a cleared row's slab; the cleared slab itself is only about 3 over the plate behind a bright scene (desert, polar, storm), so on this page the tint is carried by the recess, which does not shrink ("Controls, plates and the tone order" in `docs/game-shell/menu-look.md`). **A skipped level is not cleared** (`LevelFinished` counts it, because it moves the campaign's frontier; the tint says whether the player has a place of their own, and a skip has none). Greyscale only: gold is a cup, magenta is the player, red and cyan have their meanings ("The deliberate colours" in `docs/game-shell/menu-look.md`). The tone is the page's per-entry rest brush (`MenuPage.EntryRestBrushFor`, which the host's `ApplyNavHighlight` asks for every unfocused entry), and the row's `DisabledBackground` carries the same one, so the tint survives the pad's cursor and the opted-out page. A row that cannot be opened keeps the colours the writing pass gives it (`DisabledBackground`, `DisabledTextColor`; Myra's disabled look made the opted-out page unreadable).
- **A row's numbers stand in columns (#806)**: the level's number flush right in a column as wide as the set's longest number, then the name; "#1 Kuba" with the leader's score flush right at the end of its block; "You: 7th of 17" with the player's score flush right at the end of theirs. All in tabular figures ("Numbers" in `docs/game-shell/menu-look.md`). A name or place that would run into its score ends in "…". An unfinished attempt's mark follows the name ("#1 Rudi, not finished"), not the score.
- **The levels' board keys are read off the frame**: `LevelIdentity.Of` reads and hashes a level file, and the page needs the key of every level (142 files when measured, about 100 ms warm, more on a cold disk or the laptop), so `LevelIdentityCache` reads them on a pool thread the first time the boards can be asked (`HighScoresPage.Ask`), once a run. The frame thread only asks `TryGet` (a volatile flag and an array slot, no lock, no allocation). A row whose key is not read yet waits like a row before the boards answer (blank), the status line says "Loading the boards…" until all are in, and `Update` writes the rows again once when the last one lands. A file that will not read is *read* with a null key (its row says "No clears yet" as before). Held by `LevelIdentityCacheTests`.
- **One request per period, not per level**: `GET /v1/boards?period=&player=` (BS3D-API#7) returns every board's #1 and the asker's place (`OnlineScores.RequestSummary`, cached a minute, `SanitizeSummary`; boards match levels by `LevelIdentity`); per-board GETs would be ~130 an opening against Cloudflare's 50 in 10 s. A **404 or 405 is a service older than #7**: the page says it does not offer the overview yet.
- **Opted out, the page still opens**, says what turning online scores on gives and offers a Settings button.

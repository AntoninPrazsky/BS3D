# Recoil, precise aim and the arena as the Game draws it

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

## Recoil and camera shake

- **`Prazsky.Core.Camera.CameraShake`** produces **offsets only** (rotation, lens displacement, set-back, FOV punch), so a kick never accumulates into the pose and at rest every output is exactly zero. A **directional recoil** (thrown back, muzzle rising: what separates recoil from a wobble) plus a **random rattle**; both decay **linearly in time** so the shake truly ends, amplitude is trauma squared, and the rattle's phase runs off accumulated seconds, never a frame counter (a per-frame random shakes faster on a faster machine).
- **A blast in the scene is a RUMBLE, not a kick** (#389): `CameraShake.Rumble` is a third channel (slower and lower than the rattle) because a bomb heaves the whole world and through `Kick` read as the gun firing twice. Its dials are **not** scaled by `CAMERA_SHAKE_SCALE`. Fed from `Blasts.Update` (`BLAST_RUMBLE` x size) on the world's clock, not under the result page.
- **`RecoilCamera` is its own `ICamera` because of roll**: `BasicCamera3D` rebuilds with a fixed world up; `RecoilCamera` keeps the base pose separate and rolls the up vector about the view axis, which is what reads as "the camera was hit". `BaseRoll` is the drop cinematic's tilt, added to the shake's.
- **The near plane follows the lens's distance from the drain (#1193, `RecoilCamera.NearPlaneAt`)**: `NearPlane` (0.05) while the base lens stands within `NEAR_HOLD_DISTANCE` (36) of `DepthSubject` (the island's top over the drain's mouth); past it, it grows with the square of that distance up to `NEAR_PLANE_CEILING` (0.75, reached about 140 out). A lens within `TARGET_HOLD_DISTANCE` (20) of its target keeps 0.05 wherever it is, and past that the plane grows no faster than the square of the target's distance. `EffectiveNearPlane` is the plane the projection was built with. Every play view keeps 0.05 (it stands at most about 32 from the drain, every shipped level at 16:9 to 4:3); the drop cinematic and the other takeovers stand close in by construction; the menu's wide leg, and the result page's flight out onto it, stand 33 to 47 from their target by window shape ([The menu camera](../game-shell/menu-camera.md)) and draw with up to about 0.09.
  - **Why**: the 24-bit depth step at distance z is about z² / (near × 2^24): 0.0024 at the drain's far side from the play view, 0.05 from the meadow's valley shot (172 to 203 from the drain). There the glass and the pit sheath behind it, which part from nothing at the mouth, landed on one depth value, and the owner saw a line-like flicker round the rim and down the inner sides from afar and none close up: dark streaks down the glass and teeth along the gold collar's edge, different in every frame. Held fixed over that shot, a plane of 0.15 cleared the streaks and 0.3 the teeth (a band of about 1.5 and 0.8 of the widest play view's, below), with the shadows untouched, so the depth buffer was the cause; a change to the rule compares against those two.
  - `NearPlaneTests` walks every chapter scene's intro (and Mars's, the polar camp's, the sea's and the outback's) through its real builder and holds: every play pose keeps the plane; from every frame that shows the drain, the band of glass the buffer cannot part from the sheath spans at most a tenth more of the frame than from the widest play view (from the valley about a third of it, from the volcano's village, 500 to 613 off, about nine tenths; at 0.05 they were 4.7 and 13.5 times it); past about 140 the plane stays at the ceiling and the band grows again with the distance, back to the play view's at about 680, farther out than any frame that shows the drain; and every corner of a farther plane keeps a unit of air over the ground, off the cavern's rock and off the city's boxes.
  - ⚠ **The drain's distance decides, the target only holds it down**: the drain is what fights, so a lens far from it looking at something nearer (the village, 150 from the lens) still needs the farther plane. But the plane clips whatever stands nearer the lens than its corners (up to about twice the plane at the widest window and intro lenses), and a lens looking at something close is a close, low shot (the orbit round the meadow's hay bales about a unit over the grass, the beetle on the sand). The cost: the bales' orbit and the savanna's round its acacia keep 0.05 where the far side of the drain's mouth peeks over the island's rim from up to some 215 off, in a few per cent of their frames, and the flicker can still show there. A run that looks along its travel looks at a point 10 ahead (`IntroShot.NEAR_LOOK`) and so keeps 0.05 too, as the aurora's pass between the spruces needs; a run that keeps clear of everything looks far ahead (`IntroShot.FAR_LOOK`) and takes the drain's plane, as the Grid's push through its ring does, about 450 from the drain, where at 10 ahead the drain flickered as from the valley.
  - No pass samples the depth buffer, and the passes that work out a depth of their own (the clouds' far layer, the motion blur's background) take the planes from the projection. The Testbed and the editor draw through `BasicCamera3D`, whose plane stays 0.01.
- **`CAMERA_SHAKE_SCALE` scales the ceiling and `RECOIL_KICK` stays 1**: `Kick` saturates at 1, so a fractional kick would let two quick shots climb back to a too-strong response. The shared `Cannon`'s own stroke (`RECOIL_BACK`, `RECOIL_DECAY`, `CARRIAGE_RECOIL_BACK`) carries the difference; the executables keep the ticks (`KickRecoil`, `StepRecoil`). **All of it is drawing only**: a shot leaves along the true aim before the barrel moves.
- **The camera pose** is `GameCameraFit.CameraPosition`, back along `Cannon.StandBearing` **flattened to the horizontal** (taken straight it tilts down by however far the gun stands below the cluster and eats the camera's height), `CAMERA_HEIGHT` below the trunnions.

## Precise aim (ADS) in the Game

The shared `PreciseAim` (lean, parallax: "Precise aim (ADS) (Testbed)" in `docs/testbed.md`). `PreciseAim.Blend` eases 0 to 1 and `BlendedPose` lerps overview and leaned pose, so at 0 the overview returns bit for bit: the overview as the aim has tilted it (#720, [The field, the camera fit and tall levels](field-and-camera.md)), so the lean interpolates the tilt out.

- **The left trigger is analog (#520)** when Settings' *Precise aim* is *Hold*; toggled (the default since #1054) a press leans all the way in: `LeanAmount` maps the pull over `TRIGGER_REST`..`TRIGGER_FULL`. `TRIGGER_REST` sits a hair above the trigger's zero (MonoGame applies no dead zone, and a worn trigger must not hold the blend, and the defocus riding it, above zero).
- **It returns a pose as a value** because `RecoilCamera` takes a base pose, not `BasicCamera3D`'s ordered setters; the drop cinematic lerps over it and the shake goes on top. **Focus**: `UpdateAim` does not run while inactive (`_adsHeld` is cleared there) but `Step` still runs, so the lean eases out and an unfocused pad trigger cannot hold it; a lean toggled in is kept and comes back with the focus. `PreciseAim.FLOOR_CLEARANCE` floors the lens above the stone under it (`FloorHeightAt`). The lean also defocuses the periphery (#214; "The end of a level goes out of focus" in `docs/game-feedback.md`).
- **`Crosshair`** is drawn **only while ADS leans in** (the only pose whose lens looks along the shot) and not once the level is over, because the lean freezes under the result page (#259). That is the caller's decision: it takes an opacity. The FPS line (`InfoRenderer`) is scaled by `Viewport.Height / 2160`, so `RecomputeScale` must re-run on a resize **and** a fullscreen switch.

## Clouds, occlusion, the island

- **Clouds are the Testbed's weather minus its ambient half**: `_clouds.ApplyTo` on **both** the sky and the instanced effect each frame off the wall clock, so the cloud seen and the shadow it throws are one cloud. `CloudField.ApplyDome` is **not** optional: the two cloud colours have no defaults (unset is a black deck) and it tells both shaders where the sun stands (#220). `SkyLightRig.StepOvercast` is **deliberately left out**: its daylight palette would lighten a night city as the weather thickened.
- **The occlusion direction must be divided by `BallRenderSet.MAX_OCCLUDERS`** (raw it is up to 12x too long and every surface ball wears a hard black crescent). `BallRenderSet.OcclusionTarget` is the only builder and divides inside; the Game once skipped it.
- **The stone ring is a dish** (`ArenaIsland.DISH_DEPTH`, one constant for `IslandMesh` and `FunnelPhysics.Build`): on a flat ring released balls outside the mouth's radius parked, and Bepu spheres have no rolling resistance so a shallow grade drains them. The gun stands on the dish (`CannonRig.TrunnionHeightAt`; no collider: a drawing fact); the overview lens floor (`LENS_FLOOR_Y`), the ADS floor and the gun's ground-darkening anchor all follow it.
- **The island is a washer, the drain fills it** (#48): `ArenaIsland` builds the whole assembly for both executables and `FunnelPhysics.Build` is its collision floor ("The setting, in one copy" in `docs/scenes.md`). ⚠ **Without a `DetailTexture` the renderer falls through to the plain technique and every `SlabSize`/`CavityStrength`/relief setting is silently dead.**

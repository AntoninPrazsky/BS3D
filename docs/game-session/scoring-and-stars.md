# Scoring, stars and progress

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

## The score

The rules live in `Prazsky.BS3D/Scoring/ScoreKeeper.cs`: no MonoGame, Bepu, map or frame, because they are what will be argued about and retuned. It is fed three events and answers what the HUD and result page need.

- **Two kinds of released ball; the difference is the skill.** `ReleaseSameTypeCluster` returns a `BallsReleased`: *matched* (the group the shot completed, `MatchedBallPoints` 10), *orphaned* (what fell because that group was its last anchor, `OrphanedBallPoints` 20, **double**: cutting a support and collapsing half the cluster is the shot worth learning), and *destroyed*.
- **A destroyed ball is a third category** (a blast or a cut; `DestroyedBallPoints`): **the same as a matched ball, not the orphan's double**: the player aimed at these as at a group, and the orphan rate rewards the shot that must be read. ⚠ **What a ball was already doing when the blast reached it decides** (#396): one the walk had already reported as cut loose stays orphaned; only cluster still **standing** is destroyed, so an orphan-triggered blast can only add to a shot's worth. ⚠ `ScoreKeeper.Landed`'s spent-shot door reads **matched OR destroyed** (matched alone called a bomb-only shot a miss). This rate is reasoned, not measured: `Tools/ScoreSim` models no blast.
- **The streak**: a scoring shot is awarded at the multiplier standing when it lands, *then* raises it by `MultiplierStep` (2: x1, x3, x5), capped at `MaxMultiplier` (5); `Multiplier` reads **forward**, so `Landed` returns a `ScoreAward` carrying the multiplier applied. A shot that drops nothing resets it, and the HUD breaks the badge with a falling run under it ("The streak" in [the HUD](../game-feedback/hud.md#the-streak), #845).
- **The three events do not arrive together**: a ball is spent when it leaves the barrel (`Shot()`), what it did takes a step or more (`Landed`), a miss later still (`Missed`). **Every shot resolves exactly once, and `ContactEvents.IsListener` gives that for free**: a shot listens from `Shoot` until it attaches, touches something it cannot attach to, or is culled, and the kill-plane cull reports a miss only for a still-listening ball. **A miss resolves when the ball strikes the island**, not when culled (the player knows at once). The glass is not an ending (#432). The handler reports through `BallLanded`/`ShotSpent` and does not score (the scorer is replaced per level, so methods read the current `_run.Score`).
- **The completion bonus** is unused shots x `UnusedShotValue` = `UnusedShotWorthInShots` (1) x the balls an average shot of *that* level would clear (not needing a shot is worth a shot on every level), awarded at the clear, never on a failed level, and said over the aim point as `N UNUSED BALLS` over the figure ([the HUD](../game-feedback/hud.md#awards-and-the-score)). It was four shots until rules v2 (#1045): at four it added about three floors to almost every clear (the owner's median clear: 3.0 of its 7.9) and carried it over the four-star line by itself; time now charges for the extra shots. `StreakBonus` is the score minus `BaseScore` (the score at x1 throughout).
- **The time bonus** (#1045, the owner's ruling of 2026-11-02, his option B of 2026-11-17): a faster clear scores more. `ScoreKeeper.TimeBonusAt(t)` = `TimeBonusWorthInFloors` (10) x the level's floor x (1 - t / (`TimeBonusZeroAtPars` (2) x par)), never below zero: 100 points a ball at the start, half at the level's par, nothing at twice par. **t is the play clock** (`LevelRun.Seconds`, `PlayClock`), which stands still under every camera takeover, so the camera following falling balls costs nothing, **counted in whole seconds begun**: the bonus steps down once a second, and the figure the HUD shows through a second is exactly what a clear in it is paid; rounding up never pays more than the exact time would. Awarded at the clear (`AwardTimeBonus`, into the base score too, so `StreakBonus` stays the streak's), never on a failed level, and thrown over the aim point as `TIME BONUS` before the unused balls are counted. A level with no par has none. What the player sees: the readout under the score and the award ([the HUD](../game-feedback/hud.md#the-time-bonus)), the result page's row, Help's SCORING tab.
- **The par** is the level set's (`LevelSetEntry.Par`, [Formats](../formats-and-tools/level-formats.md)), written by `Tools/LevelGen` for every level: `ParRule.FromShots` (10 s + 2.5 s a shot, rounded up to 5 s) of the shots LevelGen's casual player takes to clear it, the player the owner's own clears followed (his shots a clear against its: R² 0.81 at a ratio of 1.1, his time about 10 s + 2 s a shot). 20 s to 115 s over the 140 levels, 40 s in the middle. **The decision's rule that a level with five or more clears since v0.6.0 takes their median instead has a hook and no data**: `ParTime.FromPlay` in LevelGen, empty until the service's clears are exported with a version filter.
- ⚠ **`ScoreKeeper.RulesVersion` is bumped in the same commit as any change to a rate or rule** (#549): online boards are keyed by level identity *and* this number, and a forgotten bump ranks scores made under different rules together. It is **2 since #1045** (the time bonus, the unused balls four to one). `ScoreKeeper.ScoreCeiling` (the bound the service refuses a submission over) is re-derived in the same commit: every term at its maximum, the whole time bonus included, and `ScoreCeilingAt` the same at a claimed time with the time bonus that time is paid, which is what the service checks a clear against. Star thresholds are not part of the version. "The scoring simulator" in `docs/formats-and-tools.md`.

## Stars (#111)

`Scoring/StarRating.cs` rates a clear 1-4 against the level's own **floor**: a cleared field released every ball it started with and each scores at least `MatchedBallPoints`, so no clear of an N-ball level scores under `10 x N`: one star is "cleared", and 2/3/4 stars are multiples of the floor (`TWO_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 2.5, `THREE_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 6.5, `FOUR_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 11), which self-calibrates across sizes. The count is `LevelRun.InitialBallCount` (captured at install: the map is empty at rating time). **A loss is not rated.**

- **Four stars are hard on purpose, and time is the lever** (#1045, the owner's option B). The landing part of a clean clear is 3.5 to 5 floors and its unused balls under one, so no clear earns four stars without the time bonus: a clean clear in the casual player's shots earns them at about three quarters of par, three within about one and a half par, two at twice par; the whole budget with a third of it missing (about 1.9) earns one. Under v1 a clear almost always earned four (the owner's save: 38 of 41). The #1045 model (the owner's 23 clean levels as play styles over all 140 levels) rates an expert 0/10/32/59 % at 1/2/3/4 stars and a newcomer 2.2 stars on average, against 0/0/7/93 % under v1.
- ⚠ **Retune the thresholds against `Tools/ScoreSim`, never by intuition.** The reasoned first values rated *backwards* (the rating rose with shots taken on every shipped level). The cause was in the keeper: the streak's ramp amortised over the shot count (hence `MultiplierStep` 2) and a flat completion bonus meant a different thing on every level (hence `UnusedShotValue` sized by the level). A full-budget clear must be the lowest of the play styles, a fast clean clear must reach four stars, and the same clear at twice par must not.
- **The result page says what the next star costs** (#385), and since #1045, when the time bonus alone could pay it, how much faster the same clear would have had to be: "Next star at 13 200 (+3 940), or 27 s faster" (`LevelResult.NextStarSecondsFaster`, asked of `TimeBonusAt` itself).

## Progress, unlocks and the skip

- **`Levels/PlayerProgress.cs`** keeps each entry's best score and stars in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D\Progress.json` (the player's profile since #353: it once sat in the build output where `dotnet clean` took it), keyed by the entry's **file**, written atomically with one `.bak` ("`Progress.json`" in `docs/formats-and-tools/player-files.md`), loaded leniently (a corrupt save must cost stars, never the game). It is written when a clear improves a best (`BS3DGame.RecordLevelResult`, inside `ShowResultScreen`) and *before* the next entry's gate is read, so a clear that crosses a gate unlocks Next Level on the screen announcing it.
- **A best carries the scoring rules it was made under** (#1045, `LevelBest.Rules`; absent in a save written before, which reads as rules 1): a score is compared only with a best of the same rules, so the first clear under new rules replaces an older best whatever the two figures and is a new best, and **the stars are kept**: a player never loses a star to a rule change. The level picker shows only a best of this build's rules (`LevelBestScore`); the level's stars still say it was cleared.
- **The star gates climb a star and a half a level** (LevelGen's `MinStarsAt`: the second entry asks for one star, entry *i* for 1.5 x (*i* - 1) rounded down), since #1045: under rules v2 a newcomer's clear earns 2.2 stars on average, and the two a level of the old ramp would have left 0.2 to spare, so a short run of one-star clears walled the campaign. `Tools/ScoreSim` refuses a steeper gate (GATE TOO STEEP).
- ⚠ **The campaign also opens one level at a time (#347, the owner's ruling)** (`BS3DGame.IsLevelUnlocked`): an entry is playable when the star total reaches its `minStars` **and** it is no further than the first unfinished entry (`FirstUnfinishedLevel`; cleared or skipped counts as finished). The star gate alone is a running sum: players ran ahead and CAMPAIGN COMPLETE fired on an incomplete campaign; it stays underneath because it sends a struggling player back to rate a level better. **A level already cleared stays open whatever the sequence says** (a save with 65 cleared levels and its frontier at 38 would otherwise lose 28 beaten levels). The picker and **the result screen** name which lock applies (`LevelResult.UnlockNote` asks `IsLevelBeyondReach` first).
- **The bounded skip** relieves the sequence rule: a failed level offers **Skip** beside Retry, `BS3DGame.SKIPS_PER_BLOCK` (one) per chapter (per campaign in a set with no chapters), kept in `PlayerProgress.Skipped`. **A skip is not a clear**: no score, no stars; it only lets the frontier past and the level stays playable (its tile says `Skipped`). It may not smuggle the player past the star gate (`CanSkipLevel` refuses when the next entry's `minStars` is unmet). `WouldCompleteCampaign` counts a skip as finished but is asked only from `CheckLevelCleared`, so the confetti never falls on one.

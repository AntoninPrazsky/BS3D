# Scoring, stars and progress

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

## The score

The rules live in `Prazsky.BS3D/Scoring/ScoreKeeper.cs`: no MonoGame, Bepu, map or frame, because they are what will be argued about and retuned. It is fed three events and answers what the HUD and result page need.

- **Two kinds of released ball; the difference is the skill.** `ReleaseSameTypeCluster` returns a `BallsReleased`: *matched* (the group the shot completed, `MatchedBallPoints` 10), *orphaned* (what fell because that group was its last anchor, `OrphanedBallPoints` 20, **double**: cutting a support and collapsing half the cluster is the shot worth learning), and *destroyed*.
- **A destroyed ball is a third category** (a blast or a cut; `DestroyedBallPoints`): **the same as a matched ball, not the orphan's double**: the player aimed at these as at a group, and the orphan rate rewards the shot that must be read. ⚠ **What a ball was already doing when the blast reached it decides** (#396): one the walk had already reported as cut loose stays orphaned; only cluster still **standing** is destroyed, so an orphan-triggered blast can only add to a shot's worth. ⚠ `ScoreKeeper.Landed`'s spent-shot door reads **matched OR destroyed** (matched alone called a bomb-only shot a miss). This rate is reasoned, not measured: `Tools/ScoreSim` models no blast.
- **The streak**: a scoring shot is awarded at the multiplier standing when it lands, *then* raises it by `MultiplierStep` (2: x1, x3, x5), capped at `MaxMultiplier` (5); `Multiplier` reads **forward**, so `Landed` returns a `ScoreAward` carrying the multiplier applied. A shot that drops nothing resets it, and the HUD breaks the badge with a falling run under it ("The streak" in [the HUD](../game-feedback/hud.md#the-streak), #845).
- **The three events do not arrive together**: a ball is spent when it leaves the barrel (`Shot()`), what it did takes a step or more (`Landed`), a miss later still (`Missed`). **Every shot resolves exactly once, and `ContactEvents.IsListener` gives that for free**: a shot listens from `Shoot` until it attaches, touches something it cannot attach to, or is culled, and the kill-plane cull reports a miss only for a still-listening ball. **A miss resolves when the ball strikes the island**, not when culled (the player knows at once). The glass is not an ending (#432). The handler reports through `BallLanded`/`ShotSpent` and does not score (the scorer is replaced per level, so methods read the current `_run.Score`).
- **The completion bonus** is unused shots x `UnusedShotValue` = `UnusedShotWorthInShots` (4) x the balls an average shot of *that* level would clear (so not needing a shot is worth several shots on every level), awarded at the clear, never on a failed level. `StreakBonus` is the score minus `BaseScore` (the score at x1 throughout).
- ⚠ **`ScoreKeeper.RulesVersion` is bumped in the same commit as any change to a rate or rule** (#549): online boards are keyed by level identity *and* this number, and a forgotten bump ranks scores made under different rules together. `ScoreKeeper.ScoreCeiling` (the bound the service refuses a submission over) is re-derived in the same commit. Star thresholds are not part of the version. "The scoring simulator" in `docs/formats-and-tools.md`.

## Stars (#111)

`Scoring/StarRating.cs` rates a clear 1-4 against the level's own **floor**: a cleared field released every ball it started with and each scores at least `MatchedBallPoints`, so no clear of an N-ball level scores under `10 x N`: one star is "cleared", and 2/3/4 stars are multiples of the floor (`TWO_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 1.8, `THREE_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 4.0, `FOUR_STAR_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` 6.0), which self-calibrates across sizes. The count is `LevelRun.InitialBallCount` (captured at install: the map is empty at rating time). **A loss is not rated.**

⚠ **Retune the thresholds against `Tools/ScoreSim`, never by intuition.** The reasoned first values rated *backwards* (the rating rose with shots taken on every shipped level). The cause was in the keeper: the streak's ramp amortised over the shot count (hence `MultiplierStep` 2) and a flat completion bonus meant a different thing on every level (hence `UnusedShotWorthInShots`). A full-budget clear must be the lowest of the play styles.

## Progress, unlocks and the skip

- **`Levels/PlayerProgress.cs`** keeps each entry's best score and stars in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BS3D\Progress.json` (the player's profile since #353: it once sat in the build output where `dotnet clean` took it), keyed by the entry's **file**, written atomically with one `.bak` ("`Progress.json`" in `docs/formats-and-tools/player-files.md`), loaded leniently (a corrupt save must cost stars, never the game). It is written when a clear improves a best (`BS3DGame.RecordLevelResult`, inside `ShowResultScreen`) and *before* the next entry's gate is read, so a clear that crosses a gate unlocks Next Level on the screen announcing it.
- ⚠ **The campaign also opens one level at a time (#347, the owner's ruling)** (`BS3DGame.IsLevelUnlocked`): an entry is playable when the star total reaches its `minStars` **and** it is no further than the first unfinished entry (`FirstUnfinishedLevel`; cleared or skipped counts as finished). The star gate alone is a running sum: players ran ahead and CAMPAIGN COMPLETE fired on an incomplete campaign; it stays underneath because it sends a struggling player back to rate a level better. **A level already cleared stays open whatever the sequence says** (a save with 65 cleared levels and its frontier at 38 would otherwise lose 28 beaten levels). The picker and **the result screen** name which lock applies (`LevelResult.UnlockNote` asks `IsLevelBeyondReach` first).
- **The bounded skip** relieves the sequence rule: a failed level offers **Skip** beside Retry, `BS3DGame.SKIPS_PER_BLOCK` (one) per chapter (per campaign in a set with no chapters), kept in `PlayerProgress.Skipped`. **A skip is not a clear**: no score, no stars; it only lets the frontier past and the level stays playable (its tile says `Skipped`). It may not smuggle the player past the star gate (`CanSkipLevel` refuses when the next entry's `minStars` is unmet). `WouldCompleteCampaign` counts a skip as finished but is asked only from `CheckLevelCleared`, so the confetti never falls on one.

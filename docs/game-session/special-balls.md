# Special balls: the kinds a level places

Part of [The game: a session](../game-session.md).

`BallType` is the thirteen colours; **`BallKind` is what a ball *is* beside its colour**, one optional key per map cell ("Levels and serialization" in `docs/formats-and-tools.md`). Each kind is taught on its first level (#735; "A card for every kind" in `docs/game-feedback.md`). Kinds the player shoots and the physics kinds: [Special balls: shot and mass](special-balls-shot-and-mass.md).

⚠ **Never add a fourteenth `BallType` for a kind**: render buckets are sized by `BallTypes.Count`, and the census, `RandomBallType`, `Transmute` and the generator (thirteen inks) would all take it.

**Two predicates carry every kind.** `BallKinds.Matchable`: the match rule reads the kind in one place, `BallsMap.GetConnectedSameTypeCells`, which skips an unmatchable ball (it stops the flood fill like an empty cell); it also gates the magazine census; an unmatchable kind carries a `BallType` **nothing may read**. `BallKinds.Removable`: can the player ever be rid of it? Both end-of-level conditions ask it. Glass is *not* matchable and *is* removable; merging the two would leave a level with one glass ball unfinished for ever.

**What ships**: rock (Anvil, Cairn, Keystone, Obsidian, Seam), glass (Diadem, Facet, Harlequin, Solitaire, Trefoil: the Mirage), bomb (Vent, Sill, Paroxysm), zap (Caldera, Fume, Paroxysm), buckshot (Sandbags, Juggler). **Acid, Frozen, Infectious, Gravity and Heavy ship in no level**; each has a test field in `Testbed/Maps/` and `levelfile=<path>` plays any level file.

## Rock and transparent

- **Rock (#324)** matches nothing and leaves only as collateral when a match cuts its last anchor. **Stone stops one course short of the glass** (#343, the owner's ruling): the top level is the only one bonded to the plate and that socket is never cut, so a rock there stays however well the level is played. What is left at the end is the stone, so cutting it loose for no score is a finish, not a stall.
- **Transparent (#325)** takes the **shot's** colour when a shot lands beside it, then is ordinary (it matches on the very shot that coloured it: `ProcessContact` colours between attach and group check). The landing decides the colour (`BallsMap.ColourTransparentGroup`). ⚠ **The colour runs through the whole connected body of glass** (#344: a lone recoloured ball is useless). **The score does not reward the colouring** (owner's ruling): it pays when it releases something; one that completes nothing is a shot that stuck and takes the streak. **Glass goes on the skin**: never buried (with no empty neighbour it can never be coloured) and never on the anchor course (an anchor that dissolves unannounced); both Mirage laws are gated in `Tools/LevelGen` ("The two ball kinds in a design" in `docs/formats-and-tools.md`).

## Bomb, zap, acid: destruction, not release

A blast's victims are never a group: **choose cells by geometry, remove them, run the disconnection pass** (`DetonateBombs`, `ResolveDisconnected`). All three are not matchable, removable, and fire when a shot lands **beside** them (a *place* is hit, so every bomb beside the cell goes: choosing one would be invisible dice).

- **Bomb (#326, #396)** destroys every ball within `BallsConstraintsBuilder.BLAST_RADIUS`, itself included. **Triggers**: a landing beside it, another blast, and **losing its last path to the ceiling**. ⚠ **A bomb a release orphans goes off**: the disconnection walk is a flood fill and finds it before the ball has moved; there is no "mid-air". `ResolveDisconnected` is the one pass all removals end in and it loops (a blast can cut a support). The radius is a lattice walk in the **grid frame**, so the cells taken do not move with the swing. Victims are **thrown outward from the body in world space** (#389: the grid-frame offset was ~12 units against a 2-unit radius, so every victim drifted one way).
- **Zap (#327)** takes **every ordinary ball of the shot's colour off the field**: the SHOT's colour is the only one the player chose (visible three balls ahead: a special, not a lottery). ⚠ It takes only `Matchable` balls. **It fires before the blast** when one landing arms both (and `SagProbe` repeats it), or the blast eats the zap ball and swallows an armed effect. Zapping the last live colour is `AnyBallTypeAlive`'s guard.
- **Order inside a landing: colour the glass, complete the group, detonate.** Bombs are collected **before** the release so the detonation re-checks each cell against the changed map; the shot's own match runs first (a blast eating a group before it was counted reads as the game refusing a good shot).
- **Acid (#328)** drills a shaft **downward** (`BallsMap.CollectAcidShaft`) to the first gap. ⚠ **A vertical line through a ball passes *between* the four balls under it** (all 0.707 off its axis), so a cylinder cannot make the shaft: it is walked cell by cell, each step taking the occupied cell below nearest the acid's axis, bounded by `ACID_SHAFT_DRIFT` (a fixed-index `level--` walk does *not* lean: parity alternates). It runs **after the zap, before the blast**.
- ⚠ **`Tools/LevelGen/SagProbe` must learn every landing step in the same change**, or it measures a game nobody plays.

## Frozen, infectious, buckshot

- **Frozen (#329)** is a coloured ball in ice that matches nothing until it **thaws when the player clears a group next to it**, keeping the colour it wore: the first state that changes over a level. **The trigger is the matched group alone** (a region that merely fell breaks no ice). ⚠ **The rule lives inside `ReleaseSameTypeCluster`**, so Testbed, Game and sag probe thaw with no line added. It breaks **one ring**; destroyers destroy ice and never thaw it; `LevelGen` refuses a block with no **matchable neighbour**.
- **Infectious (#331)** is a **sick** ordinary ball and the only rule with a TICK: each time a shot **resolves** (never when it is fired: a shot flies for about a tenth of a second, and a tick at the muzzle could turn the ball being aimed at into stone after the player committed; a kill-plane miss resolves through the same `OnShotSpent`) it infects the healthy neighbour **nearest the ceiling** and **hardens into a rock**. ⚠ **It is the first kind besides `Normal` that is `Matchable`**: an infection the player cannot shoot out is a timer, not a mechanic (and the generator's repair, whose `PutBallAt` defaults to `Normal`, would silently cure it). It **moves rather than multiplies**, so the sick count only stays level or falls. ⚠ **The tick runs after the release and before the census**, or loadable colours, cleared and lost are one shot stale. A **miss ticks too**, on the shot **resolving**, not at the muzzle. `LevelGen` refuses a sick ball on the anchor course.
- **Buckshot (#257)** is a clump nothing matches (`Matchable` no, `Removable` yes) that the player clears by **cutting what holds it up**. ⚠ **A clump that can never come down makes the level only losable**: `BallsMap.GetUncuttableFromCeiling` floods from the top through rocks and buckshot and `LevelGates` refuses any clump it reaches; **a level with an infection is refused outright** (its rock trail can wall a clump mid-level). A released clump is drawn **loosened** so it falls as a spray (`BallRenderSet.StoreBuckshot`).

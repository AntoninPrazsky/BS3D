# The level picker and the result page

Part of [The game: shell](../game-shell.md).

## The level picker (`LevelSelectPage`)

Select Level opens a grid of `MenuTile`s (`MenuButton`'s behaviour in tile shape over `MenuClickable`), **one chapter to a page, paged left and right** (#273). A tile carries the number, the name, a star line and the player's own best score where rated (#684).

- **Locks**: a locked tile is `Button.Enabled = false` (Myra presses no disabled button and the nav walk skips it) and dimmed to the aside grey. It names its reason (#347): the price ("8 ★") or the sequence ("#38 first"), and must name the right one; a **skipped** level says `Skipped` (the only place it is found again); a lock shows no score (#266: no hint of a level ahead; **High Scores is the one page that shows a level ahead's real board**, #898, "High Scores" in `docs/game-session/online-boards-ui.md`).
- ★/☆ are Inter's (`FontTile`, `MenuFontStars`): Anton has neither and FontStashSharp silently draws blanks.
- **The backdrop turns to the level looked at** (#405): the tile under the pointer or cursor asks `BackdropScreen.RequestPreview` to hang that level in **its own scene, dome, weather and material** (reversing #249 for this page only). It **settles** (`PREVIEW_SETTLE_SECONDS`), **does not restart the flight** (on the close pass the map waits for room, #408), and **locked tiles ask nothing**.
- **The level's online boards stand right of the band** (#684): month and all time, top five and the player's place, for the level the Leaderboard button opens. The plate holds its height in every state. ⚠ **The band moves left to make room**: it is *measured* (`Widget.Measure`; its widest row is the thirteen pips), the plate is centred in the strip right of it (#701's rule), the band shifts through its right margin so the three gaps match, the board narrows, and under `BOARD_MIN_WIDTH` is absent. Its width is `BOARD_WIDTH` up to 16:9 and grows on a wider display (`BoardLayout.Ceiling`, "The result page's plate" in [Boards and High Scores](../game-session/online-boards-ui.md)), so a name is cut with "…" only where the board really has no room. A board is keyed by the level file's hash; a clear calls `OnlineSession.ForgetBoards` (a cached page would put pre-clear rows under the post-clear rank).
- **The pager**: the chapter's name is the heading between two wrapping arrow slabs, with a readout in a **fixed-width block** so the arrows do not jump (data from `LevelSet`'s `Block*` members). **The pips** (a disc cleared, a ring not, the shown one bright; drawn by `PipGlyph`, #847) are each a `MenuTile` that jumps to its chapter (#348). ⚠ **A Myra button smaller than its content lets it overflow instead of clipping**, so the visible plate and the mouse's rectangle part ways (a click on the ring read as a dead control): a pip's plate is sized to hold its mark.
- **It opens on the frontier** (the *last* chapter holding an unlocked level, not "the first with an uncleared one": a skipped level would send the player back to chapter 1) **and stays where they left it**; an **unchaptered set** is one scrolling grid (`CampaignHasBlocks`).
- **One detail line under the grid** holds what does not fit a tile (the rules, a lock's arithmetic), fed by the pointer (Myra's `MouseEntered`, which fires on disabled tiles) or the cursor (`MenuPage.NavFocusChanged`). It is one line of fixed width and height, cut with "…" where it will not fit.
- **The page's lines that are built from numbers and names are `PickerCopy`'s** (#806): the chapter's readout ("Chapter 2 of 14 · 3 of 10 cleared · ★ 4 of 40"), the star total, a locked tile's line, the detail line (a quoted name takes ‘ ’), the boards plate's heading and the Leaderboard button, so `PickerCopyTests` holds them to the typesetting rules over every shipped name. The boards' own lines are there too (`YouLine`, High Scores' `Leader` and `Place`). The fixed words ("LEVEL", "THIS MONTH", the plate's status lines) are literals in the pages, held by review.
- ⚠ **It is the one page pulled DOWN into a band and it takes the camera's framing** (#472): tiles are smaller, the plate bottom-aligned. `BackdropScreen.FramingLift` lowers the wide leg's aim (a fraction of the frame's half-height, so the same share at every aspect) and `HoldWideLeg` stops the flight at its wide leg's end. **Both are given back in `Leave`.**

### Sideways, the row and the scroller

Walk rules: [Menu navigation](menu-navigation.md). The picker exposed these:

- **`MenuPage.PageSideways` returns whether it acted** (the menu ticks only then). A turn calls `BS3DGame.RefreshNavEntries(keepFocusOn)` and must keep the **focused entry** (else the cursor falls to ◀) and the **held-direction state** (else a held Right pages the campaign in frames). **What a page leaves, the row takes** (#805): when `PageSideways` declines, `StepNavAcrossRow` moves to the nearest entry beside the focused one *on screen* (`ToGlobal`, `Bounds`, not walk order): About's Play/Next, the Jukebox's controls. **The shoulders have their own door, `MenuPage.ShoulderTurn`** (`PageSideways` by default): About declines `PageSideways` across its player's row (from Play rightwards, from Next leftwards), so the arrows walk the two and turn the page past the row's ends, and it turns on the shoulders from anywhere (#807).
- **The walk descends into `ScrollViewer.Content` by name** (it is not among an `IContainer`'s `Widgets`; the first Down used to land on Back) and **`ScrollNavEntryIntoView`** shifts `ScrollPosition` by how far the entry is outside the viewport, clamped to `ScrollMaximum`. ⚠ **Both sides of that comparison must be global (#740)**: `ScrollViewer.ActualBounds` is in the widget's own space, so `NavViewport` takes only its size and the corner from `ToGlobal`.
- **`KeepNavFocusInView`** runs each frame: a focus *set* rather than stepped is revealed on its first laid-out frame, and a list the wheel moved takes the focus to the first entry wholly in view, silently. `MenuPage.NavArrival` names where the cursor arrives.

## The result page

- **The camera is let go, driven from the page** (the session keeps updating under it, #241, so its `UpdateCamera` would be a second writer). `ResultPage` flies the lens onto **`BackdropScreen`'s own orbit** so Main Menu continues rather than cuts. **On a clear the level's framing is wrong** (it aims at a cluster that went down the drain): `Enter` reframes as the bare island (`FrameOrbitFor(null)`, #639); a loss keeps it.
- **It takes a snapshot (`LevelResult`), not the session**: the level does not stop at the clear, so a live read printed a breakdown that did not add up. A clear's headline is its **star rating** (#111), then "NEW BEST" (below), a note projecting the next star (`NextStarScore`/`NextStarGap`, `StarRating.Rate` in reverse, #385) and, exactly when the next level is shut, `UnlockNote` (#397). **A clear does not always open the next level's sequence lock** (the frontier moves only when the level cleared *was* the frontier), so the note must not quote the star gate unconditionally; `nextlocked=<stars|sequence>` on `result` shows both.
- It names the level (#313: `Level 13 · Star`; the button reads `Next: Elephant`); **a failure adds `Skip to: Elephant`** (#347) under Retry, with `Skipping spends this chapter’s one skip` as its own line.
- ⚠ **On this page, outside the plate, there is no dim**: `MENU_TEXT_DIM` is for asides on a dark plate, and this page has neither plate nor scrim, so the failure reason, `New best` and the skip note vanished over a bright sky. Use `MENU_TEXT` or `MENU_TEXT_BODY`, and make every line off the plate a `ShadowedLabel` (but NEW BEST, which carries its own outline): white alone, the skip note still all but vanished over the Meadow's cloud (#918; ["Text over a live arena"](../game-feedback/level-end-defocus.md)).
- **NEW BEST is a celebration at the loud end (#926)**: on a clear that beat a best, the word is struck onto the page after the last *earned* star seats (`NewBestCelebration.Arrival`, 1.94 s in on a four-star clear, 0.74 s on a one-star one), grows in on an under-damped spring (from 0.1 through a **1.36 times overshoot** at 0.2 s, one ring under it, within 1 % of rest by about 1 s), stays at `NewBestBanner.FONT_DESIGN` (200 design units against the breakdown's 80 and the heading's 170) and sounds `PlayNewBest` on the frame it lands ([the sound](../game-feedback/sound-effects.md#a-new-best-926)). Its letters are filled with a **pastel rainbow** (`NewBestCelebration.Fill`: three raised cosines a third of a turn apart over `LIFT` 0.52, so no channel is darker than that and the lightness never swings) laid across the whole word, so each letter is shaded across its width; the hue flows through the word once in ten seconds, and a pale gloss sweeps across it every 3.2 s. 24 sparkles burst from it as it lands and 8 glints keep twinkling for as long as the page is up. The owner's standing note is that celebrations are built too discreetly and he scales them down, so the numbers err bold.
  - **The tree holds only the place** (`_newBest`, an empty `Widget` of the word's exact size, so the layout is the finished one from the first frame and the word costs the column about 9 px at 720p over the old line); the word is drawn by `ResultPage.DrawOverUi` into the host's overlay batch after the desktop (`MenuPage.DrawOverUi`, called by `BS3DGame.Draw` before the focus prompt), about the widget's centre. A Myra label draws one colour and cannot overshoot its neighbours.
  - **The fill is per-vertex colour, not a shader** (`GradientTextRenderer`): it is FontStashSharp's `IFontStashRenderer2`, so each glyph arrives as a quad and every vertex is recoloured from where it is; the quads are drawn with `BasicEffect`, which every backend has, so Potato and GamePi draw it as they draw the rest of the page (no `.fx`, nothing for `GamePi/Shaders` to compile). The clock is the page's own (`_bannerClock`, never stopped; the reveal's clock latches), so a resize that rebuilds the tree resumes where it was.
  - ⚠ **`BasicEffect` picks its technique when it is first *applied*, not when its flags are set**: a pass taken from `CurrentTechnique` in the constructor belonged to the plain technique and drew every glyph as a flat white box; the constructor applies it once and takes the pass after.
  - ⚠ **A stroked glyph (`FontSystemEffect.Stroked`) drawn through `DrawText` sits `effectAmount` down and to the right of the plain glyph**: the outline is drawn that far back up and left (Myra's rich-text path, which `ShadowedLabel` uses, does not show it).
  - ⚠ **The glyph atlas is premultiplied**, so the vertex colours the renderer writes are (colour times alpha, alpha).
  - The pastel fill is a deliberate exception to the greyscale chrome (["The deliberate colours"](menu-look.md#the-deliberate-colours-and-what-admits-one)). Checked at 1280x720 and 3840x1600 and on the Potato path; the pad and keyboard paths are untouched.
- **The stars are struck, four widgets (#139)**: a `Label` is one colour, so one per slot, written from a single `_revealClock` in `ApplyStars`; the clock is the **only** state (a resize mid-reveal resumes) and the cue fires from a `while` (a long frame still owes every sound).
- **The breakdown lands in a final layout (#665)**: `PinBreakdownColumns` fixes each column at its widest *final* string (`CacheBreakdownText`), so captions do not slide as numbers arrive.
- **Its figures are tabular and its two number columns flush right (#806)**: the "count × worth" details, the values and the total are set by `FontFigures` for their label's face in `Refresh` (`SetBreakdownFigures`) and measured by `FontFigures.Width`, so no 1 sits off its column ("Numbers" in [The menu's look](menu-look.md#numbers)). **The "balls unused" detail is `UnusedShotsAwarded × UnusedShotValue`, the level's own price of a ball and not the flat `UnusedShotPoints`** (`LevelResult` carries it as a fact of the run, so with no ball spare the row still reads "0 × 160" and the product is always the figure beside it, `ResultCopyTests`). Matched and orphaned worths are two figures and this one is three on every shipped level (128 to 976 by `ScoreSim`'s balls and budgets), so the "×" of the unused row is not held under the others' by construction.
- **The words that change with the ending are `LevelResult`'s** (the heading, the milestone, the level line, the bare score, the next-star note, the details, the Next and Skip buttons' names, the notes), so `ResultCopyTests` holds them to the typesetting rules on every ending. The fixed words — Retry, Main Menu, the breakdown's captions and the online status lines — are still literals in `ResultPage`, held by review. The unlock note is prose: set through `SpaceWrap` at the prose leading, with ties holding a price to its star and "Level" to its number.

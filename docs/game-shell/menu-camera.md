# The menu camera

Part of [The game: shell](../game-shell.md).

`BackdropScreen`'s camera is a **flight** (#254), not a fixed orbit. It is the game's one `RecoilCamera` with the shake at rest, the one the gameplay screen poses while playing, so it must stop when a session is on the stack (`MenuPage.UpdatesUnderlying`). Everything advances by elapsed seconds.

## The legs

- **The wide leg is the establishing shot**: one turn per 90 s (`WIDE_ROTATION_SPEED`), roughly level with its target, a look *across* the scene. Its stand-off is **solved from the horizontal half-angle** (a disc of radius R fits a frustum of half-angle t past `R / sin(t)`; the island's R is 26), with a second bound against a map deeper or broader than any shipped.
- **Once a cycle it flies at the map** (or, one cycle in three, at the gun, and one in four over the glass, both below), each cycle rolling its legs (`WIDE_SECONDS`, `APPROACH_SECONDS`, `CLOSE_SECONDS`, `RETREAT_SECONDS`, each with a `…_JITTER_SECONDS`) and its clearance (`CLOSE_CLEARANCE` ± `CLOSE_CLEARANCE_JITTER`). The wide roll's floor is the quality probe's settle (below).
- The close stand-off rides a **clearance floor, not a fit**: 11.4 to 17.1 units across the shipped set (`Cannonball`, a pyramid in a 12-wide field, 11.4 to `Bolt`'s 34-level column 17.1; `[orbit]` lines, 2026-10-12). `CLOSE_MAP_SHARE` never binds; `CLOSE_CLEARANCE` of air off the cluster's bounding sphere decides.
- **The cluster's pass always cranes up and always looks up (#599)**, from `CRANE_LOW_PITCH` (the underside against the sky, the face that says the thing hangs) to `CRANE_HIGH_PITCH` (about the wide leg's own look up, so the retreat pulls straight back). The ends are **angles**, turned into heights against the pass's stand-off every frame; as heights they once started half the cycles over the top looking down. **The pass over the glass is the one leg that looks down** (#1214, below): the owner asked for it on 2026-11-20 as a deliberate leg of its own, reversing #599 for that leg alone.
- **The title steps aside without leaving**: the wordmark scales to `WORDMARK_ASIDE_SCALE` across the approach and back across the retreat (small, in its corner: the owner's ruling), so a full-size name does not cover the close pass.

## The pass at the gun (#1040)

The front end stands a gun on the island ([The menu's own gun](front-end-pages.md)), and **one cycle in three the excursion goes to it instead of the cluster** (`GUN_PASS_SHARE`, rolled with the rest in `RollCycle`; `menugun=pass` makes every cycle one, `menugun=off` none). It is the same flight: the same clock, the same `Closeness` (so the wordmark steps aside to `WORDMARK_ASIDE_SCALE` for it exactly as for the cluster's pass), the same hold. The wide leg is still first and still 12 to 20 s, so the quality probe still measures wide frames, and **`HoldWideLeg` holds it**: the level picker held the wide leg through a 62 s run of `menugun=pass` with no pass begun. The excursion turns to the gun *as it begins* (strictly past the wide leg, so a held wide leg's stopped clock does not begin one) and only if a gun stands; a cycle rolled for it with none standing flies the cluster. The camera comes to whichever gun is drawn: a session kept for Continue wins over the menu's own (`BS3DGame.StandingGun`, `IStandingGun.Position`).

- **Legs**: approach 5 to 7 s, close 10 to 16 s, retreat 6 to 9 s (`GUN_*_SECONDS` and their jitters), 21 to 32 s in all. Longer than the cluster's approach and retreat because the lens swings round the island to reach the gun's side.
- **Where the lens stands** (`GunPass.Pose`, pure geometry with tests): a stand-off from the gun's trunnions (the barrel's reach framed to `GUN_FRAME_SHARE` of the frame's narrower half-angle, solved per frame, times a roll of 0.85 to 1.25; about 8 to 12 units) and `GUN_LIFT` to `GUN_LIFT + GUN_LIFT_JITTER` (3 to 5) over the pins, looking `TARGET_LIFT` over them. At 0.85 the carriage's trail legs just leave the frame's foot and the loading window fills the middle; at 1.25 the whole gun stands in the foreground with the cluster overhead.
- **It turns round the gun on the outer side only**: a 180 degree sweep (`GunPass.SWEEP`) from directly beside the gun on one flank, over the breech, to the other flank, across the **whole excursion** (`Rise`), so the lens is never parked and the turn begins in the approach. A first version swept 220 degrees and was photographed skimming the cluster's lowest balls on the flank; past 90 degrees either side the lens comes in towards the cluster's side of the gun. Which flank it starts on is the one the wide lens is already on (`GunPass.DirectionFrom`), so the approach takes the shorter way round the island.
- **The blend is polar about the island's axis** (radius, bearing and height each linearly, the bearing unwrapped through the pass), not a straight line: a line from a wide stand-off to the far side of the gun would cross the cluster. The field of view eases back to 60 degrees (it is wider on the Neon City's wide leg, #1039).
- ⚠ **Two guards, both continuous in the lens.** `GunPass.KeepClear` holds it outside the cluster's bounding sphere by `GUN_CLUSTER_AIR` (3.5, more than the cluster's own pass leaves, because a ball filling a corner of this pass's frame is noise), and `GunPass.KeepInside` inside the scene's open ground (#1039). On the shipped set neither moves the lens: `GunPassTests` sweeps every shipped level at three window shapes and the stand-offs and lifts above and holds the lens outside the sphere with the 3.5 air to spare, 1.7 units to spare at the tightest (the check was seen to fail at 12.5 units of air). They are the guarantee for a map the set does not have.
- **Captured** (Meadow at 1920x1080 and 3840x1600, Neon City at 3840x1600 and in the half-screen 1920x1568 window that #1039 was filed for, `menugun=pass`, a frame every 3 to 4 s through 60 to 70 s): the gun across the funnel in the wide leg, the lens coming in over the island, both flanks in profile, the view from behind with the cluster overhead (the player's own view), and the retreat with the gun left in the island's foreground. The loading window and the rounds in it are readable from the flanks and from behind; no frame has the lens in the cluster, the glass, the gun or a tower, and in the half-screen Neon City the lens stayed over the island with the first towers 41 units out. During the retreat the aim eases back to the cluster, so the gun slides out at the foot of the frame for the last few seconds.

## The pass over the glass (#1214)

**One cycle in four the excursion goes over the glass** (`GLASS_PASS_SHARE`): the lens climbs off the wide turn to high over the plate, looks almost straight down at the map through it, comes down to a few units over the glass while it turns a quarter of the way round, lingers with the cut and what it bends filling the frame, and climbs back out. It is the same flight as the other two (the same clock, `Closeness`, wordmark step-aside and `HoldWideLeg`), so the wide leg the quality probe measures comes first and the level picker never begins one. One draw in `RollCycle` decides glass, gun or cluster, so the gun keeps its one in three and the cluster has the remaining five in twelve.

- **Only where the pane bends** (`BS3DGame.RefractsCeiling`): never on Low or on the Potato path, where it would show a flat plate from above and nothing it was flown for, and never over a session's plate (the result page's flight, `AdvanceOrbit`'s `frontEnd`). A cycle rolled for it without that flies the cluster's pass, and an `[orbit] glass pass: ... the cluster's pass instead` line says why. `menuglass=pass` flies it every cycle, over a plain pane too, on the floors of every roll, so `shot=` lands on the same moment in every run ([launch arguments](front-end-launch-arguments.md)); `menuglass=off` never.
- **Legs**: approach 5 to 7 s (the lens climbs from beside the island to high over it), close 14 to 20 s, retreat 6 to 9 s (`GLASS_*_SECONDS` and their jitters). On `menuglass=pass` the wide leg ends at 12 s and the pass flies 12 to 17 s (approach), 17 to 26.8 s (descent), 26.8 to 31 s (at the glass) and 31 to 37 s (retreat).
- **Where the lens stands** (`GlassPass`, pure geometry with tests): over the plate, out along its bearing just far enough to look `TILT` (12 degrees) off straight down at the plate's centre, so the top of the frame is the plate's far side and the tip over the top is a pitch, not a roll; an exactly vertical look has no right-hand side, and `RecoilCamera` would snap there. The far height frames the footprint's circumradius to `FAR_SHARE` (0.55) of the frame's narrower half-angle (about 32 units over a twelve-wide field, 40 over Pinwheel, 52 over a twenty-wide one), capped `ROOF_AIR` (8) under a roof (the Big Top's canvas, the cavern's vault; read every frame and eased, the vault being placed off the frame). The descent takes `DESCENT_SHARE` (0.7) of the close leg down to 3.5 to 6 units over the glass (`GLASS_NEAR_HEIGHT`: two or three of the cut's diamonds across the frame's height), **geometric**, so the glass grows in the frame at a steady rate; the retreat climbs back to the far height in its first `CLIMB_SHARE` (0.6) before it swings out. On the closeness's own curve it brought the lens down past the rim of `Bolt`'s plate within 1.3 units in a 21:9 window.
- **Between the cables, not through them.** The four cables rise from the plate's corners (`CeilingRigging.CableOffset`), so the lens comes in over the **middle of the side nearest it** (`GlassPass.FirstBearing`), turns a quarter of the way round during the close leg (`TURN`), and leaves over the middle of the next side; over the plate it stays `CABLE_AIR` (2.5) off every cable. The blend with the wide turn is polar about the axis, as the gun's is, with the bearing carried unwrapped.
- **The glass clears as the lens comes over** (`BS3DGame.SetMenuCeilingClarity`): from the menu plate's `MENU_CEILING_ALPHA` (0.7, for a nearly level look against a bright sky) to the played `CeilingPlate.GLASS_ALPHA` (0.48), on the flight's closeness. At 0.7 the pane was a milky lid over the cluster the cut bends; at 0.48 the balls under it read in colour, broken across the facets. A tour replayed from the Scene page during the pass takes the clearing back with its blend, so its nearly level shots see the menu's 0.7.
- **A picked map can hang under the lens** (#408): on this pass `LensClearOf` also passes a lens that stands `GlassPass.HANG_ABOVE` (1.5) over the incoming plate's top face and `HANG_CABLE_AIR` off its cables, since the bounding sphere reaches over the face and would otherwise hold the map for the whole pass. The plate the lens looks down at is eased towards the higher of the hung plate and the one the framing drifts to, so it climbs before a higher plate hangs and comes down only once a lower one has.
- Held by `GlassPassTests` over every shipped level, at 16:9, 21:9 and 5:4, from twelve starting bearings, at both ends of every roll, under an open sky and under a roof low enough to cap the far height on every level (the Big Top's canvas is not: the highest far lens of the set, over `Organ`, stands at 60.8 at all three shapes, under the crown's 73.5 less `ROOF_AIR`): the whole flight, blend included, stays out of the box the map stands in (its footprint from the lowest ball to the winches) by 2.0 units past 1.5 of air, at least 4.2 off every cable, exactly 12 degrees off straight down and never looking outwards, `ROOF_AIR` under the roof, under 22 units a second. Each check was seen to fail with its rule broken (arriving over a corner: 0.1 off a cable; climbing on the closeness's curve: 0.19 inside the box). The tests cannot build `BackdropScreen`, so they mirror its wide pose, its framing and the pass's rolls, and `TheMirroredFiguresAreBackdropScreensOwn` fails when the source no longer says what they mirror.

## Framing is the map's

- `FrameOrbitFor` takes the aim height from the cluster's own middle and both stand-offs from its measured reach (the set runs from a four-level pancake to a twenty-four-level column; `FitClusterWorldOffset` hangs a deep field higher). A session frames too as it installs a level (`ResultPage` flies out onto this orbit). A new preview drifts onto its framing (`FRAMING_EASE_SECONDS`).
- ⚠ **The lens stays inside the scene's open ground (#1039).** The narrower the window, the further out the wide leg stands, because the island has to fit the horizontal half-angle: 47.1 units at 1920x1568 against 33.2 at 3840x1600. The cities' first towers stand nearer than that: **49.7 units in the City and 41.2 in the Neon City** (`City.OpenRadius`, the nearest box's footprint; the `[orbit]` line says it), so a half-screen window flew the wide leg through the Neon City's towers. `BS3DGame.OpenGroundRadius` gives the clearing (the Potato path's own city on that path), the forest's lamps on the desktop path (`ForestLampLayout.OpenGroundRadius`, 43.7 units: the nearest a pole's tipped shade reaches, #1166), and infinite in every other scene. `OpenGroundFieldOfView` holds the lens `OPEN_GROUND_MARGIN` (6 units) inside it and widens the field of view by the ratio of tangents instead: the Neon City's wide leg at 1920x1568 stands at 35.2 through 75.4° (60° elsewhere), up to `OPEN_GROUND_MAX_FOV` (100°). Past that, only for a window taller than wide, the lens goes on out. A tour from the menu starts on the lens's actual field of view.
- ⚠ **A map is never hung around the lens (#408).** A preview requested mid-flight is solved at once but **hangs only when the lens stands clear of its bounding sphere** by `HANG_CLEARANCE` (under the smallest clearance a cycle can roll, so the wait always ends), drifting at `CLEARING_EASE_SECONDS` meanwhile. Otherwise a thirty-level column hung around a lens skimming a sixteen-level pancake and the drift backed the camera out through the balls. **On the pass over the glass a lens over the incoming plate also counts as clear** (`LensClearOf`): `GlassPass.HANG_ABOVE` over its top face and `HANG_CABLE_AIR` off its cables (`GlassPass.ClearAbove`), since that lens stands inside the sphere for most of the pass with nothing of the map near it.

## The wide leg's length is the probe's

The adaptive-quality probe runs off this same `Update` and settles inside the first ten seconds, so it reaches its verdict on wide frames; a cycle that flew in sooner would let a screen full of cluster decide the tier the whole game runs at. Three routes that reopen the probe (a fresh program, a rolled preview, a release from the result screen) restart the flight on the wide leg. **Four reopen it wherever the flight has got to**: the fullscreen toggle, a change of the render resolution and Auto quality turned back on (all three under Settings, the flight going on behind it), and a hot phone's re-measure ([Adaptive quality](adaptive-quality.md)). Over the cluster's pass that is left alone on measurement (959 balls under the neon city held 330 to 420 FPS through a full cycle). **The pass over the glass is not judged at all**: `Update` passes `judged: false` to the probe for as long as it flies, so a window waits it out.

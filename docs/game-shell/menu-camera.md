# The menu camera

Part of [The game: shell](../game-shell.md).

`BackdropScreen`'s camera is a **flight** (#254), not a fixed orbit. It is the game's one `RecoilCamera` with the shake at rest, the one the gameplay screen poses while playing, so it must stop when a session is on the stack (`MenuPage.UpdatesUnderlying`). Everything advances by elapsed seconds.

## The legs

- **The wide leg is the establishing shot**: one turn per 90 s (`WIDE_ROTATION_SPEED`), roughly level with its target, a look *across* the scene. Its stand-off is **solved from the horizontal half-angle** (a disc of radius R fits a frustum of half-angle t past `R / sin(t)`; the island's R is 26), with a second bound against a map deeper or broader than any shipped.
- **Once a cycle it flies at the map** (or, one cycle in three, at the gun, below), each cycle rolling its legs (`WIDE_SECONDS`, `APPROACH_SECONDS`, `CLOSE_SECONDS`, `RETREAT_SECONDS`, each with a `…_JITTER_SECONDS`) and its clearance (`CLOSE_CLEARANCE` ± `CLOSE_CLEARANCE_JITTER`). The wide roll's floor is the quality probe's settle (below).
- The close stand-off rides a **clearance floor, not a fit**: 11.4 to 17.1 units across the shipped set (`Cannonball`, a pyramid in a 12-wide field, 11.4 to `Bolt`'s 34-level column 17.1; `[orbit]` lines, 2026-10-12). `CLOSE_MAP_SHARE` never binds; `CLOSE_CLEARANCE` of air off the cluster's bounding sphere decides.
- **The lens always cranes up and always looks up (#599)**, from `CRANE_LOW_PITCH` (the underside against the sky, the face that says the thing hangs) to `CRANE_HIGH_PITCH` (about the wide leg's own look up, so the retreat pulls straight back). The ends are **angles**, turned into heights against the pass's stand-off every frame; as heights they once started half the cycles over the top looking down.
- **The title steps aside without leaving**: the wordmark scales to `WORDMARK_ASIDE_SCALE` across the approach and back across the retreat (small, in its corner: the owner's ruling), so a full-size name does not cover the close pass.

## The pass at the gun (#1040)

The front end stands a gun on the island ([The menu's own gun](front-end-pages.md)), and **one cycle in three the excursion goes to it instead of the cluster** (`GUN_PASS_SHARE`, rolled with the rest in `RollCycle`; `menugun=pass` makes every cycle one, `menugun=off` none). It is the same flight: the same clock, the same `Closeness` (so the wordmark steps aside to `WORDMARK_ASIDE_SCALE` for it exactly as for the cluster's pass), the same hold. The wide leg is still first and still 12 to 20 s, so the quality probe still measures wide frames, and **`HoldWideLeg` holds it**: the level picker held the wide leg through a 62 s run of `menugun=pass` with no pass begun. The excursion turns to the gun *as it begins* (strictly past the wide leg, so a held wide leg's stopped clock does not begin one) and only if a gun stands; a cycle rolled for it with none standing flies the cluster. The camera comes to whichever gun is drawn: a session kept for Continue wins over the menu's own (`BS3DGame.StandingGun`, `IStandingGun.Position`).

- **Legs**: approach 5 to 7 s, close 10 to 16 s, retreat 6 to 9 s (`GUN_*_SECONDS` and their jitters), 21 to 32 s in all. Longer than the cluster's approach and retreat because the lens swings round the island to reach the gun's side.
- **Where the lens stands** (`GunPass.Pose`, pure geometry with tests): a stand-off from the gun's trunnions (the barrel's reach framed to `GUN_FRAME_SHARE` of the frame's narrower half-angle, solved per frame, times a roll of 0.85 to 1.25; about 8 to 12 units) and `GUN_LIFT` to `GUN_LIFT + GUN_LIFT_JITTER` (3 to 5) over the pins, looking `TARGET_LIFT` over them. At 0.85 the carriage's trail legs just leave the frame's foot and the loading window fills the middle; at 1.25 the whole gun stands in the foreground with the cluster overhead.
- **It turns round the gun on the outer side only**: a 180 degree sweep (`GunPass.SWEEP`) from directly beside the gun on one flank, over the breech, to the other flank, across the **whole excursion** (`Rise`), so the lens is never parked and the turn begins in the approach. A first version swept 220 degrees and was photographed skimming the cluster's lowest balls on the flank; past 90 degrees either side the lens comes in towards the cluster's side of the gun. Which flank it starts on is the one the wide lens is already on (`GunPass.DirectionFrom`), so the approach takes the shorter way round the island.
- **The blend is polar about the island's axis** (radius, bearing and height each linearly, the bearing unwrapped through the pass), not a straight line: a line from a wide stand-off to the far side of the gun would cross the cluster. The field of view eases back to 60 degrees (it is wider on the Neon City's wide leg, #1039).
- ⚠ **Two guards, both continuous in the lens.** `GunPass.KeepClear` holds it outside the cluster's bounding sphere by `GUN_CLUSTER_AIR` (3.5, more than the cluster's own pass leaves, because a ball filling a corner of this pass's frame is noise), and `GunPass.KeepInside` inside the scene's open ground (#1039). On the shipped set neither moves the lens: `GunPassTests` sweeps every shipped level at three window shapes and the stand-offs and lifts above and holds the lens outside the sphere with the 3.5 air to spare, 1.7 units to spare at the tightest (the check was seen to fail at 12.5 units of air). They are the guarantee for a map the set does not have.
- **Captured** (Meadow at 1920x1080 and 3840x1600, Neon City at 3840x1600 and in the half-screen 1920x1568 window that #1039 was filed for, `menugun=pass`, a frame every 3 to 4 s through 60 to 70 s): the gun across the funnel in the wide leg, the lens coming in over the island, both flanks in profile, the view from behind with the cluster overhead (the player's own view), and the retreat with the gun left in the island's foreground. The loading window and the rounds in it are readable from the flanks and from behind; no frame has the lens in the cluster, the glass, the gun or a tower, and in the half-screen Neon City the lens stayed over the island with the first towers 41 units out. During the retreat the aim eases back to the cluster, so the gun slides out at the foot of the frame for the last few seconds.

## Framing is the map's

- `FrameOrbitFor` takes the aim height from the cluster's own middle and both stand-offs from its measured reach (the set runs from a four-level pancake to a twenty-four-level column; `FitClusterWorldOffset` hangs a deep field higher). A session frames too as it installs a level (`ResultPage` flies out onto this orbit). A new preview drifts onto its framing (`FRAMING_EASE_SECONDS`).
- ⚠ **The lens stays inside the scene's open ground (#1039).** The narrower the window, the further out the wide leg stands, because the island has to fit the horizontal half-angle: 47.1 units at 1920x1568 against 33.2 at 3840x1600. The cities' first towers stand nearer than that: **49.7 units in the City and 41.2 in the Neon City** (`City.OpenRadius`, the nearest box's footprint; the `[orbit]` line says it), so a half-screen window flew the wide leg through the Neon City's towers. `BS3DGame.OpenGroundRadius` gives the clearing (the Potato path's own city on that path, infinite in every other scene). `OpenGroundFieldOfView` holds the lens `OPEN_GROUND_MARGIN` (6 units) inside it and widens the field of view by the ratio of tangents instead: the Neon City's wide leg at 1920x1568 stands at 35.2 through 75.4° (60° elsewhere), up to `OPEN_GROUND_MAX_FOV` (100°). Past that, only for a window taller than wide, the lens goes on out. A tour from the menu starts on the lens's actual field of view.
- ⚠ **A map is never hung around the lens (#408).** A preview requested mid-flight is solved at once but **hangs only when the lens stands clear of its bounding sphere** by `HANG_CLEARANCE` (under the smallest clearance a cycle can roll, so the wait always ends), drifting at `CLEARING_EASE_SECONDS` meanwhile. Otherwise a thirty-level column hung around a lens skimming a sixteen-level pancake and the drift backed the camera out through the balls.

## The wide leg's length is the probe's

The adaptive-quality probe runs off this same `Update` and settles inside the first ten seconds, so it reaches its verdict on wide frames; a cycle that flew in sooner would let a screen full of cluster decide the tier the whole game runs at. Every route that reopens the probe (a fresh program, a rolled preview, a release from the result screen) restarts the flight on the wide leg. **The fullscreen toggle is the exception, left alone on measurement** (959 balls under the neon city held 330 to 420 FPS through a full cycle).

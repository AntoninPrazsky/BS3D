# The city: draw order and windows

Part of [Rendering](../rendering.md).

## Drawing the city near to far

The city is **one instanced draw**, but of `City.PrepareVisible(camera)`'s `Visible` scratch array: the buildings in the frustum, **ordered near to far**.

- **The frame was pixel-bound.** At 3840x1600 the city cost 13.9 ms at `ssaa 2` and 4.4 at `ssaa 1`: ~12.7 ms was pixel work. Culling is the cheap half (off-screen buildings cost no pixels); what costs is a building **on screen and behind another**, whose pixels run the full city shader and are overwritten. **Ordering is what pays**: 13.88 to 7.66 ms mean and the worst second from 32.3 to 101.5 FPS (level, firing, 3840x1600, `quality=high`).
- **Reference machine** (Ryzen 7 5700U iGPU, windowed 1600x900, dome 13, #67): `High` neon 103 to 27.5 ms, city 76 to 23.0; `Medium` 10.5 / 9.3; `Low` 9.6 / 8.4. The block-radius sweep is flat now (14/12/10/8 blocks: 27.5-28.6 ms), so `Low` keeps the authored skyline (`Game/QualityLevel.cs`).
- **`CityPS` must neither `discard` nor write depth**, or the hardware shades before the depth test and the ordering buys nothing. The depth prepass (`DrawDepth` / `InstancedDepth`) stays unused: no hidden pixels are left to reclaim.
- **The sort is a counting sort and approximate** (`DEPTH_BUCKETS` 256; a bucket out of place costs overdraw, never correctness), keyed on the distance to the building's **near side** (centre less radius), since the near surface does the occluding.
- **No allocation:** bounds taken once, buckets and `Visible` allocated with the city, and the **`BoundingFrustum` is held and re-pointed** (it is a class; `new` per frame allocates). Bounds come free from the world matrix (`M41..M43` centre, half the `M11/M22/M33` diagonal a conservative radius).
- The `logfps` line carries `city N/M` (in-frustum of total, ~1090 of 1788 from the play camera). **Check a culling change by image invariance**: a fixed camera (`campos`/`camtarget`) with and without it must differ only in clouds, lit windows and the cluster; every tower silhouette by exactly zero.

## The city's windows

`CityPS` (`InstancedModel.fx`) lays windows out per building and decides per window whether it is lit; the look is `CitySceneConfig`'s `Window*` properties.

- **The raised moulding is invisible at tower distance** (#435): `WindowFrameWidth` is ~0.085 world units, one pixel at 60 units at 1600x900, and `resolvable` switches it off past that. What a window reads by there is four **flat** cues: the **surround** in its own tone (`WindowFrameTone`, a multiple of the wall albedo so neon-city dark walls keep dark frames), the **sill** and its shadow, the **reveal's shadow** along the head (darkens the pane, halves the lamp behind it), and **glazing bars**.
- ⚠ **Box-filter each piece (`WindowSpan`), do not smoothstep it**: a smoothstep across a feature narrower than a pixel still peaks at a half, so a sub-pixel bar draws as a shimmering half-bright line; the box filter gives it its true fraction and sub-pixel pieces blend into the facade's average.
- The Game's tier still sets `WindowFrameWidth` (0 at `Low`, which removes moulding and surround); the sill, reveal and bars draw on every tier. Cost +0.05 ms city, +0.06 neon (1600x900 ssaa 2, `nocap`, against `main`).

## Window reflections (#1065)

The owner: the towers' windows should shine by reflecting the buildings round them, sharply, on `High` and `Ultra`. A pane mirrored only the sky (`ShadePixel`'s environment term along the reflected ray), so it showed the same whatever stood across the street.

- **The map.** `CityReflectionMap` rasterises the towers' boxes (not the cornices, half a unit past their tier; not the far skyline, off the map) into a grid of `TEXEL` (one unit) over the city, `Vector4`: the highest roof over each texel (the street level where none stands), the tower's building id (`floor(anchor * 0.37)`, the one `CityPS` takes from `Style.zw`, both parts packed in one float), its facade kind, and the Chebyshev distance in texels to the nearest tower texel. 870 x 870 for the day city, 12 MB, built with each city while the tier reflects (the Game drops it on `Low` and `Medium`, which never read it).
- **The march.** `InstancedCityReflect` (`InstancedModel/CityReflection.fxh`) is the towers' program (`CityShade`) plus, for a glass pixel within `CityReflectionReach` (40) of the lens, the mirror ray marched over the map for at most `CityReflectionSteps` (16) steps. ⚠ **Through empty texels it jumps** by the distance field, a texel short of the nearest tower; **beside a tower it steps a texel at a time**, so the face it enters is known. A texel a step could not reach across a street from a grazing pane, which is where glass mirrors most, and those panes kept the sky. The face met is shaded with its tower's tone, its kind's windows on a world grid (the map keeps no boxes), each lit by its own hash in the tower's lamp or neon, under the sky and the sun. The street, a climb over the roofs, the map's edge and the end of the steps keep the sky. ⚠ The march starts a texel and a half out from the pane **along its normal**: along the ray, a grazing ray's start was tens of units down the street, past every nearer neighbour.
- ⚠ **The tower is mirrored as glass mirrors** (Fresnel alone, `CityReflectionStrength` 1), and a glass curtain wall's coated panes four times as strongly (`CITY_REFLECT_CURTAIN_COATING`, capped at a whole mirror). Only the sky it replaces keeps the city's dimmer (`SpecularAmbientStrength` 0.07 × `WindowReflectionBoost`): that dimmer is there so a bright sky does not bleach a thousand grazing facades, and through it a tower was a hundredth of itself and could not be seen. The sky taken away is exactly what `ShadePixel` gave the pixel, with its blend of plaster and glass (smoothness and boost lerped by the glass share), so a pixel only partly glass loses no more sky than it had; a strength under 1 blends the sky and the tower alike towards the plain pane.
- ⚠ **Gated by the distance from the lens, not by a pixel's footprint.** Ungated it cost +1.9 ms from a street and +4.9 ms from the arena: the play view is a wall of distant panes, each of them marching. A footprint threshold in world units let nearly all of them through at 3840x1600 with supersampling 2. Within 40 units it reads where it is seen, the chapter intros' flights between the towers, and the play camera has no pane that close.
- **A technique of its own**, included last in `InstancedModel.fx`, so its uniforms close the constant buffer and no other program moved: `CityPS`, `MainPS`, `CityFarPS` and `GlassPS` disassemble identical to before it. Selecting it and marching nothing costs +0.03 to +0.07 ms; the cost is the march.
- **Cost** (Testbed 3840x1600, ssaa 2, `sceneseed=1066`, `alt=cityreflect=0;cityreflect=1` in one process): down a day street (`campos=45,-84,230 camtarget=45,-55,40`) 9.26 → 10.00 ms; down a neon street (`campos=37.5,-84,230 camtarget=37.5,-55,40`) 10.36 → 10.84 ms; from the arena (`campos=0,-2,40 camtarget=-60,8,-160`) +0.02 ms in either city. **On the reference APU** (the same poses and seed, 1600x900, ssaa 2, 72 paired cycles a pose): down the day street +3.86 ms on a frame of 42.19 (the median of the paired differences; the sign held in 89 % of cycles), down the neon street +2.32 on 53.11 (85 %), from the arena +0.21 and +0.39, inside the noise (54 and 62 %). That is the same share of the frame as on the desktop (+9 and +4 % against +8 and +5 %), and `Low` and `Medium`, the tiers the APU plays at, never reflect.
- Picked per draw by `QualityPreset.CityReflections` (`High`, `Ultra`); the map is built the first time a tier wants it. ⚠ The Testbed draws them by default (`cityreflect=1`, its authored look): a Testbed figure standing in for `Low` or `Medium` passes `cityreflect=0`. Not under OpenGL ES (`GraphicsSupport.CityReflections`): the technique compiles there as `CityPS`, and no map is built. The Testbed's `cityreflect=`, `cityreflectreach=` and `cityreflectsteps=` dials ("Sweeping variants inside one process" in `docs/testbed/capture-and-measurement.md`).

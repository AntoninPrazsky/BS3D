# Glass: refraction, the crystal cup, the drain and the ceiling plate

Part of [Rendering](../rendering.md).

Glass bends what is behind it by two routes. The cup re-resolves a finished frame under a layer nothing stands in front of; the ceiling plate has the cluster in front of it, so it bends a **copy** of the frame and draws inside the scene. The plate's sun shadow is separate: "The ceiling's glass shadow" in [Sun shadows](sun-shadows.md). The layers are in [The post-process pipeline](post-processing.md).

## The result page's crystal cup (#426)

- The cup draws again into `PostProcessPipeline.RefractionTarget` (configured like `ForegroundTarget`, own depth buffer) through `InstancedRefraction`: red and green are the screen-uv shift the nearest surface sends the eye by (its view-space normal carried `RefractionDepth` world units in), blue is how edge-on it is.
- Where the layer has coverage, `ForegroundCompositePS` re-resolves the frame (`ResolveLinear(uv)`, one copy) at the shifted coordinates, per channel with `REFRACTION_DISPERSION`, darkens towards the outline (`REFRACTION_EDGE_DARK`) and lays it under the layer premultiplied. Only the Game, only the crystal tier (`TrophyPodium.Refracts`); lazy.
- **Natural, not perfect (#640):** `InstancedModelRenderer.Crystal` selects `InstancedCrystal` (`Glass.fxh`): a milky **veil** (`CRYSTAL_VEIL_AMOUNT`) with a thin-film rainbow and **specks**, in the *mesh's* space so they turn with the cup. ⚠ Straight golden rutile threads were removed (a thin straight line cannot be told from a rendering fault).
- **The drain's glass (#640):** `DrainGlass` selects `InstancedDrainGlass`: cord, **scuffs** down the slope (`DRAIN_SCUFF_SPIRAL`), a **frost** band inside the mouth, a few **seeds**. ⚠ Too many scuffs read as rain streaks, a wide frost band as smoke; judge against the front end's close view.

## The ceiling's glass bends what is behind it (#541)

- `PostProcessPipeline.GrabScene` leaves the scene target, box-filters it into a target of its own (`SceneGrab`, linear, no curve) and re-binds: the one mid-frame target switch, which is why **the scene target is `PreserveContents`** (every executable clears it explicitly on bind).
- ⚠ **Where the copy is taken is the whole correctness.** From under the plate (play camera, result orbit, most of the front end) a copy at the pane would hold the cluster and glass beside a top ball would drag it into the pane. So `BS3DGame.GrabCeilingBackground` runs straight after `BeginSceneDraw` and, from under the plate's underside, takes the copy there (sky, backdrop, island). From level with or above, it does nothing and `DrawCeilingGlass` copies just before the pane. The pane writes its pixel whole (alpha 1); fireworks and the gun's glass come after it (no depth write, #299). ⚠ **From under the plate the session draws the pane before its additive layers** (the aim beam, the smears, the line sparks, the muzzle rays, the blasts, the wormhole; `BS3DGame.CameraUnderCeiling`): they stand in front of the glass and hold no depth, so a pane drawn after them painted the copy over them and the aim beam vanished against the plate (#1013). From level with it or above, the pane keeps its slot at the end.
- **`InstancedGlass`** traces each pixel's ray through the slab in mesh space (in at index 1.5, across to the first of six planes, out, on for `GLASS_BEHIND_DISTANCE` 30), samples the copy there per channel (`GLASS_DISPERSION`, shift capped softly at `GLASS_MAX_SHIFT`). **A flat parallel slab only offsets a ray**: the bend lives in the cut (`CutSlabMesh`'s faceted edge, flutes and diamond field; "The setting, in one copy" in `docs/scenes.md`). A ray reaching the top over the ground crown takes the crown's facet (`GlassRimAt`).
- ⚠ **Three first-capture errors:** Fresnel alone darkened grazing views (the removed light must be laid back as the reflected sky); total-internal-reflection rays darkened the view (black bands from the edge): they are sent off as the eye's ray mirrored in the far face; the diamond cut shattered edge-on views into slivers, so it fades out between 78 and 87 degrees off the normal.
- **Hand-made (#640):** `GLASS_CUT_UNEVEN`/`GLASS_CUT_TWIST` (per-cell facet slope and twist off a hash, vanishing with the tilt so cells still meet), **cord** (`GLASS_CORD_STRENGTH`) and **seeds** (bubbles on two planes, `GLASS_SEED_DEPTHS`; more and bigger read as too many). **+0.10 ms with the drain's wear** (RX 6900 XT, Release, level Heart, 1920x1080 High ssaa 2: 4.61 to 4.72 ms).
- **Tiers:** every rung but `Low` (`QualityPreset.CeilingRefraction`), only while the plate's box is in the frustum (`BS3DGame.CeilingInView`). Reference APU **~1.2-1.6 ms on every rung** where in frame (probably the copy, not the pane); `Medium` on the desktop ~0.1 ms. The Testbed does not refract the plate. Why `Low` gives it up: `docs/game-shell.md`.

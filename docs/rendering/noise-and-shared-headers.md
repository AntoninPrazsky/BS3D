# The noise library and the shared shader headers

Part of [Rendering](../rendering.md).

## `Noise.fxh`

`BS3DLibs/Prazsky.Shaders/Content/Shaders/Noise.fxh`: gradient noise (2D/3D, quintic fade), fBm with a **rotation between octaves**, ridged fBm, and jittered Voronoi (nearest distance, and the edge field `F2 - F1`, in 3D also the winning cell's id). Scene shaders and `InstancedModel.fx` include it. GPU-only: nothing plants objects on these fields, so no C# mirror is needed and the hashes are quality-first (Hoskins' sine-free hash). Callers budget noise evaluations per pixel.

**The look of a procedural image is decided by its fields first.** A sum of plane-wave sines keeps its planes however many terms it has and reads as a demo-scene plasma. Three things separate a modern result: **rotated octaves** (aligned octave lattices read as a grid), **domain warping with noise, twice** (and colour by the warp intermediates: the dream's marbling) where sines under a sine warp still slide as blobs, and **occlusion and contact** (SDF ambient occlusion, crevice darkening, normals perturbed by the field's own gradient so every light agrees).

## `PerturbNormalFromHeight`

The tangent-free normal tilt every height field ends in: the scenes' terrain, sea chop, regolith and island stone through `PerturbNormalFromHeight`, every ball style's relief (the vinyl's moulding, the wool's strands, the gem's facets, the special kinds) through `PerturbNormalFromHeightAtAnySize`. ⚠ **It carries a determinant floor, and the guarded version must stay the only copy**: at a grazing angle `ddx`/`ddy` of the world position go near-degenerate and `normalize(0)` is a NaN speck on a far surface. Twelve copies once existed and only two had the floor (#297): a fix does not travel beyond the scene it was noticed in. The one body is `TiltNormalByHeight`, which takes the floor; the two entries differ only in it.

- ⚠ **The floor is an area, and the tilt comes out as the true slope times determinant / max(determinant, floor)**, the determinant being the pixel's own area on the surface, |ddx × ddy| of the position (|ddx| times |ddy| times the sine of the angle between them). `PerturbNormalFromHeight`, the scenes', floors at 1e-4 world units squared (a pixel 0.01 units across), which a landscape's pixels are far over. A ball is half a unit in radius, and under that floor every ball over about 25 display pixels in radius at `ssaa` 2 had its relief scaled by (pixel / 0.01)²: a seventh of itself on the menu's close pass, whole in play, so the vinyl's moulding, the wool's strands and the gem's facets showed only from afar (#1216). **Every ball technique calls `PerturbNormalFromHeightAtAnySize`**, whose floor is `RELIEF_FLOOR_SHARE` (0.5) of |ddx| times |ddy|, so it engages only where the two derivatives shear to within 30° of parallel (on a ball, the limb seen along a diagonal of the screen, about 75° from face-on) and the tilt is the true slope everywhere else at every size; `BallReliefSizeSourceTests` refuses a ball file that calls the world floor. The scenes keep theirs: their surfaces near the lens (the island's cap, the gun) sit under it too, but they were tuned that way, and a change there is a look change in every scene. The scenes call the same arithmetic as before the split; the compiler schedules some of their programs differently (16 of the 51 effects' bytes differ, derivatives hoisted and registers renamed, `d3dcompiler_47` `ps_5_0`), and a frame of the sea, of the cavern and of the island's cap from each build differs by no more than one code in any channel (Testbed, `nopost clockstill sceneseed=0`, the cluster behind the lens and the gun's magazine, which deals at random there, left out).

## Shared headers (#581)

- `Hash21` lives in `Noise.fxh`; ⚠ **keep its constants**: they placed every lit window and meadow flower. `Noise.fxh` also has integer hashes `HashMix`/`HashCell`/`HashSalt`/`HashBits` for what is rolled once a cell (#674); a seed is held in a `HASH` and a computed salt passed through `HASH_SALT`, because under OpenGL ES the same names are a float key hash ([OpenGL ES](opengl-es.md)).
- `Craters.fxh` (moon, Mars), `Rocks.fxh` (outback, Mars), `Grass.fxh` (meadow, savanna, each with its own `GRASS_COMB_STRETCH`/`GRASS_FBM_GAIN`), `ForestGround.fxh` (forest, aurora's night forest), `SceneFog.fxh` (#963: a scene's height mist and haze, closed form along the ray; the aurora's snow, far stand and sky, and `InstancedModel`'s triplanar programs, behind a branch on `SceneFogDensity`, 0 in every other scene).
- **A shared header holds functions and static constants only**; the uniforms it reads stay declared in each scene *before* the include, so no scene's constant buffer moves. Genuinely diverged copies stay separate (`CraterLayer`/`CraterField`, `RockLayer`, `Fbm3` in `Space.fx`, the other scenes' `TerrainHeight`s).

# The Potato path (GamePi, #789)

Part of [Rendering](../rendering.md).

GamePi (Raspberry Pi 5, `docs/build-and-release.md`) draws through its own path: none of this area's effects exists there (DesktopGL's MojoShader stops at Shader Model 3.0) and the V3D could not afford them. The host half is `Game/BS3DGame.Potato.cs`, branched into from `BeginSceneDraw`, `FinishSceneDraw`, `DrawTranslucentsBehindGlass`, `CompositeForegroundLast` and `BeginOverlayLayer`; the nine effects are in `GamePi/Shaders`.

## Shape

- **Forward, into the 8-bit back buffer**, or with Resolution below native (#801) into an 8-bit colour+depth target scaled up by `PotatoUpscale.fx`'s one bilinear tap before the menus and HUD draw at native. ⚠ Its depth is **discarded before it is left** (`Platform/TargetDiscard`: `glInvalidateFramebuffer`) or the tiler writes it to memory: the scale-up cost ~2.9 ms with it, ~0.9-1.3 without.
- No `PostProcessPipeline` (no HDR, bloom, defocus, motion blur, grain, aberration), no `SceneRenderer` (the island stands under the dome's gradient), no `Sky.fx`, no shadows. Every effect ends in `PotatoOutput.fxh`'s `ToDisplay` (exposure, ACES fit, sRGB), so colours stay linear to the last line; an RGBA16F target plus resolve cost 9.0 ms against 4.5 drawn straight (#785).
- **`PotatoModel.fx`** keeps `InstancedModel.fx`'s lighting model and nothing more: `PotatoLit`, `PotatoTextured` (island stone, no relief), `PotatoBall` (every `BallShading` as one lit coloured ball with heartbeat and ripple). `InstancedModelRenderer` recognises it by technique and draws through `DrawPotato`; tintless kinds get stand-in colours; translucent light (`EmissiveTint`) is kept apart so the alarm flash and the cup's glints are not capped at the glass's alpha.
- ⚠ **One indexed uniform array per GL shader** ("GamePi" in `docs/build-and-release.md`).
- **No anti-aliasing filter at 720** (`Fxaa` +3.3-3.8 ms, 4x MSAA +1.8 ms: neither held the refresh; edges are drawn instead, below). `upscale=soft` (cubic B-spline) is **off by default: +1.5-1.8 ms** (V3D full-screen passes cost ~0.5-0.6 ms per texture tap at 1080p).

## The Windows build runs it too (#808)

With `potato` or Potato on the Quality row (next start): `Prazsky.Shaders` compiles `GamePi/Shaders` again for DirectX 11 into `Content/Potato/Shaders`, a Potato run's content manager (`Game/PotatoContent.cs`) looks under `Content/Potato` first (six of the nine share a desktop effect's name), and the run is held at the tier (`Platform/QualityLock`). `PotatoProfile.fxh` holds what the two compilers need apart:
- **Profile names** (DirectX 11 takes nothing below SM 4, MojoShader nothing above 3: techniques compile `POTATO_VS`/`POTATO_PS`) and **`VPOS`** (SM4 has none).
- ⚠ **A pixel shader's input must begin with the position under DirectX, and nothing says so when it does not**: stages pair by input order, so every input read its neighbour's value (all six ports compiled and drew; the confetti fell black). `POTATO_PIXEL_HEAD` is that member under DirectX, nothing under OpenGL.
- `PotatoShaderSourceTests` hold these in the sources' text; `build.yml` compiles DirectX, `gamepi-shaders.yml` OpenGL.

## Cost levers (Pi 5, 1920x1080, `nocap logfps`, mean of the last six one-second readings of a 26 s run)

- **The balls were the frame** (Girandole, 1204 balls: 30.8 ms; without their draw 12.3; `ballflat` draws them unlit to isolate shading). `BallRenderSet.LodBias` 2: 25.0. Upload **nearest first** (`OrderForPotato`) and draw with **no `clip()`**: a shader that can discard defeats early depth rejection (26.8 ms with the clip, **15.5 ms** without). Since #794 only the aim ghost is a size (`GhostScale`); dithered balls go in a second run of the instance buffer through `PotatoBallDither` (the technique with `clip()`), so only they pay. ⚠ A VNC client costs 3-5 ms: compare only runs in the same state.
- **A transparent material has one wall (#797):** `DrawShell`/`DrawHollow` skip the far wall when `InstancedModelRenderer.Potato` (Pennant, a Bubble level, 22.2 to 18.7 ms, 2026-10-05).
- **Scene lamps at the vertex (#795):** the volcano and neon city came out near black. `PotatoModel.fx` takes the desktop's `SceneLight*` uniforms; `BeginPotatoSceneDraw` lights the neon ring and the volcano (`VolcanoLava`). ⚠ **Per pixel is too dear** (Cabinet 19.9 to 34.1 ms): `PotatoVS` runs them unrolled with constant indices (a loop indexing three arrays by its counter is the dynamic indexing GL forbids). Lamps needing a backdrop (campfires, big top, planetshine, earthshine, lightning) push none. Lava gets `BallCrustTint`/`BallCrustDark` and a `BallGlow` (`LavaGlow x 0.3`, matched to the desktop's Caldera luminance); it glows evenly, not through cracks (the owner's verdict).
- **Compute per-draw constants on the CPU** (#804): MonoGame compiles with **no preshader**, so uniform-only expressions run per pixel. `DrawPotato` multiplies out sRGB decodes, hemisphere, reflectance, roughness and the crust/emission/glow/ripple colours; the heartbeat's `exp`s go to the vertex. Ball 391 to 250 instructions; native Girandole 16.56 to 13.58 ms. **`V3D_DEBUG=shaderdb` is the instrument** (no run-to-run noise).

## Edges are drawn where they are (#804)

A full-screen filter guesses edges from colour and pays for every pixel; Potato has only **geometric edges**, so they are drawn (discontinuity edge overdraw).
- **Rim for every ball** (`PotatoBallRim`, `BallRimMesh`, `rims=<pixels>`): after all balls, `RimPass` draws each as an instanced strip on its exact silhouette circle, blended, depth-tested, **not depth-written**, alpha 1 on the outline falling to 0 a pixel outside, measured per pixel (round at any LOD). **No depth bias** (the ring hides behind its own ball); dithered balls and the shot's stretch get none. ⚠ Shade it at the normal **half a pixel inside the limb for both rows** (the grazing normal peaks Fresnel into an outline). +0.3 to +1.2 ms.
- **Fins for other outlines** (`EdgeFinMesh`, `PotatoFinLit`/`PotatoFinTextured`, `fins=<pixels>`; `POTATO_FIN_PIXELS` 1, `fins=0` off, `finsall`/`finsless=` for taking it apart): island, drain, plate, barrel, collar, carriage, wheels (**not the rollers**: dozens of instances would each be handed every fin). A collapsed quad per candidate edge that `PotatoFinVS` opens a pixel outward where it is an outline or a sharp convex **crease**. Rules (`EdgeFinTests`): **no fin on a concave edge** (hidden by the surface) except a **concave crease where shading breaks**, with the fin on the face that faces the eye more and a **decal depth bias** (`FinRasterizer`); a crease is a **shading break** (vertex normals > 30 degrees apart), not a sharp meeting; an open rim opens only where its face is drawn; no edge a wall runs on past (`MeshBuilder`). Only meshes built inside `EdgeFins.Wanted()` get fins (all: 142 000 edges, 177 ms of load); `SelectLive` picks the frame's edges on the CPU and fins are shaded at the vertex. **About +0.66 to +0.78 ms on the Pi at native; on by default** (heaviest level 15.2 ms against 16.1; Resolution Auto steps to 720 if a Pi does not hold it).
- ⚠ **`RasterizerState.DepthBias` is in the depth buffer's [0, 1] and the framework multiplies by 2^24 for GL**: -2 put every fin in front of everything.
- ⚠ **The muzzle's collar is lathed inside out** (its profile runs up the lathe's axis; normals and front faces inward); only its rims get fins. Fix filed as #812, unmerged (it pales the round's colour, tuned in #478).
- **The drain's seams are the band's own to draw:** the gold band goes under the stone and the pit sheath, so no mesh edge marks where the eye sees it end. Each band vertex carries its height off the surface and `PotatoBand` fades it over its last pixel (`PotatoBandUnderPit` in pit scenes only). ⚠ **In pit scenes the glass goes down before the band** (its half-covered pixels stood in the depth buffer and failed the glass: dark specks); the band writes depth (else the near glass wall overdrew the lip); no rim fins on it (`EdgeFins.Wanted(rims: false)`).

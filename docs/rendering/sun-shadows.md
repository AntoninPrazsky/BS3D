# Sun shadows

Part of [Rendering](../rendering.md).

One orthographic shadow map from the sun, fitted round the camera, drawn by `SceneRenderer.DrawShadowMaps` and read by every receiver through `Shadows.fxh` (#469, #471). **What a scene pays is mostly the receiver, not the casters**: two thirds or more of the map's price is the receiving wherever there is ground, and only the city's towers cast as much as they receive ("Which scenes, and the fit", below). (The cloud shadow, `Clouds.fxh`'s `CloudSunlight`, is separate.)

## The map

- **`SunShadowMap`**: a 32-bit `Single` target (clip depth in red; MonoGame exposes no sampleable depth buffer) and **no depth buffer** (#591): the caster pass keeps the nearest caster with `BlendFunction.Min` (`SceneRenderer.ShadowCasterBlend`). Saved 64 MB at 4096 and 0.35 ms in the forest (laptop).
- **Fit:** each frame `Fit` fits the window to a box (`ShadowConfig.Extent` 260 units square, a height range) in light space, centred **ahead of the lens** by `SHADOW_LOOK_AHEAD` (0.3 of its width: shadows reach ~208 units instead of 130, #662).
- ⚠ **Snap the window to whole texels in light space AND anchor the light's view on a fixed point (the world origin)** (#566). The sun is fixed per dome, so only the box's centre moves; fractions of a texel re-rasterise every caster edge and shadows swim. Anchored on the camera the snap rounded a constant and the window slid.
- **Size and tier:** `ShadowConfig.MapSize` 4096 (0.063 units a texel); `QualityPreset.ShadowMapCap` holds Medium and Low to 2048 (a *cap*: a tier only takes away, #298); `Ultra` doubles to 8192 via `QualityPreset.ShadowMapScale` -> `SceneRenderer.ShadowMapSizeScale`; Testbed `shadowmap=` (`ShadowMapSizeOverride`) pins a size over both. The Game's `[fps]` line prints `shadow 4096`/`off`. The Testbed has no tier; **pre-#484 figures were at 2048, compare through `shadowmap=2048`**.
- **Cost** (Testbed, the play vantage `campos=0,-4,30 camtarget=0,-8,0` over `Maps/Full.json`, ssaa 2, `fpscap=1000`, `alt=shadowmap=2048;shadowmap=4096;shadowmap=8192` in one process, 46 to 55 paired cycles, the sign held in every one, 5900X / RX 6900 XT): the savanna (dome 14) at 1920x1080 2.91 ms at 2048, +0.04 at 4096, +0.18 at 8192; `Ultra`'s step from 4096 to 8192 at 3840x1600 (`nopost`) **+0.03 ms on the desert and +0.17 on the savanna** (4.91 to 4.94, 8.03 to 8.20). **Memory stops High at 4096**: 4 bytes a texel (the 32-bit `Single` target, no depth buffer) = 16.8 MB at 2048, 67 MB at 4096, **268 MB at 8192**, a quarter of a gigabyte and four times High's, so the adaptive probe never picks Ultra (only the player: the Settings row or `quality=ultra`). A finer map sharpens the penumbra, it does not soften it.
- ⚠ **Drop the first windows of a measurement launched minimised without activation** (`SW_SHOWMINNOACTIVE`, the project's way of launching without stealing focus): Windows resizes such a window once, about twenty seconds in, and only the `[fps]` windows after the `[camera] … aspect` line that reports it measure the real frame. Averaging them in once made 4096 and 8192 read as equal.

## Drawing and casters

- **`DrawShadowMaps(scene, camera, sun)` runs in all three executables before the scene target is bound** (a mid-frame target switch costs a multisample resolve; the Game calls it first in `BeginSceneDraw`) and leaves the back buffer bound. **No-op** without a map, at `SceneDetail` <= 0.5, at `ShadowStrength` 0, and with the sun under `SHADOW_MIN_SUN_HEIGHT` (0.08): receivers then get strength 0.
- **Casters:** `Acacia.fx`'s `ShadowCaster` (scatter, hearth stones, culling off) and `InstancedModel.fx`'s `InstancedDepth` via `DrawDepth` (island `ArenaIsland.DrawShadow`, gun `CannonRig.DrawShadow`, cluster). Both use `Shadows.fxh`'s `ShadowViewProjection`, so caster and receiver cannot disagree.
- **The glass does not cast, as a rule** (a map has no opacity: the drain's glass would cast as stone). The ceiling's glass casts by arithmetic in every receiver (below).
- **The host draws the island and gun.** In the Game `GameplayScreen` registers as `BS3DGame.StandingGun` when `BuildLevel` builds a session and **`TearDown` must clear it** (else the front end casts a phantom gun); it also draws the gun in the front end ("A session kept for Continue leaves its gun standing" in `docs/game-shell.md`). The editor registers nothing.
- **The cluster casts** (`BallRenderSet.DrawShadow`; Testbed `ballshadow=<0|1>`). The ball collection must have run first (Game screens collect before `BeginSceneDraw`; `DrawSetting` opens an empty one, #672; the Testbed collects at the top of `Draw`). ⚠ **What is not on the map is collected after `BeginSceneDraw`, so it casts nothing**: the aim ghost (#894) and the kind card's ball (#735). The depth shader applies a ball's dissolve (below) but not the ghost's beat, a size in the same channel that only `PatternVS` turns into a vertex-side scale, so the ghost's shadow would be a whole-size ball. Both are still bucketed, LOD-picked and drawn with the rest, and receive the sun and the map. ⚠ **Merge buckets per LOD (at most four `DrawDepth` calls), not per bucket**: every LOD's renderer wraps the same `Effect`, so each technique switch lands on shared state. Laptop (`Giraffe`, 420 balls): +0.06 ms at `ssaa=1`; not re-measured on the desktop.
- **A fading ball's shadow thins with it** (#920). `DepthPS` cuts `BallCommon.fxh`'s two-sided dissolve clip over cells of **one map texel** (`floor(SV_POSITION.xy)`: the bound target is the map, so no uniform is needed), and the receiver's 3 x 3 box averages that dither into a proportionally lighter shadow. ⚠ **It must not read `DissolvePixelSize`**: that is a measure of the scene target, set by `BallRenderSet.Draw` after this pass, so in the caster it still holds the last frame's value. Only a lone positive dissolve thins (dead weight, #620, up to `BallRenderSet.DEAD_DISSOLVE`): a colour crossing (the magazine's re-colour, thaw, lock-in, infection) draws a ball at `+d` and at `-d` in two buckets, the two dithers are complements over the same texels, and its shadow stays whole. Zero is never clipped (`DissolveNoise` is a `frac()`), so the island, the gun, the city and the scatter write the map they always did. ⚠ **A change to the form of the clip in `BallCommon.fxh` is a change to `Depth.fxh`'s copy.** At 0.5 about 0.4 of a ball's shadow is left and at 0.9 about 0.06 (display luminance on the cap, which understates the linear light; the map was whole before). **Cost of the clip in the caster: nothing measurable**, with no ball dissolving (what a fading cluster's shadow costs is not measured). Desktop Testbed Release over `Maps/Full.json`'s 1000 balls, none of them dissolving, 3840x1600, ssaa 2, `nopost`, `fpscap=1000`, `campos=0,30,45 camtarget=0,-2,0`, dome 1, 5900X / RX 6900 XT; a throwaway build with a dial that draws the caster without the clip, alternated with the shipped one in one process, 66 to 70 paired cycles: the desert 7.08 ms either way, the forest with its scatter casters 12.57 against 12.58, the sign holding in 79 and 67 % of the cycles. The map's own cost at that pin (`alt=shadow=1;shadow=0`, 60 and 51 cycles, every sign held) is 0.45 ms on the desert and 1.13 in the forest. The Testbed has no argument that dissolves a ball: a look needs a local patch that writes a dissolve onto the cluster's instances, with `at=0.01:F5` to freeze it.

## The receivers

- `Shadows.fxh` is the one copy of `ShadowMap`, `ShadowViewProjection`, `ShadowTexel`, `ShadowTexelWorld`, `ShadowStrength`, `ShadowBias`, `SunShadow()`. Receivers multiply the **sun term** only; `ShadowReceiver` pushes the parameters. `InstancedModel.fx` folds it into `ShadePixel`'s `sunlight` (island, drain, gun, city and balls receive from one push, so balls pay for taps even where nothing shadows them: gate with a per-renderer flag like `DirLightStrength` if it matters). `Savanna.fx` reads its **base** normal, not the combed one.
- **Filter:** 3 x 3 box, bilinear-weighted over a 4 x 4 block of texels (#674), faded over the last 6 % of the map; thin fronds still leave square holes in the depth map. **On DirectX the block is four `GatherRed` reads** (#1101; `ShadowMap` is a `Texture2D` there for the method call): each at the corner its 2 x 2 quarter's texels share (`texelBase * ShadowTexel`, exact, stepped by integer offsets of two texels), half a texel from every edge of the footprint decision, and compared and weighted by the same expression as the taps, so the frame is the point taps' frame to the pixel. ⚠ **A gather's components run anticlockwise from the bottom left**: `w` is the quarter's top-left (smaller u and v), `z` its top-right, `x` its bottom-left, `y` its bottom-right. **Under GL** (Shader Model 3.0 has no gather) the block is still sixteen point taps, `tex2Dlod` and not `tex2D` because they sit under a `[branch]`. Hardware PCF (`SamplerState.ComparisonFunction`, four bilinear comparison taps) reads the same sixteen texels in the same four instructions: all it would move is the sixteen compares and weights, into the sampler, whose weights come in 8-bit sub-texel steps rather than the shader's floats.
- **Cost of the read** (#1101): every receiver's pixel program reads the map in four `gather4` where the point taps took sixteen `sample_l` (the palm shader's two call sites, 8 against 32). One process a figure, A B B A, 5900X / RX 6900 XT: the Game at Ultra (the 8192 map), 3840x1500, in play with `Giraffe`'s cluster set in the forest (`levelfile=`, seconds 22 to 40): **10.48 ms against the taps' 10.58** (four runs each, all within 0.03 of their build's median); the Testbed's pin (Release, 3840x1500, ssaa 2, `Maps/Full.json`, the game camera): forest 9.00 against 9.09, tropical 5.74 against 5.80. A tenth of a millisecond: the sixteen reads were never most of what a receiver costs.
- **Bias:** the lookup is lifted along the normal (`SHADOW_NORMAL_OFFSET_TEXELS` 1.5 texels of `ShadowTexelWorld`, `SunShadowMap.TexelWorld`: the fit's width over the map's size, pushed once a frame with the rest) plus slope-scaled `SHADOW_BIAS_UNITS` (0.12 world units, x3.5 edge-on). ⚠ **0.45 with no normal offset (until #642) is a ball's diameter at x3.5**: a ball on the island's dish threw no shadow at its foot and read as levitating.

## Which scenes, and the fit

- **`ShadowConfig` (`Strength`, `Extent`, `MapSize`) is on the base `SceneConfig`; `Strength` 0 = no map at all.** ⚠ **The receivers `RegisterShadowReceivers` collects (`Backdrop.ShadowReceivers`) must match the scenes `Backdrop.TryShadowFit` fits**: fitted-not-receiving casts into a map nobody reads; receiving-not-fitted gets 0 every frame.
- **Eleven backdrops have a map** (savanna, meadow, forest, mountains, desert, outback, beach, volcano, Mars, polar ice at strength 0.9; the Moon at 1, for its rocks, #680). **Out:** the sea (a shadow inside its Fresnel/foam terms is an unrequested look decision), the storm (no ground), the other six sky-replacing scenes (no sun over the horizon; the Moon is the one that keeps a ground).
- **The city and neon city** are not `SceneRenderer`'s (`GetSceneConfig` answers null); both hosts call `SetHostShadowScene(scene, shadows, groundY, below, above, receivers)` once at load. The neon sun is under `SHADOW_MIN_SUN_HEIGHT`, so it costs nothing and the day/night split stays in one place.
- Casters by scene: savanna scatter and hearth stones; tropical 110 palms and rocks (`Palm.fx`'s own caster); forest via `ForestScatterRenderer.DrawShadow` (host); island and gun in all (host). ⚠ **`Palm.fx`'s caster must sway too** (`Sway()` is one function both techniques call), and **the tropical rocks cast with sway strength 0** (their `TEXCOORD0.x` is a circumference this shader reads as sway weight).
- **The fit is computed per scene** (`TryShadowFit`): a height range stored in `ShadowConfig` would be a second copy of the terrain proportions drifting silently. `SHADOW_FIT_MARGIN` 10, `SHADOW_ISLAND_HEADROOM` 12 over `ArenaIsland.TOP_Y`. Mountain and volcano take **half** their relief (a range fitted to a peak coarsens the bias at the gun's feet).
- **A map nobody casts into is skipped** (gate: the `extraCasters` callback): the editor draws no island, gun or wood and would pay taps to learn everything is lit.
- **Cost** (desktop Testbed Release, no level loaded, dome 8, 1600x900 ssaa 4, `nopost nooverc`, `fpscap=1000`, `campos=0,6,60 camtarget=0,-8,0`, a 2048 map, `alt=shadow=0;shadow=1` in one process, 47 to 61 paired cycles, the sign held in every one, 5900X / RX 6900 XT): forest +0.84 ms, tropical +0.76, meadow +0.49, city +0.62. That price is three things, taken apart by a throwaway build whose dial casts nothing into the map, with the ceiling glass's shadow (`glassshadow=`) a variant of its own (four variants in one process, 42 to 52 paired cycles, the totals within 0.03 ms of these; the meadow twice, the two runs within 0.02 ms): **the map's reads** (every receiver's taps, the terrain's among them) forest 0.41, tropical 0.38, meadow 0.29, city 0.24; **the casting**, everything drawn into the map (the island and the gun in every scene, with each scene's own: the forest's wood, the tropical's palms, rocks and bank, the meadow's planting, the city's towers) 0.25, 0.17, 0.11 and 0.29; and **the ceiling glass's analytic shadow**, which runs in the same receivers under the same gate and so comes and goes with the map, 0.19, 0.21, 0.10 and 0.11. Each step's sign held in at least 96 % of the cycles in the forest, the tropical and the meadow's second run, and in at least 87 % where another session's captures shared the GPU (the city, the meadow's first run). A cluster as a receiver, `Giraffe`'s 420 balls filling the frame under its dome 14 (`campos=0,6,44 camtarget=0,4,0`, `ballshadow=0` so the cluster casts nothing, 51 cycles a run): the map costs +0.54 with the level loaded and +0.51 in the same scene and dome with no level (the Testbed's default 10 x 10 x 16 field and its own ceiling fit, against `Giraffe`'s 17 x 17 x 18). Those are two processes, so the cluster's +0.03 is within what two runs of one pin differ by: as a receiver it costs next to nothing. ⚠ **The reads are most of it wherever there is ground; the casters lead only in the city**: everything cast in the forest costs 0.25 ms against 0.41 of reads, in the meadow 0.11 against 0.29, so the receiver's reads or the terrain branch are what to lower.

## The city's shadows

**Towers cast and receive; the street does not receive.** All towers cast (an off-screen tower's shadow falls on a facade in view). ⚠ **The street receiver was built and removed**: `CityStreets.fx` already darkens a canyon by an occupancy term at every sun height (#399); the map's tap answered a settled question, so `SetHostShadowScene` gets no `receivers` and `CityStreets.fx` does not include `Shadows.fxh`. Judge it from `campos=300,80,-300 camtarget=0,-40,0`, dome 8, `arena=none` (the play camera sits in a canyon with no cast shadow in frame).

## The ceiling's glass shadow (#553)

Computed **in every receiver** by `Shadows.fxh`'s `CeilingGlassShadow`, which `SunShadow` multiplies in (a transmittance map cannot say where the cut sends the light, so no caustic).

- **Analytic slab footprint:** each receiver carries its ray to the sun up to the axis-aligned slab's underside and top and tests both landings against `CutSlabMesh`'s rounded-rectangle outline (union, for a low sun entering the side). Read off the plate's renderer (`GlassHalfExtents`, `GlassCornerRadius`, `GlassCutPeriod`/`GlassCutSlope`, filled by `CeilingPlate.Fit` for the refraction), so shadow and glass cannot disagree.
- **Zones:** uncut glass takes `CeilingPlate.SHADOW_TAKE` (0.2) of the sun; the crown's rim is darkest (`GLASS_SHADOW_RIM`); the diamond field draws caustics (`GLASS_SHADOW_CAUSTIC`).
- ⚠ **It acts on the light the map lets through**: `1 - ShadowStrength x (1 - lit x glass)`; multiplying the factors drew the caustic lattice into the cluster's umbra.
- **Gated** inside the receivers' `ShadowStrength` branch and by `CeilingShadowCentre.w` (0 = no plate); **stated per frame**: `SceneRenderer.CastCeilingShadow(renderer, centre)` before `DrawShadowMaps` (Game: `BS3DGame.CastCeilingShadow` from `GameplayScreen.Draw` and `BackdropScreen.Draw`); a screen that stops drawing a plate stops shadowing silently. Testbed `glassshadow=0|1`. **Cost about +0.08 ms wherever a plate is stated**: the savanna (dome 14) and the meadow (dome 1) at the play vantage over `Maps/Full.json` (`campos=0,-4,30 camtarget=0,-8,0`, 1920x1080, ssaa 2, `nopost nooverc`, `sceneseed=7`), `alt=glassshadow=0;glassshadow=1` in one process, 62 and 63 paired cycles, the sign held in every one, 5900X / RX 6900 XT. At the fit's pin (the cost in "Which scenes, and the fit" above, no level loaded) it is 0.10 to 0.21 ms, the forest and the tropical the dearest. It is arithmetic in every receiver's sun term, so it is paid where the plate is out of view too. Not measured on the APU.

## Checking a shadow

- **`shadow=<0..1>`** pins `SceneRenderer.ShadowScale` over `Strength`; 0 is the same code path as `Strength` 0. Alternate it (`alt=shadow=0;shadow=1`): two worktrees of `main` return pairs differing in more than the thing under test. `detail=0` is not a substitute. Nothing in the Game writes `ShadowScale`. "Sweeping variants inside one process" in `docs/testbed.md`.
- ⚠ **Photograph a shadow under a sun that is up, and a low one to be sure.** At the savanna's dome 14 the terrain has the island's footprint cut out, so the island's shadow falls into the hole it casts from ("nothing happened" is geometry); a lower dome shows the ellipse. But a sun can be too low: a meadow capture at dome 5 drew no map (under `SHADOW_MIN_SUN_HEIGHT`). **Dome 8 (14 degrees) is the one to check a new scene with.**

# The forest

Part of [The scenes](../scenes.md).

A temperate woodland glade: a mossy, needle-strewn clearing whose hills dress in a dark conifer canopy, with procedural trees, rocks and stumps standing on it. `scene=forest`.

## What it is made of

- **Terrain**: `ForestBackdrop` / `Forest.fx` (`ForestSceneConfig`), the meadow's displaced-grid machinery. `SceneCatalog.IsSolidTerrainScene` lists it (the island's footprint is cut out, a dark pit shaft backs the drain's glass); one shared list, because two hand-kept copies once both omitted it.
- **The wood is shared**: `ForestScatter` decides where each piece stands, `ForestScatterRenderer` draws it, in all three executables. **The planting is the backdrop's** (`ForestBackdrop.Planting`, `SceneRenderer.ForestPlanting`, #962), as the aurora's is: every host hands it to its renderer (`planting:`), so what the backdrop dresses the wood with on High and Ultra hangs on the trees that are drawn. **The caller keeps the scene gate and the placement**: `Draw` touches no GPU state and draws whenever called, so each host wraps it in `if (scene == SceneKind.Forest)` and draws after the terrain, before the island. It passes `SceneRenderer.FineMeshes` to `Draw` and `DrawShadow`, which pick the spruces' crown and the planting a tier draws (`ForestScatter.ConifersAt` and the rest).
- **Trees**: a **spruce** of tiers of branch whorls (each a drooping `LatheMesh` skirt with a shadowed tuck: the sawtooth says spruce, a smooth cone says toy), a broadleaf crown of overlapping lobes, rocks and stumps; snags and logs (#462) on High and Ultra only. **On High and Ultra the spruce's crown is separate boughs** over a dark interior, laid out by the lathe's own whorls (#733), all growing out of one shaded bark stem that runs from the trunk's top into the leader (#960), and the boughs wear needles drawn on their plates (#751). It casts into the sun's map as drawn. See "The spruces on High and Ultra" in [The aurora](aurora.md); this wood plants no crown snow, so it draws no lumps. High and Ultra's wood is a section of its own below.
- **Each kind is built at several proportions AND its own structural seed**, because the eye counts silhouettes and a uniform scale or yaw does not change one. `ForestScatter` splits instances between variants (`ModelInstance[][]`, one instanced draw each, two per tree); foliage tints are `ForestScatterRenderer.CONIFER_TINTS`/`BROADLEAF_TINTS` (a table shorter than its variant array throws). ⚠ Two lathes sharing an edge must share `irregularityPhase`, or the seam opens.
  - Variety is in the **mesh, not a per-instance stretch**: normals go through the world matrix with no inverse transpose, so a squashed tree would shade as the shape it was authored at. A **rotation** is safe.
- **Planting** is the savanna's clumped generator through `TerrainMirror.Forest`. ⚠ `MathHelper.SmoothStep` is not GLSL `smoothstep` (value-interpolate, not clamp-then-hermite): it once scattered the first forest six thousand units into the sky.
- **`ScatterSpacing`** keeps plants apart (one rule with the savanna: packing fraction 0.72, a retry budget, a signed clearance test; nothing is dropped for want of room). ⚠ Changing it **re-rolls a scatter at an unchanged seed**.
- **The wood takes the dome's mood by its pigment** (#108): its red and blue were near zero, so a coloured light had nothing to multiply. `ApplySkyTint` moves each tint `SKY_TINT_STRENGTH` towards the dome's **hue only**, re-encoded only on a dome switch.

## Traps from the first build (black eggs on the ground)

- A crown spans `[trunkHeight, trunkHeight + crownHeight]`, not `+-h` about the base. Lathe profiles run top, outside, underside (what `LatheMesh` documents): bottom-up they were inside out, and a shallow inside-out dome even reads *lit*. A span whose first ring is on the axis got a `+Y` normal for the whole span: take the tangent from the ring that is off the axis.
- Config colours are linear radiance, the material-diffuse uniform is sRGB: encode once at the boundary (`ColorSpace.LinearToSrgb`); a tinted draw multiplies luminance by 1.25, so meshes are built at `SCATTER_MATERIAL_DIFFUSE`. A procedural-mesh renderer defaults to white specular (wet obsidian): pass matte `BasicEffectParams`, a **zero** specular meaning no override, so matte is a dim non-zero colour.

## The terrain

- **Two programs over one body**: `ForestFloor(input, detail)` is called with a literal from `ForestPS` and `ForestReducedPS` (MGFX cannot parse a uniform argument in a `compile` statement). `SceneRenderer.SceneDetail` selects; **Low drops the needle strands and the tree shadows**. ⚠ **`SceneDetail` must re-select the technique on write**: as a plain auto-property the reduced program shipped and **never drew at any tier** while looking correct (caught by making it flat red for one run).
- ⚠ **The floor's cost is occupancy, not work**: removing any one piece saved nothing, pairs saved a lot, and a `[branch]` on a uniform measured 2.72 against 2.70 ms (the register footprint is fixed at compile time). Compile a second program.
- **The base normal is per pixel** (three `TerrainHeight` taps; an interpolated normal leaves a Mach band at every cell edge). **Hills** are five domain-warped sine octaves summing to 1 (so `HillHeight` keeps its meaning).
- **The floor is litter, not lawn** (#281): band-limited noise relief (`Fbm2BandLimited`, `NeedleRelief`; summed plane waves were the lines and waves); rusty needle litter (`LitterColor`, `LitterColorDark`) with bare `EarthColor`, and on it moss (`ForestColor`/`ForestColorDark`, `MossCoverage`), bilberry (`UndergrowthColor`), dry grass (`DryGrassColor`) and needle strands near the lens. **Three fields draw all of it** (dampness, litter mottle, a fine one): a field per layer cost +0.64 to +1.04 ms.
- **The canopy is colour** (`TreelineColor`, its own ramp) on Low and Medium; High and Ultra stand the far stand on it (below). **Haze is two-stage** (`ForestHaze`, shared by the floor and the far stand) and reaches `HorizonColor` exactly at `HorizonHazeDistance`, ⚠ **which must stay at or inside the grid's half-extent** or the mesh edge shows against the dome.
- ⚠ **`CloudNoise` clusters around zero** (sigma 0.18): `* 0.5 + 0.5` spans 0.35-0.65 and ACES flattens it to one tone. Spread by about 1.6 and saturate before using it as a mottle, anywhere.
- **`ForestShadow` plants a *virtual* tree per hash-grid cell** (the shader cannot know where the real ones stand); density rides `canopyRamp`, zero in the clearing. ⚠ **It drew squares until #648**: a cylinder crown tested only in the cell the ray step landed in (cut flat at the cell's edge), height compared with world y, `CloudHash22` (-1..1) used as 0..1. Now a 3x3 block of cells round where the sun ray reaches an average crown's height, each crown a **sphere** shaded by its chord length, its size from the hash's fractional bits (equal crowns are polka dots). To isolate such an artefact: `scene=forest sceneseed=0 campos=0,30,60 camtarget=0,-14,0 fov=60`, `shadow=0`, then `ForestShadow` returning 1.0. The checkered rectangle on the island's top under a high sun is the ceiling glass's caustic shadow (#553), by design.

## High and Ultra's wood (#962)

The owner, of the wood on High and Ultra: the trees are "strange and unrealistic and too few", a "weak, sloppy video game". The references (`C:\Users\panrd\AI\sd\out\962-zimage` and `962-klein`, prompts `prompts-962.json`: a clearing's edge, an interior, the undergrowth, wooded hills, the canopy from below, a snag and a log, and img2img over the play camera and one inside the wood) agree: a wall of crowded trees of every age round the clearing, two to four times as tall as the gap; ridges jagged with tree tips to the horizon, spruce stands and lighter broadleaf stands in patches; ferns and fallen wood at the feet; broadleaf crowns of separate leaf clusters with the sky between. Everything here is `SceneRenderer.FineMeshes`; **Low and Medium draw exactly the wood they drew** (the same arrays, the same meshes, the same draws).

- **More trees, of every age** (`ForestTreeConfig.FineCount`, `SaplingCount`, `FineConiferFraction`, `FineMinRadius`/`FineMaxRadius`, `FineClusters`): 400 more grown trees from 54 to 290 out, gathered round 60 cluster centres (three times the base wood's, so they close the wall rather than piling into its groves), and 160 saplings at 0.16 to 0.42 of a tree's size, four in five of both spruces (the references' wall is mostly spruce, and a broadleaf in leaf is the dearest tree on the screen); 40 more boulders, 15 more stumps, 16 snags and 26 logs (`FineCount` on each kind). **Planted after everything else from the same stream** (`ForestScatter.FineConifers` and the rest: each variant's base instances first, the extras after), so nothing a lower tier draws moves, and the aurora, whose config asks for none, gets its own arrays back.
- **The broadleaves in leaf** (`ForestDressing`, `MeadowTreeMesh.ForestInLeaf`, #751's broadleaf half): the very tree Low and Medium draw with solid clumps, grown with the meadow's twigs and broad-leaf cards (#697, `Acacia.fx`'s `BroadLeafMask`) instead; the grown wood (#889) and the twigs within `ForestScatterRenderer.FINE_NEAR_RADIUS` of the arena, the plain wood past it. The host's renderer draws and casts none of the broadleaves there (`broadleavesInLeaf`); the leaves cast, dappled. `ForestInLeaf` builds the same wood and lumps as `ForForest` (the cards take a stream of their own). The bark is a beech grey taken warm (`ForestDressing.BARK`): ⚠ the plant material lights a surface with the dome's violet and teal at full strength, and a neutral grey came out lavender beside the spruces' brown trunks. The leaves are `LEAF_SHADE` (0.75) of the solid crowns' colour.
- **Ferns** (`FernClump`): clumps of 9 to 14 arching fronds (`LeafMask`, a pinnate frond) in 80 patches over the floor, one or two at the feet of half the trees out to 150, and 14 clumps of 4 to 8 on the island's bank against the drum (#983, standing on `IslandBerm.SurfaceY`, the bank's own surface), casting nothing. ⚠ Wound clockwise seen from the face their normal is on, like every card: the plant material turns a card's normal by the face it sees, and wound the other way a frond seen from above shaded as though it faced the ground (`WindingCheck` reports it).
- **The far stand** (`ForestFarStand`, `ForestFarStandConfig`, `Forest.fx`'s `ForestStand`): the hills past the planted wood, from 270 to 560 out, on a jittered grid (6.5 units inside 420, 8 past it), open where a broad field says glade, never in a cell the planted wood stands in, in spruce and broadleaf patches a grove or two across (`BroadleafShare` 0.4). Spires are `AuroraStandMesh` (a broad one of five tiers and a narrow one of six, three each past 420); broadleaves `ForestCrownStandMesh` (a lobed dome on a stem, two rolls); eight instanced draws after the floor, lit and hazed as the floor under them (`ForestHaze`), casting nothing. ⚠ **Past about 560 the haze carries a tree to the dome's teal skirt**, and teal spires against the violet sky read as cut-outs: the stand stops there and the farthest ridges stay the hills' own colour. ⚠ Its colours are the canopy colour's (`SpruceColor` near `TreelineColor`): at twice that the stand read as lit plastic over the dark floor.
## The sky and the fireflies (#961)

- **The forest brings its own dome, 15** (`SkyDome.SceneDefault`, so the Game's scene picker and the Testbed's `scene=forest` enter under it unless `sky=` says otherwise; the Game's Sky setting does not override it, as for every scene that states a dome). It is the late twilight (sun 11 degrees, so the canopy still throws its shadows): an indigo sky over a teal skirt, the hills going blue-black and the broadleaf crowns keeping their green. Before it the forest kept whatever dome was up, usually the Game's 13, a flat saturated teal (57 % of the upper sky bright and saturated at the play camera, 31 % under 15). Judged on a sweep of all twenty at the play camera; the choice is among the domes that keep the sun above the shadow map's floor (4 degrees puts 7 and 16 out) and are not red or pink. **Dome 9** (the volcano's blue hour) is the quieter runner-up, with no bright saturated sky at all, but its hills went the brown of bare earth, which a conifer wood must not.
- ⚠ **The weather decides how bright the sky is, not the dome.** Under the Broken deck the upper sky's mean (the top third of the frame outside the cluster, play camera, `nopost`) is 90 under dome 13 and 89 under dome 15: the deck's lit side takes the sun's light and is the brightest thing over a dark wood whichever dome it hangs under, and every dusk dome but the red and pink ones measured 80 to 90 under it. So the forest is **Overcast** (`ForestSceneConfig`): a lid with the teal skirt under it, mean 55 and nothing bright and saturated, the hills' conifer green showing, and no cloud shadows sweeping the floor. `weather=broken` on the Testbed brings the old deck back to compare.
- **The fireflies are a swarm of twelve black bodies that show only as the light they give** (`ForestFireflies`, `ForestFireflyConfig`; the three hosts draw them whenever the scene is the forest). Each is rolled its own period (2.4 to 4.5 s), phase and slow wander (1.6 units sideways, 0.4 up and down, one circuit in 16 to 42 s) from the scene's seed, hovers 1.2 above the ground 32 to 72 units from the origin, glows for 0.4 of its period (one to two seconds) on a **squared sine**, and is carried to a brightness of 10 over the glare threshold, which is what makes the halo: the bloom's energy goes with how far over the threshold the body is, and a body of a few pixels has no halo of its own.
- ⚠ **The body must stay black.** A lit body (the first build's green sphere with an ambient term) shows as a hard olive disc the moment the sine leaves zero and again as the glow ends: the owner's "something flickers on the ground and I cannot tell what" was that, measured (a six-pixel hard dot appearing, swelling to white in a third of a second and vanishing, once in three seconds on the far floor). The ambient is zero, the diffuse black and the specular dim but **non-zero** (zero means "no override", which is the renderer's white specular). A glow under `MIN_SWELL` is not drawn.
- **The floor itself is steady.** With the camera pinned the floor's block means drift slowly and monotonically (the cloud shadows' sweep, measured under the old Broken deck: up to 8 levels over 23 s, 3 under `weather=clear`; the overcast lid has none to sweep), and a 0.03-unit camera nudge changes about 1 % of the floor's pixels by more than 4 levels (edges, parallax), so it does not shimmer under motion. A report of a flicker here is the fireflies unless a capture of the floor says otherwise.
- **Photographing one**: the fireflies come from `ForestScatterRenderer.DEFAULT_SEED`, so the same `scene=forest sceneseed=0` always plants the same ones, and `campos=-32.8,-6,35 camtarget=-32.8,-13.3,-31` has one in the middle of the frame. ⚠ Consecutive `shotframe=` shots at 1920 x 1080 are about 0.48 s apart on the scene clock (the readback is on the render thread), so a glow of one to two seconds shows in two to four frames.
- **Cost**: about 4.5 microseconds of CPU a lit firefly (about five lit at a time: 20 to 30 microseconds a frame, Testbed, 1920 x 1080), one draw each; nothing is drawn for a firefly in its dark part.

# The Grid

Part of [The scenes](../scenes.md).

The twentieth `SceneKind` (#393): an early-1980s computer-graphics backdrop drawn from **named mathematics rather than noise**, flat-shaded, cheap because of that. Made of `GridBackdrop`, `Grid.fx`, `GridSceneConfig` (groups Terrain, Towers, Landmark, Lighting) and `GridLife`. Launch: `scene=grid` (`tron` parses the same). It has a chapter since #420.

**In both families, after the Moon and the aurora**: `IsSolidTerrainScene` (flat floor, footprint cut out, pit shaft) and `ReplacesSky` (no dome, black clear, its own rig through `TryGetLightRig`); see "The sky-replacing family" in [Space](space.md).

## Rules and traps

- **⚠ Pin `sceneseed=` for any A/B**, or it is two different scenes (the Testbed re-seeds per run); `sceneseed=0` is the shipped arrangement. **⚠ `fpscap=400` measures nothing here** (both halves sat at the cap): use `fpscap=2000`.
- **Light comes only from the seams**: a near-black body lit by its own bright seams (grid lines, the Hilbert trace, every solid's edges), fed to the same bloom (`GLARE_THRESHOLD`). The model is TRON's backlit compositing; the solids are plain `BoxMesh` prisms.
- **Never an `atan2`/azimuth construction** (spirals, rings): an unwrapped angle jumps at its wrap line (Mars's gullies, the aurora's curtains).
- **Backdrop-only**: balls, island and gun stay on the ordinary lit path, tinted by `GridLightingConfig`'s cool rig; there is no emissive shading mode. No seventh `SceneLights` branch and no `TryGetSceneEvent`. The viewpoint is low and close (overhead flattens the pattern to a swatch). The ambience is an electrical hum (explicit `case SceneKind.Grid` in `ProceduralAmbience.BakeScene`).

## The floor

- **It is genuinely flat**: `TerrainHeight` is the constant `GridLevelY`, the normal always up, so the mesh is coarse (`GRID_MESH_N` over `GRID_EXTENT`) and there is **nothing to reduce**: no reduced-tier technique. The sky is `VoidColor` and a dither (no `Stars.fxh`), not exactly zero (a frame at zero reads as a hole).
- **A Hilbert curve traced along a plain grid.** `GridHilbertIndex` is `xy2d` unrolled over `GRID_HILBERT_BITS` (a 64-node tile). Nodes are lattice **vertices** on every `TraceStride`-th line, and a segment is lit where its two end nodes are **consecutive** along the curve: three index lookups a pixel.
  - **⚠ Light the segment, not the cell boundary**: lighting the boundary between consecutive cells, which the curve crosses at right angles, gave a ladder of rungs and thousands of dead ends. A trace is widened (`AccentWidthScale`), or it is invisible from play distance.
  - **The tiles chain**: lighting the extra link `GRID_HILBERT_LAST` to 0 makes each row of tiles one infinite curve (every node exactly two lit segments, no seam). `GridMod` is a bitwise AND, valid only for a power-of-two tile.
- **Lines are filtered per axis**: one isotropic `fwidth` smeared lines running away from the camera into a bright band. `GridAxisFootprint` measures each axis; `GridLineMask` never draws a line thinner than a pixel but dims it by the share it covers, fading to average coverage once a pixel spans half the spacing. The solids' seams use it too.

## The solids

- **Placement.** `GridTowerConfig`: `Count` solids on a ring (`RadiusMin`/`RadiusMax`), drawn from `Seed` and the launch's roll (`sceneseed=`) by `GridLayout.PlaceSolids`, the one placement every host draws (`BuildGridTowers`, the Potato path, and the light cycles that ride clear of them); it retries up to twenty times. `CubeFraction` are large near-cubes (so a face shows most of a 32x32 board), the rest towers: a tall narrow prism reads as a building. Every solid has a roof (free cameras look down).
- **⚠ Keep clear of the landmark ring as a hard rule.** The ring is built after the solids, so four seeds in ten put a solid on it. `GridLayout.PlaceSolids` asks `GridLayout.Landmark` (what `BuildGridLandmark` builds from) and rejects draws within a 15-unit gap of the ring's footprint (`GridRing.FootprintDistance`), up to 200 draws.
- **⚠ A solid needs seams, or it is a pattern floating in the dark**: `BodyColor` is within a few codes of the void, so every edge of every face glows (`EdgeWidth`, `EdgeColor` past the glare threshold; each vertex carries its face-local position and size, `FaceLocal`).
- **⚠ One value a face, or a cube is a wireframe, not a volume**: `GridTowerVertex` carries the outward normal and the body is `BodyColor * (floor + (1 - floor) * N.L)` against `FaceLight`, the floor well above zero so a face never vanishes into the void.
- **Life on the windows.** `GridLife` (`SIZE` 32; a row is one `uint`, so a generation is carry-save addition of shifted rows, `StepRows`, bit-exact, zero allocation). Each board ticks every `LifeStepInterval` on a staggered phase.
  - **⚠ Named patterns, not random soup**: soup on a 32x32 torus settles stale and random flips wreck oscillators. Each board is stamped, centred, from a deck (convoys, pentadecathlons, pulsars, Kok's galaxy, methuselahs, glider fleets), turned so ships travel up or down a tower. Nothing in a deck has period 1 or 2.
  - **A board moves on rather than being stirred**: stale for four generations, it takes its next pattern; one also yields after `LifePatternGenerations`. Boards are seeded from the placement stream.
  - **The board wraps round the solid like a label**; ⚠ **centre on the pattern, not the board** (an odd-width pattern sat half a pane aside): `GridLife.CentreX`/`CentreY` report where it landed and `GridLifeCentreOffset`, pushed per draw, corrects it. A cell alive only a generation ago fades as `exp(-age / PhosphorDecay)` (`GridLifeAge` per draw).
  - **One shared buffer, one draw call per solid** (`_gridTowerRanges`): only `GridLifeTexture` and `GridLifeAge` change between calls, which is what forces a call per solid.

## The landmark ring

`GridLandmarkConfig`: a ring on edge, far out, with its own Life round its band: the one silhouette nothing else here has (every other solid is a box). A faceted torus of `Segments` trapezoid segments, facing the arena. **⚠ Segment joins must not glow, or the ring is a barrel of ribs**: the seam shader lights all four borders of a quad, so each quad reports a face-local X pinned mid-way across a wide face and only the long borders draw, as two rails.

## The light cycles (#956)

The owner, "for fun": a few light cycles as a homage to the film, in its own render style. `LightCycles` (the cycles drawn by `GridBackdrop` after the void's quad, their walls after the island, below) rides three along the floor's lattice (`LATTICE` 16, the trace's stride) in right-angled runs, each trailing a wall of light; references in `C:\Users\panrd\AI\sd\out\956-zimage` and `956-klein`.

- **Where they ride**: in a ring `RING_MIN` 56 to `RING_MAX` 124 round the axis, clear of the island's hole and out among the solids' inner edge: a solid's footprint is centred from `GridTowerConfig.RadiusMin` 140 out, and in nearly half the layouts a solid's footprint reaches in among the cycles (the largest cube, 75 with its jitter to 79.5, to 100 square on an axis and to 84 on a diagonal). So every segment of a run keeps `SOLID_CLEARANCE` (the body's `BODY_REACH` and 3 units of floor, 6.85) off every solid's footprint seen from above, and neither a cycle nor its wall ever enters one (#1136).
  - **`LightCycles.Lattice`** says which steps of the lattice a rider may take: *open* where the step stays in the ring and clear of every solid, *live* for the run it is taken in (one to `MIN_RUN` segments straight on since the last turn) where it is open and a live step that is not back follows it, straight on making the run a segment longer and a turn, which only a run `MIN_RUN` long may take, starting it again at one; found once per layout by striking out every open step with nothing live after it until none is left. At every node a rider takes the move its hash rolls (by its seed and the node's place in its run, below) where its run may take that step and it is live, else the other two in turn, so it never turns back, never turns short of `MIN_RUN`, never leaves the ring and never rides into a solid. ⚠ **A step is taken only with a way on after it**: the first step clear of the solids, taken with no look past it, turned the riders round 178 times over 205 layouts' 25 minutes; the live set holds the way on however far it is followed, not only for the next step.
  - **Long straight runs** (#1162, the owner: they turned too often): a rider goes straight on until it has ridden `MIN_RUN` (2) segments since its last turn, and past that straight on at `STRAIGHT_SHARE` 0.75, else a right angle the way one bit of its hash says. It turns every 2.6 s on average and never within 1.33 s of its last turn; the longest run is 12 segments, the longest chord of the ring, and the ring's edge forcing a turn is why the mean is under the 3.3 s the share alone would give. ⚠ **The lattice holds the shortest run, not only the picker** (its live steps, above): with the rule in the picker alone, the ring's edge still forced a turn a node after a turn (12 % of the runs) and among the solids the riders turned round 21 times. At 0.6 straight on with no shortest run they turned every 1.4 s, and 46 % of the runs were a single segment.
  - The run is a function of the rider's seed, the solids' layout and the wall clock: the same at any frame rate, and where a rider is, is the run's point at `SPEED` times the wall clock. ⚠ **The layout moves with the launch's roll, and the runs with it**: a host building runs of its own builds them on the solids the renderer stands (`SceneRenderer.GridSolids`), or it follows runs nobody draws. No allocation a frame (a fixed ring of the last nodes, one array for the walls, the lattice built with the backdrop).
  - `LightCycleRouteTests`, over 205 layouts (`sceneseed` 0-199 and five from the ends of an int, 98 with a solid's footprint inside the ring) and the first 25 minutes of every run: no body nearer a footprint than 3.01 and no wall nearer than 6.86, no reversal, never out of the ring, and one run whether asked every frame, in jumps back and forth or through `LightCycleRuns`; over those runs and 200 riders on the bare ring, 3.9 segments between turns on average and never fewer than 2.
- **The look** (`LightCycle.fx`), the scene's rule that light comes only from the seams: a near-black body of the film's primitives (three boxes and two disc wheels), one flat value a face, every edge glowing in the rider's colour past the glare threshold, the wheels ringed at the rim and the hub (each vertex carries its face-local seam data). ## The Potato path

The Raspberry Pi's renderer (`PotatoBackdrop.Grid`, #829, #1044) draws the floor as a plane in its own dark body with a trace of the lines' light, and the lattice as thin boxes radiating the line colour, every `TraceStride`-th one bolder in place of the Hilbert trace. Since #1044:

- **The solids** are the desktop's, where it stands them (`GridLayout.PlaceSolids`): near-black bodies, flat-shaded a face at a time by the rig, each edge glowing in `EdgeColor` as a thin box centred on it, 1.4 times `EdgeWidth` so it holds a pixel from the gun as the floor's lines do. No Life on their faces. ⚠ A vertex-lit piece's glow is added whatever its albedo: at 0.04 of the windows' light the bodies were mid-teal blocks, and at a fifth of the way to the line colour with a quarter of its light the floor's cells were teal.
- **The landmark ring** stands where `GridLayout.Landmark` puts it, its segments dark boxes and its four long borders glowing rails. The solids keep off its footprint, as the desktop's do (#559).
- **The light cycles** are the desktop's three riders on their own seeds among the same solids, so the same runs (`LightCycles.Rider`, `PotatoLightCycles`). Each cycle is `CycleBody`'s parts as boxes and two flattened spheres for the disc wheels, following its run every frame. Its wall goes through `PotatoModel.fx`'s `PotatoTrail`, added after the island as `LightCycle.fx`'s is, brightest along its top and foot and fading to its tail.
- Cost, Cantor off screen against main: 9.85 ms at native against 9.62, 7.81 at 720p against 7.64.

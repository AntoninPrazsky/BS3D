# The savanna's trees and plain

Part of [The scenes](../scenes.md).

What is planted on the savanna ground ([The savanna](savanna.md)): the acacias, the baobabs and doum palms, and the dressing between them. A savanna's character is in what is scattered across it, so these are real 3D geometry on the shared instancing path, not billboards. The ring of fires and their hearth stones are in [The savanna's campfires](savanna-fires.md).

## How the plain is built

- **`SavannaScatter`** owns where and what (the forest's split; the effect and the frame's light stay the renderer's). It builds everything **once**, off one seed and one occupancy list, into `ScatterBucket`s: one instanced draw each, with **the instances uploaded once at build** rather than re-uploaded on every draw. The whole plain is some thirty draws. The hearth stones keep the dynamic path (they are drawn per fire with that fire's light).
- Plants stand in a ring clear of the island (`AcaciaConfig.MinRadius`/`MaxRadius`; the inner edge is 60 so wider crowns do not hang over the play camera's shoulder), **82 % around cluster centres** (`Clusters`; a centre no plant reaches is not a grove), on the terrain (`TerrainMirror.Savanna`), kept apart by `ScatterSpacing`'s rule (one copy with the forest's). Drawn opaque and depth-writing after the terrain, before the birds.
- **The plain is re-rolled on every `sceneseed`**, as every change to what is planted is. The volume rule (#653) holds: counted over `sceneseed` 0 to 4, 0 overlapping pairs on every seed.
- Everything on the plain casts and receives sun shadows (see [Shadows](savanna.md#shadows)).

## The acacia

`Acacia.fx`, `AcaciaMesh`; shaded from the scene's own sun and dome (`dot(N, SunDirection)` plus the zenith-to-horizon ambient off the normal), so a tree sits in the savanna's light. They were camera-facing billboards with a `clip()`ed silhouette until #202, which read as a paper cutout: one flat shape however the camera moved. **Do not go back to billboards.**

- **Four kinds** (`AcaciaKind`): mature (about half carry a second, smaller tier offset to one side, with sky between the layers), broken (one off-centre tier and a bare splintered spar), young (slender, forking high under one small crown) and dead (no foliage, boughs branching on into twigs, bleached `DeadwoodColor`). Shares are `AcaciaConfig.YoungFraction`, `DeadFraction`, `BrokenFraction`. Nine tree meshes, each at rolled proportions and its own structural seed, and each in two woods: the tier's (see [The fork](#the-fork-grown-for-high-and-ultra-758)).
- **The umbrella is a `FoliageStyle.Tier`**: a low dome over a nearly flat underside (`TopFlatten`, `BottomFlatten`; the tuck alone cannot give a floor, it narrows the lower half to a cone), thin at the rim and thickest in the middle. The old crown, a dome flattened a little on top, is a bush on a stick. Several tiers go into **one mesh** (`FoliageMesh.Generate` appends to a caller's lists), so a two-tier tree is one canopy draw. The style also serves the bushes of Low and Medium (`Crown`; High and Ultra draw [the thorn bush](#the-bush-883)), scrub and treeline (`Scrub`).
- **The boughs fork twice**: from each bough's middle two thinner limbs splay to the rim, the spokes an umbrella shows under its crown. One bough per spoke reads as a stick under a lid.
- **The canopy's leaf mottle is `Fbm3` of world position**, so it has no seam and does not swim as the canopy turns with its tree; the trunk takes none.

### Leaf sprays (#610) and the wood they grow from (#782)

- `AcaciaMesh.Leaves` lays each foliage tier as `LeafSprays`: flat cards in three layers (`LAYERS`: dense on top, thinner under, a ragged fringe beneath that droops at the rim), about 750 a tier. `Acacia.fx` cuts eleven pairs of leaflets and the rachis out of each card (`LeafMask`) and turns them into the spray's plain outline once a pixel is wider than half a leaflet, so far crowns do not sparkle. It lights both sides, wraps the sun a little, darkens the lower layers' ambient (`LAYER_AMBIENT_FALL`) and lets the sun through when the lens looks towards it (`TRANSMISSION_*`), only where the shadow map says the sun reaches. The cards cast with their leaflets cut out, so shade is dappled.
- **Buckets**: the plate is `LowOnly`, the sprays `DetailOnly`; the Game's Low tier keeps the plate and the mottle, Medium and up draw the sprays. Cost: the play camera 2.61 to 2.79 ms (desktop, Testbed, `sceneseed=3`, `nopost`, 1600 x 900 ssaa 2, `fpscap=400`). The laptop is not measured.
- ⚠ **Every spray grows from wood.** Uniformly laid sprays measured **58 to 90 % of cards clear of any wood, the worst 3 to 8 card lengths off**: leaves in the air. `LeafSprays.HangOnTwigs`: twigs grow from the wood under the disc (an acacia tier's supports are the ends of the boughs and limbs that carry it, recorded by `BuildWood`; a baobab tuft's is its twig end) to points spread over the three layers, and the sprays hang along each twig's outer 70 % with every stem (the card's `u` = 0 edge, where the mask starts) on it. The layers keep their spray counts; an acacia twig carries 8 sprays, a baobab twiglet 24. The twigs go into the wood mesh, whose 16-bit indices they stay well inside (the test guards that too, #670's trap).
- ⚠ **Every spray points along its twig** (#882). `HangOnTwigs` takes a card's heading from the twig's tangent at its stem (the curve's derivative at `f`, seen from above) and leans it `LEAN` to `LEAN + LEAN_SPREAD` (0.8 to 1.1) of the forward run, 38 to 48 degrees, to alternate sides, the last card on a twig carrying straight on (`LAST_LEAN`; the meadow's rule, #697). It follows half of the twig's climb (`TWIG_CLIMB`, at most `MAX_CLIMB`, 26 degrees), droops with its distance from the centre (a quarter of its share of the radius) and tilts a little at random, so the tier stays thin. A twig standing on end (a horizontal run under `MIN_HEADING` of its length) has no heading and points its cards out of the tier's centre, rising as steeply as a spray may (`MAX_CLIMB`). The trap: pointing every card out of the tier's centre whatever its twig does hangs the cards of a twig that ends nearer the centre than the bough it grows from backwards, against their wood. Measured on seven acacias, **40 to 65 % of the cards pointed back along their twig, the median 71 to 111 degrees off its tangent**; the baobab's tufts, whose one support is at the centre so twig and card both run out, 0.1 %. Along the twig it is **none, on all nine trees** (a twig standing on end carries cards that rise out of the tier's centre as steeply as a spray may, which is what keeps them off the wrong side of its tangent), the median 44 to 45 degrees, and the steepest tenth of the acacias' cards 21 to 25 degrees off the horizontal (a twig climbs more under a thicker tier, #884). The rolls are taken in the same order whatever the heading, so no twig, stem or card count moves (636 cards for a one-tier acacia, 1272 for a two-tier one, 19 716 and 21 624 for the two test baobabs), and the frame costs what it did: the same savanna drawn by a build with the old heading and one with the new, alternated four times each at three fixed cameras, the run medians differ by at most 0.02 ms (a count of cards does not set the cost alone, the angle they lie at moves overdraw and the shadow pass, so this was measured). `LeafAttachmentTests` fails above 3 % past a right angle and when the steepest tenth tilts past 30 degrees.
- Both trees are built by a static `Build` that returns the lists and a **skeleton** (every tube as the segment it was swept along) with no device, so `LeafAttachmentTests` holds every card's stem within its own length of the wood and its direction to its twig's. The meadow's crowns are built this way (#697); the forest's and the aurora's conifers and the tropical palms are **still to be held to the same test**. Frame cost 3.06 ms (desktop, Testbed Release, `scene=savanna sceneseed=3`, fixed camera `campos=40,8,30` at the default target, the arena, `nopost`, 1600 x 900 x 2, `fpscap=1000`; aimed at `0,6,0` 2.92 ms, at `70,4,0` 2.69 ms).

### The fork, grown for High and Ultra (#758)

The owner's playtest note: where the trunk turns into boughs "it does not fit: not smooth like on real trees", and on High and Ultra it should have more detail. The coarse wood is a trunk tube cut flat at the fork with every bough's tube starting at its centre: tubes poking out of a cylinder's cut end.

- **Two woods per tree mesh.** `AcaciaMesh.Wood` is the coarse wood, seven-sided, for Low, Medium and the Potato path. `AcaciaMesh.FineWood` is the grown fork for High and Ultra (`SceneRenderer.FineMeshes`, set from `QualityPreset.FineMeshes`; the Testbed's `finemeshes=0`). They are two buckets on the same instances (`ScatterBucket.FineMeshes`). The savanna's draw and its shadow casters each take the tier's one, or a tree casts twice.
- **The trunk becomes the boughs** (`GrownTrunk`). From `CROTCH_HEIGHT` fork radii under the fork it eases out to `CROTCH_SWELL` and takes the shape of the boughs' root circles together. Each circle is as thick as its bough, with its outer side on the swollen circle, so a groove runs down the trunk from every saddle: the references' trunk of fused limbs. A low dome (`CROTCH_DOME`) closes the top inside the boughs. Its saddles dip between neighbours (`SADDLE_DIP`) and fade to a single apex.
- **Each bough roots just inside the trunk**, `BOUGH_ROOT_DEPTH` fork radii under the fork. It leaves along the trunk's own surface, then turns on one Bezier through the coarse bough's middle to the coarse bough's end. The ends are the coarse wood's, so one set of crown twigs and leaves hangs from either wood. Limbs and a dead tree's twigs leave their bough the same way: from inside it, along its axis. Each root is one bent sweep, where the coarse wood leaves two open tube ends at the knee. Sides: 20 on the trunk, 12 on a bough or spar, 8 on a limb, twig or root.
- ⚠ **Traps found while building it**, each from a capture:
  - A trunk only swollen and lobed, with the boughs leaving through its side, left the lobes' rim standing round the boughs as a lip.
  - A sweep's first ring stands square to its first chord, which already leans with the turn. Its normals pointed 18 to 23 degrees down at the fork against the trunk's 7 up, so the boughs shaded a tenth lighter than the trunk under them. The first `BOUGH_BLENDED_RINGS` rings take the trunk's normals, fading into the bough's own.
  - Rooted on the trunk's top ring, the bough left a band of the dome's rim showing. Rooted just under it, the bough is outside the top ring by the fork.
  - A saddle dip carried to the dome's top left its last ring as a line of points up the axis, a seam of open slivers.
  - **The bark stays world-space.** Bark laid along each bough was built and photographed. It drew a seam at the fork, because the trunk and every bough sampled unrelated stretches of the field where they meet.
- `AcaciaForkTests` holds the fork. Near the fork, no open edge wider than a twig ring may stand outside the trunk, and the same check must find the coarse wood's cut end. Every crown twig must root on the fine wood. The fine wood must stay within 16-bit indices.
- **Size and cost.** A fine wood is 2 600 to 5 300 triangles against the coarse 1 700 to 3 200. A dead tree's is 2 600 against 340. It costs **+0.06 ms** a frame at High and at Ultra. Measured on the reference desktop in the Testbed, `alt=finemeshes=0;finemeshes=1` in one process, 3840 x 1600, `ssaa=2`, `sceneseed=0`, `nopost`, 42 paired cycles, the sign held in 98 to 100 % of them. At the play camera on Heart (savanna, 364 balls) the frame is 10.67 ms at High and 10.75 at Ultra (`shadowmap=8192`). From 30 units off a mature grove it is 9.20 and 9.37, and the difference +0.06 and +0.07. The Game on Zebra agrees: 3840 x 1581 windowed, `fpscap=1000`, `sceneseed=0 seed=1`, the two builds alternated twice, the minimum of the run medians. High goes 11.47 to 11.56 ms and Ultra 11.64 to 11.70.

### Bark, colour and haze

- **Per-instance colour in the shader**: `Acacia.fx` reads the instance's `Custom` vector (TEXCOORD5): `x` leans the draw's `DiffuseColor` towards its `DiffuseDry` (a per-plant dryness), `y` lifts or lowers brightness by up to 12 %, so one variant is several shades.
- **`BarkStrength`** ridges the wood with a two-octave `Fbm3` stretched up the trunk (zero on foliage and stone; the termite mounds take it for their flutes). The bark weathers: lichen in patches (`LICHEN_*`, scaled by `BarkStrength`; ⚠ a grey-green only a little paler than the bark, since at 0.36 it photographed as snow on the trunks) and the wood darker over the last `FOOT_HEIGHT` units above the root (`FOOT_DARKEN`).
- ⚠ **A plant hazes.** `HorizonHazeDistance` is handed over so a far tree fades towards the skyline as the ground under it does; without it the far scatter stood in full colour on a hazed hillside and read as pasted on. It is stretched by `PLANT_HAZE_REACH` (1.5): at the ground's own distance the treeline came back as pale lumps on a pale hill, and the references keep a far wooded edge darker than the field.

## The size of the trees (#663)

`AcaciaConfig.TreeSize` (1.7) scales every kind of acacia tree, not the bushes; `BaobabHeight` is 24 and `DoumPalmHeight` 18. From the play camera (`level=Zebra`) the umbrellas on the skyline were smaller than the campfires' flames in front of them. References put a mature acacia at about a third of a forty-metre platform's width: about 15 units tall and 20 across against the island's 52, its flat crown much wider than tall, the baobab near twice its height, the doums over it.

- **The plain stays as open as it was**: the trees went from 98 to 55 (`AcaciaConfig.Count` 97 with `BushFraction` 0.433, so the same 42 bushes), about 208 plants on a seed where there were 251.
- The prologue's shots aim at the plants' own figures and needed no change. Cheaper for the fewer trees: Game, `level=Zebra`, 1920 x 1080, `quality=high`, `nocap`, 201.9 / 202.5 to 205.7 / 205.7 FPS.

## The crown's thickness (#884)

The owner, on the savanna: some crowns are "far too flat ... I would stretch the crowns up a little". Radius (6 x `TreeSize` x w) and height (9 x `TreeSize` x h) are rolled apart in `SavannaScatter` (w 0.8 to 1.25, h 0.85 to 1.25), and a tier's half-thickness used to be a fixed share of the height whatever the roll, so a mature tier's radius ran from 3.0 to 7.0 times its half-thickness.

- **One thickness per kind, and a cap.** `AcaciaMesh`'s `MATURE_TIER`, `SECOND_TIER`, `YOUNG_TIER` and `BROKEN_TIER` (`TierProfile`: where the tier was centred, how thick it was half-through at #663, how thick it is now, all shares of the tree's height) hold the half-thickness: mature 0.14 to **0.21**, a mature tree's second tier 0.10 to **0.12**, young 0.11 to **0.155**, broken 0.13 to **0.18**. `MAX_TIER_ASPECT` (**3.8**) thickens the wide-short end of the roll until a tier's radius is at most 3.8 half-thicknesses, and leaves the narrow-tall end alone. Both are the numbers to change to stretch the crowns more or less.
- **Upward only, by the nominal figures.** The *nominal* underside (the centre less the half-thickness, the volume slab's bottom the planting keeps apart by), the radius, the trunk and the fork are where #663 left them: the centre rises by what the tier grew (call it g) and the nominal top by 1.6 g (a plate's top stands 0.6 of its half-thickness over its centre). The drawn plate is not the slab: `FoliageStyle.Tier` flattens its underside (`BottomFlatten` 0.75) and the rim's lobes swell it, so its lowest point stands only 0.40 to 0.45 of the half-thickness under its centre, and **the drawn underside rises with the tier by 0.55 to 0.60 g** (0.3 to 0.9 units over the savanna's rolls, 0.6 for a mature tree at the middle of it) while the drawn top rises by 1.7 to 1.9 g. The slab's underside, always the lower, is the conservative one for the planting. A second tier stands on the first and rides up with the first's nominal top, which leaves the drawn sky between the two within a third of a unit of what it was (the lowest point of the upper plate over the highest of the lower one moves by -0.36 to +0.10 units over eight two-tier meshes and the roll: the second tier's own growth, 0.3 to 0.4 units, lifts its underside by 0.6 of that, and the first's drawn top rises 0.1 to 0.3 g more than the 1.6 g the second rode up by). Every share the leaves and the bough ends hang from (`LAYERS`, the ends' 0.2 to 0.6) is a share of the half-thickness and follows it, so the boughs' ends rise by 0.4 to 0.8 of what the tier grew. A one-tier mature crown now reaches 1.01 to 1.06 of the tree's height (0.88 to 0.92 before), a two-tier one 1.16 to 1.18 (1.02 to 1.03).
- ⚠ **The ratio that reads is not the nominal one.** A tier is a lobed ellipsoid flattened above and below (`FoliageStyle.Tier`), so its plate is about 1.4 times wider than twice its radius (the rim's lobes) and its three layers of sprays hang over about 1.2 half-thicknesses. The middle of the roll's nominal 4.8 is about 11 wide to thick as drawn. Measured over the twenty mature meshes of `sceneseed` 0 to 4 (the leaf cloud's width over its 5th to 95th percentile of height, one-tier trees): nominal radius over half-thickness 3.3 to 6.5 before and **2.2 to 3.8** now; as drawn 8.4 to 14.3 before and **6.0 to 9.1** now. The references' crowns, 2.5 to 3 times wider than thick, are well past "a little": the crown is still an umbrella over visible boughs.
- **Nothing is added.** Vertex, triangle, card and twig counts are the same on every kind (a checksum over all of them is identical before and after): the sprays follow the disc's area, not its thickness. What a taller crown costs is overdraw: the savanna's frame at the fixed camera (desktop, Testbed Release, `scene=savanna sceneseed=3`, `campos=40,8,30` at the default target, `nopost`, 3840 x 1600, `ssaa=2`, `fpscap=1000`, origin/main and this build alternated three times, the run medians) is 10.82, 10.86 and 10.86 ms before and 10.88, 10.88 and 10.90 after, +0.02 to +0.06 ms a cycle (0.4 %).
- **The volume rule (#653) holds.** Counted over `sceneseed` 0 to 4, 0 overlapping pairs on every seed (208 or 209 volumes each). The planting is the plain's: of the 275 trees on the five seeds one stands elsewhere.
- **The Potato path** draws a canopy of 960 indices (one tier) as itself, so it stretches there; a two-tier canopy (1 920) is over `PROXY_OVER_VERTICES` and is the ellipsoid of its bounding sphere. `CanopyBounds` is not changed, so that blob is not (read from `PotatoBackdrop.AddScatter`, not photographed).
- `AcaciaCrownTests` hold it device-free: the cap on every tier over the roll, the nominal underside where #663 left it (and the second tier on the first's nominal top), the drawn plate's lowest point 0.3 to 0.55 of its half-thickness under its centre and above the slab, no tier thinner than it was and the middle of the roll thicker, the width unchanged.

## The bush (#883)

The owner, on the savanna: the green balls between the trees "are like giant green crumpled melons ... on High/Ultra this flora should be more detailed". The bushes were two `FoliageMesh` in `FoliageStyle.Crown`: a 16 x 11 sphere pushed into lobes, 320 triangles, the sphere's normals, nothing inside its silhouette.

- **The melon is the bush, not the scrub.** At the play camera (Game, `level=Zebra`, `sceneseed=3`, `quality=high`), a scratch build of origin/main that skips one bucket: without the bushes the big pale olive lobed domes are gone and only the small dark scrub is left, without the scrub the big domes stay. A bush is 37 to 46 to a seed and 4 to 11 units across (the dome's 3.0 to 4.2 half-width x scale 0.7 to 1.3); the 70 scrub (`ScrubSize` 2.2, so about 0.6 of that) are darker and in `FoliageStyle.Scrub`. ⚠ **The scrub is still that dome on every tier**: it is the one thing left on the plain that reads as a melon, and it is not touched here.
- **`ThornBushMesh` is High and Ultra's bush; Low, Medium and the Potato path keep the dome.** Two buckets (wood, leaf cards) on the dome's own instances, `ScatterBucket.FineMeshes` true, the dome's `false`, exactly as an acacia's two woods (#758), so the plain is planted once and a tier change rebuilds nothing. The Potato path takes the buckets that are not `FineMeshes == true`, so it draws the dome it always drew (960 indices, under its `PROXY_OVER_VERTICES`). The dome's pixels do not move on the tiers that draw it: the Testbed at `finemeshes=0` differs from origin/main by at most 2 of 255 in any pixel of the frame (the noise of its dither), and the Game at `potato` is identical over the plain's whole band (the Game's own frames cannot be compared pixel for pixel, its clouds, fires and birds move with the clock).
- **What it is**: four to six thin stems out of one root, each forking into two or three branches that fan up and out into a mound, half of them forking again (5-sided tubes, `TubeGeometry.AddSweep`); twigs grown from the branch ends (`LeafSprays.HangOnTwigs`, six sprays a twig) and the sprays hung along them, each pointing along its twig (#882). 565 cards, 1 130 leaf triangles and 2 050 to 2 230 of wood a bush, wood well inside 16-bit indices (`ThornBushTests`). The cards are `Acacia.fx`'s leaflet sprays, so it is the trees' leaf at the bush's scale, and the wood shows through it, which the dome never did. Three thorn meshes stand for each dome mesh (`THORN_PER_DOME`), their instances dealt round in turn, so six shapes where there were two.
- **The sprays lie in a mound, not a plate** (`LeafSprays.LeafShape`, a new argument of `HangOnTwigs`): three layers (height in half-thicknesses, reach as a share of the radius, and how many times the disc's area the sprays would cover) `(0.40, 1.00, 2.20)`, `(-0.10, 0.92, 1.50)`, `(-0.60, 0.78, 0.75)`, a spray 0.22 of the radius long, drooping 0.9 half-thicknesses at the rim (a tier's, `LeafShape.Tier`: 0.20 and 0.5). The default is the tier's, and the rolls are taken in the same order, so **no acacia or baobab list moved**: a checksum over every vertex, index and skeleton of the nine acacias and the two test baobabs is identical on origin/main and here. 47 to 67 % of the sprays' stems stand inside half the reach and 41 to 52 % under half the height, which is the point: the leaf is through the bush, not on its skin (`ThornBushTests`).
- **Smaller, and inside the volume the dome was planted by.** `SavannaScatter.ThornSize`: the bush reaches 0.78 to 0.88 of the dome's radius (`THORN_REACH_*`) and stands 0.75 to 0.97 of its own reach high, never over 0.8 of the dome's height (`THORN_HEIGHT_*`), so 1.2 to 4.7 units high at scale 0.7 to 1.3 (a mature tree is 13 to 19). The planting is the dome's, by the dome's slab (0.9 of its radius, its whole height), and the bush is inside it at every roll: at most 95 % of the slab's radius and 77 % of its height over 216 rolled bushes (`ThornBushTests`, with the 16-bit and cost bounds). Counted over `sceneseed` 0 to 4, **0 overlapping pairs on every seed** (208 or 209 volumes) and every instance's position is the same bit for bit as on origin/main (a hash of the positions, 1 092 to 1 153 distinct on a seed): nothing was re-rolled.
- **The colours run the trees' way, the dome's the other.** The dome's base is `CanopyDry` and its dry end `CanopyColor` (the trees' are the reverse), so a bush's dryness leans it green. Dryness is rolled evenly on 0 to 1, so the plain has the same shades either way, a relabelling rather than a fault, and it is left as it is so no pixel of the tiers that draw the dome moves. The thorn bush takes the trees' order, so dryness means one thing across the plain. Its leaf is the canopy's colours taken 0.35 of the way to their own grey (`THORN_LEAF_GREY`: the references' silver-green, not the trees' green). ⚠ Two earlier grades photographed wrong: 0.6 grey and a quarter brighter made the shaded cards teal, 0.4 and a third brighter made the sunlit shrubs near white at the play camera's distance. Its wood is the trunk's colour doubled and taken 0.6 to grey (`THORN_WOOD_*`) with the bark's ridges at 0.3. Both follow a level's repainted canopy or trunk.
- **Cost: +0.03 ms at High and +0.06 at Ultra, nothing at Medium and Low.** Reference desktop (RX 6900 XT), Release, 3840 x 1600, `ssaa=2`, `fpscap=1000`.
  - The Testbed, `alt=finemeshes=0;finemeshes=1` in one process, `nopost`, origin/main and this build alternated twice (14 cycles a run), the cycles in which the baseline held still pooled (16 to 24 each). From `campos=40,8,30` (`sceneseed=3`) High against Medium is −0.007 ± 0.001 ms on this build and −0.004 ± 0.003 on origin/main, which draws only the acacias' fork; from eye level beside a bush (`campos=48,-10.2,-36 camtarget=60.4,-11.3,-26.5`) −0.005 ± 0.002 and −0.004 ± 0.002; from `campos=-100,4,130 camtarget=-118,2,185` (`sceneseed=0`, six or more bushes in view, a frame of 8.5 ms) **+0.016 ± 0.002 against +0.009 ± 0.002**, the sign held in 90 % and 62 % of the cycles. So the thorn bushes are +0.007 ms in the frame that shows the most of them.
  - The Game, `level=Zebra`, `sceneseed=0 seed=1`, windowed at 3840 x 1600, the two builds alternated three times, the minimum of the run medians (the three agree to 0.01 at High and Medium, to 0.1 at Ultra): `quality=high` 11.40 to 11.43 ms (+0.03), `quality=ultra` 11.81 to 11.88 (+0.06), `quality=medium` 4.95 to 4.96 (+0.01).
  - ⚠ The cost is triangles, not pixels: 3 180 to 3 360 a bush against the dome's 320, so 120 000 to 155 000 on a seed's 37 to 46 bushes against 12 000 to 15 000, and the sun's shadow map draws them too. ⚠ **Measure it on a quiet machine**: a first set of the same runs while other sessions were building had cycles swinging by 0.5 ms and resolved nothing under 0.2 ms.
  - Medium and Low draw the dome as before, so their frame is the same. They pay once, at the scene's load: the scatter builds the six thorn meshes whatever the tier, as it builds an acacia's fine wood (+1 to +3 ms on a 30 to 40 ms build, Release, software device), and holds about 1 MB of buffers. A tier change rebuilds nothing.
- `ThornBushTests` and `LeafAttachmentTests` hold it device-free (the thorn bushes built with the sizes the scatter makes them at): inside the dome's slab at every roll, leaf through the whole cloud, 16-bit wood and a few thousand triangles, every card's stem on its wood and pointing along its twig (the steepest tenth within 36 degrees of the horizontal: a mound climbs more than a plate), and the acacias' and baobabs' card counts unchanged.

## The dressing (`SavannaDressingConfig`)

Each a planting with a count, a size and a colour (every count is a look decision):

- **Grass tufts** (`GrassTuftMesh`: a dozen tapering blades fanning up and out from one root, each ribbon emitted twice so it is two-sided under the ordinary cull). ⚠ A spiked `FoliageMesh` was tried first; at sixteen slices the spikes rounded into a yellow potato from any distance. Tufts are `DetailOnly`: the Low tier skips them, the most draws for the least silhouette.
- **Scrub** bushes in thickets round the groves.
- **Termite mounds** (`TermiteMoundMesh`, a lathe spire three to four times as tall as its column is wide, with an apron at the foot: the reference mounds' proportion).
- **Kopjes** (piles of three to six `RockMesh` boulders rolled rounder than the forest's, the biggest set into the ground, one or two on its shoulder, the rest half-buried round the foot, at `KopjeRockSize` 8: a landmark two or three acacias tall in the references) and **lone boulders** of the same meshes. Lichen is the foliage mottle at 0.35 on the stone.
- **Fallen trees** (`DeadwoodMesh`, a bent tapered log with capped ends and broken stubs).

# Terrain grids, billboards and the CPU terrain mirrors

Part of [The scenes](../scenes.md).

What every terrain scene shares underneath its own look: the camera-centred grids (#589), the billboard index buffers (#589) and the C# copies of each terrain's height field that everything standing on the ground is placed by (#590).

## The terrain grids and the billboards (#589)

- **Every terrain scene's camera-centred grid comes out of one reference-counted cache, `TerrainGridCache`**, keyed on (vertices a side, extent) and reached through `AcquireGridMesh` on `BackdropServices`. A grid carries nothing scene-specific (each shader moves it by its own `OriginXZ` and lifts it in the vertex shader). Four pairs are shared: 360 over 1000 (desert, tropical, Mars), 360 over 1200 (mountains, volcano, Moon), 220 over 1200 (meadow, forest) and 380 over 1600 (the sea and the tropical lagoon); the savanna (400 over 1200), the outback (400 over 1000), the polar sheet (420 over 1200) and the Grid's 8-vertex quad (over 1200) each hold their own. At load the cache holds eight grids and 34.0 MB (a 12-byte position a vertex, 32-bit indices) where a grid a holder would be fifteen and 61.3 MB. The mountains' High/Ultra fine grid (720 over 1200, near first, 18.6 MB) is built on first use, outside those figures; the aurora's two (#963: a fine grid round the camera, 481 over 360, near first, 8.3 MB, and a coarse one to its last ridges, 260 over 1800, 2.4 MB) are built at load, on top of them.
- **The cache owns every buffer: a scene gives its grid back (`ReleaseGridMesh`) and never disposes it.** That matters for the two grids that change: the volcano's and Mars's step down to 256 a side under the reduced detail program (#540), and each step releases the full grid without taking it from scenes still drawing it (the cache holds ten grids at the reduced tier). Grids are built eagerly, so a scene switch builds nothing.
- **A grid is drawn only where the lens looks** (#1032, #1035). The cache also holds each grid in blocks (`TerrainGridCache.Order.Blocks`: 16 quads a side, each block one run of the buffer, its table on the `Grid`; `TerrainGridOrderTests` holds that it carries every triangle once, in runs of its blocks), and `TerrainGridCache.DrawSeenBlocks` draws the runs of blocks whose box the frustum touches. `TerrainPass` takes every row-order grid that way (a block's box spans heights -1000 to 3000, so only the lens' sides and its near and far planes decide); the volcano draws its own grid through it with its own height range. A grid closes round the camera and its vertex program ran on every vertex of it: on the reference APU at `Low`, Spyglass −0.63 ms (−0.52 with blocks of 32), Caldera −0.34, the meadow, the savanna and the desert about −0.1; captures of all ten `TerrainPass` scenes from two vantages differ only by what moves (the flakes, the clouds, the water, the gun's random rounds). The mountain's near-first grid (#781) keeps its rings and draws whole.
- ⚠ **Every billboard index buffer comes out of one builder, `BuildQuadIndexBuffer`, which refuses a count past `MAX_BILLBOARD_QUADS` (16,383).** Past 16,384 quads a 16-bit index wraps and later quads silently draw the first ones' vertices (five copies of that loop existed; only the volcano's counts were clamped, `MAX_BILLBOARD_PARTICLES` stays its dial). Snow and spray go through `BuildBillboardParticles` with their own seeds; the flame keeps its own vertices and takes the builder's `mirrored` order, because its quads stand on their base.
- **The tropical beach's dressing is uploaded once**: each palm, rock, scrub, tuft and driftwood variant holds a static instance buffer (`StaticInstances`, the savanna's `ScatterBucket` pattern) instead of copying matrices into a shared dynamic buffer every draw. It measured no gain, so it is one pattern instead of two, not a performance change (BestPractices §9). The savanna's hearth stones keep the dynamic path.

## The terrain mirrors (#590)

**Everything that stands on a terrain is placed by a C# copy of the field the terrain shader displaces its grid by**: the scatters' trees and rocks, the savanna's fires, the volcano's vents and lamps, and the chapter intro's lenses. All of them are `TerrainMirror`'s static, config-taking methods: `Desert`, `Mountain`, `Outback`, `Polar`, `Savanna`, `Tropical`, `Meadow`, `Forest`, `AuroraBase` and `Aurora` (#963: the forest's field on `AuroraSceneConfig.Terrain` under the snow's drifts and bank, and the drifts round the planting read off `AuroraMoundMap`, the very half floats the shader reads, bilinear by the shader's own arithmetic), `Volcano` (the cone, and since #748 the field's front and the land past it, through `VolcanoCountry`; `VolcanoCone` is the cone alone, what the Potato path's grid stands on) and `Moon` (#680), on `ShaderMath`'s one copy of the helpers. The renderer keeps only the live-config wrappers a host asks through (`SavannaGroundHeight`, `VolcanoGroundHeight`, `PolarGroundHeight`, `AuroraGroundHeight`). The savanna's and forest's ramps keep their hand-spelled clamp-then-hermite rather than `ShaderMath.SmoothStep`, which divides by `(R + T) - R` (not `T` to the last bit).

**`mirrorcheck` compares them with the GPU** (the Testbed switch, "Checking the terrain mirrors" in `docs/testbed.md`). Each terrain shader gains a `HeightProbe` technique by defining its height function and including `HeightProbe.fxh` last; the Testbed draws one quad through it into a 256x256 `Vector4` target covering +-512 round the arena, each pixel evaluating the shader's own height at the world XZ it hands back, so no grid, camera or vertex interpolation stands between the two numbers. Tolerance 0.01 world units. No frame of any executable selects the technique.

Measured max |dh| (desktop RX 6900 XT, 65,536 samples a scene), all passing: savanna 0.000033, meadow 0.000734 (on the brook's swale, #892), forest and aurora 0.000069 (the aurora seen to fail on its own terms, #963: its drifts 5 % high gave 0.0149 with 95 samples over, its mound map 5 % high 0.0160 with 319), beach 0.000634, volcano 0.001717 (the cone, and since #748 the land past its field: the land's second hill octave moved 0.1 gave 1.43 with 7181 samples over), desert 0.000232, mountains 0.000748, outback 0.005643 (its walls steeper since #967), polar 0.000992, Moon 0.000109. The volcano also prints how far its mirror stands off the ground actually drawn (the scoria the mirror leaves out by design, a quarter of it past the field's front): **1.35 at most, 0.17 on average**, the clearance a path over the cone must allow.

- **The savanna's mirror is the plain plus the fires' mounds** (#887: `SavannaPlain` plus `SavannaMound`, the shader's `TerrainHeight` plus `HearthMound`, the lifts pushed from C#), so a height read anywhere near the ring is the mound's. Seen to fail: the mound's lift 10 per cent short gave max |dh| 0.448, 125 of 65,536 samples over tolerance. The mounds are off (`MoundFootScale` 0) where a path draws no fires: the Potato savanna plants and draws the plain alone.
- **Seen to fail (BestPractices section 10).** Before trusting the passes, the forest mirror's second octave was changed from 0.26 to 0.27 and the check run again: forest and aurora failed at 0.290 max, 61,795 of 65,536 samples over tolerance, exit 1.
- ⚠ **The noise hash is ill-conditioned in float, and the GPU's compiler rounds differently from C# in two places.** `NoiseHash22` takes `frac((x + y) * z)` of a product near 20,000, where a float carries the fraction to about 1/500, so a different rounding of the steps moves the hash by whole quanta and a ridged field raised to the third power magnifies it into units (a lens pulled several units off `TerrainMirror.Mountain` could be sixteen units inside the drawn range). Both causes were **measured, not guessed**, and both are now matched on the CPU (#598):
  - the compiler **fuses the hash's `dot` into multiply-adds**: `ShaderMath.Hash22` fuses its dot the same way (`FusedMultiplyAdd(z, x + 33.33, FusedMultiplyAdd(y, z + 33.33, x * (y + 33.33)))`);
  - it **folds literal constant offsets** (`cellId + seed + 23.7` with a literal `seed` becomes `cellId + 37.4`): the outback mirror writes `cellX + (seed + 23.7f)`.
  - The shaders stay as they are; what moved is everything the CPU derives from the noise (the four terrains' mirrors, `CloudField`'s weather mirror, `SavannaTrails`), now on the quanta the GPU draws.
- ⚠ **Still an assumption about the GPU**: this is the desktop's compiler output. It is the same DXBC on every machine (fxc compiles once at build time), so the fusion is in the bytecode and should hold on the laptop, but it was not measured there. A durable fix is a hash well conditioned in float on both sides, which changes the look and wants its own issue and captures.
- **Run `mirrorcheck` on every mirrored scene after touching a terrain shader's height or its mirror.**
